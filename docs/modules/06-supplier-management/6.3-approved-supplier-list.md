# 6.3 Approved Supplier List

> Defines, per part, which suppliers may supply it and which is preferred. Part of [Module 6: Supplier Management](../06-supplier-management.md).

## Purpose

Most stocked parts can be bought from more than one source: the same oil filter might come from the OEM distributor (dear, always in stock), a wholesaler (cheaper, weekly delivery), or an importer (cheapest, 8-week lead). The approved supplier list (ASL) records, for each part, who the business has vetted to supply it and in what order of preference — so that reordering is a policy decision made once by the Purchasing Manager, not a fresh judgement call by whoever raises the PO on a busy Monday.

The ASL is what turns Inventory's reorder suggestions into actionable purchase orders: when stock hits reorder point, the system drafts PO lines against each part's preferred (rank 1) supplier, using that supplier's part-specific lead time and MOQ. Rank 2+ suppliers are the fallback when the preferred source is out of stock, on stop, or has jumped price — the buyer switches with one click, still inside approved territory.

It also encodes quality policy. For safety-critical categories (brakes, steering, suspension) the business may approve only OEM or certified-equivalent sources; a cheap uncertified import may be fine for wiper blades but must never be orderable for brake calipers. Parts with no approved supplier at all surface as a standing warning — they cannot be replenished reliably.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Purchasing Manager | Approve/remove suppliers per part; set preferred and rank |
| Buyer | View; propose additions/rank changes; use fallback suppliers on POs |
| Inventory Controller | View; sees no-approved-supplier warnings on reorder screens |
| Quality/Workshop Manager | Veto rights on safety-critical categories (approval requires their sign-off) |
| System Admin | Bulk import/export ASL |

## Workflow

Main process: replenishment via the ASL.

```
Inventory reorder point breached for part
        │
ASL lookup for part
        │
   ┌────┴──────────────┐
approved suppliers      none ──> "No approved supplier" warning
   │                            on buyer's exception list
preferred (rank 1) suggested
on draft PO line, with part-specific
lead time + MOQ applied
        │
Buyer reviews ──> preferred OK? ──yes──> PO raised
        │no (out of stock / price jump / on stop)
Switch to rank 2/3 supplier (one click, reason captured)
        │
PO raised against fallback; switch logged for
Purchasing Manager review (frequent switches = re-rank signal)
```

1. **Maintain.** The Purchasing Manager builds the ASL part-by-part or in bulk (by category or by supplier), recording rank, part-specific lead time, and MOQ.
2. **Suggest.** Reorder suggestions and manual PO entry default to the rank-1 supplier; the line shows that supplier's current cost from their active price list (6.2).
3. **Fallback.** Buyers may select any approved supplier; selecting a non-preferred one requires a reason code (`out_of_stock`, `price`, `lead_time`, `quality_hold`).
4. **Review.** A monthly exception report shows parts ordered off-preference repeatedly, parts with no approved supplier, and approved suppliers with no active price for the part — the inputs to re-ranking decisions.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Approved suppliers | `suppliers.approved.index` | ASL browser: by part, by supplier, exceptions |
| Part ASL editor | within index | Manage the supplier set and ranking for one part |
| Supplier detail — Approved Parts tab | `suppliers.show` | All parts this supplier is approved for |

**Primary screen — Approved suppliers (`suppliers.approved.index`):**
- **Header:** counts — parts with ASL coverage, parts with none (red), parts whose preferred supplier has no active price; view toggle (by part / by supplier / exceptions).
- **Main area:** grid — part number, description, category, rank-1 supplier, their cost, lead time, MOQ, alternates count; exceptions view lists uncovered and stale entries first.
- **Actions:** Add Approved Supplier, Reorder Ranks (drag), Set Preferred, Remove (with reason), Bulk Assign by Category, Export to Excel.

## Fields & Validation

ASL entry form (per part-supplier pair):

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Part | FK search | Yes | Active stocked part |
| Supplier | FK select | Yes | Active supplier; must not already be on this part's ASL |
| Rank | int | Yes | ≥ 1; unique per part; ranks auto-compact on removal |
| Is preferred | boolean | Yes | Exactly one preferred per part; setting it demotes the previous rank 1 |
| Lead time (days) | int | No | 0–365; defaults from supplier profile (6.1); overrides it for this part |
| Minimum order qty | decimal(10,2) | No | ≥ 0 |
| Notes | text | No | e.g. "certified equivalent — cert on file", "pack of 4 only" |
| Quality sign-off | approval | Safety-critical categories | Quality/Workshop Manager approval recorded before entry activates |

## Business Rules

1. When raising a PO, the system suggests the preferred supplier for each part (parent rule); selecting an unapproved supplier is blocked for safety-critical categories and warned + reason-coded for all others.
2. Parts with no approved supplier flag a warning for the buyer (parent rule) and appear on the standing exceptions report; reorder suggestions for such parts generate no PO line, only an alert.
3. Exactly one preferred supplier per part; `is_preferred = true` and `rank = 1` are kept consistent by the service layer.
4. Removing a supplier from a part's ASL requires a reason (`quality`, `discontinued`, `commercial`, `duplicate`) and does not touch historic POs.
5. Deactivating a supplier (6.1) suspends all their ASL entries; parts where they were preferred are flagged for immediate re-ranking rather than silently promoting rank 2.
6. An approved supplier with no active price-list entry for the part (6.2) is marked "stale price" on the ASL; POs against them require manual cost entry with buyer confirmation.
7. The ASL is exportable to Excel for buyer review (parent rule) and importable back with a strict template (Admin), validating every row and rejecting rank conflicts.
8. Off-preference order reasons aggregate onto the supplier performance view (6.4): a preferred supplier repeatedly skipped for `out_of_stock` is a fill-rate conversation waiting to happen.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Preferred supplier goes on credit stop with us mid-month:** the buyer marks the supplier `on stop` (6.1 flag); ASL suggestions automatically fall to rank 2 with a banner explaining why, and reversals restore rank 1 suggestions.
- **Part discontinued by preferred supplier's new catalogue (6.2):** the delta-review acknowledgement writes a "discontinued" flag onto the ASL entry; the exceptions report demands a re-rank or replacement approval.
- **Two parts merged in Inventory:** ASL entries from both merge; duplicate supplier rows collapse keeping the better rank, and the Purchasing Manager gets a merge summary to sanity-check.
- **Buyer needs an emergency one-off from an unapproved local motor factor (customer's car on the ramp):** allowed outside safety-critical categories via an `emergency_purchase` reason with Manager PIN; the exception report lists it, prompting either formal approval of that supplier or a talking-to.
- **Rank tie from a bad bulk import:** import validation rejects duplicate ranks per part with row numbers; nothing partial is applied.
- **Preferred supplier's part-specific lead time diverges from reality:** actual GRN lead times (6.4) are shown beside the ASL's stated lead time in the editor; a >30% divergence highlights the field for correction because reorder points depend on it.
- **Same part approved at different branches with different suppliers:** the ASL is company-wide by design; branch-specific sourcing preferences are handled by buyer selection at PO time, not per-branch ASLs — noted in the editor to stop duplicate-entry attempts.
