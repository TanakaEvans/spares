# 6.5 Supplier Contacts

> The people behind each supplier — who to phone for orders, returns, and account queries. Part of [Module 6: Supplier Management](../06-supplier-management.md).

## Purpose

A supplier is an organisation, but the daily work runs through people: the sales rep who visits weekly and can conjure stock that "isn't in the system", the internal-sales desk that takes phoned orders, the returns clerk who issues RMA numbers, and the accounts contact who resolves invoice disputes before they hit the payment run. When those names live in one buyer's phone, every leave day or resignation breaks the supply chain a little. Supplier contacts puts the whole roster on the supplier profile with a `responsible_for` designation, so anyone in the business knows who to contact for orders, returns, or accounts — and the system knows who to send what.

Routing is the practical payoff. Purchase orders email to the contact responsible for "Orders"; remittance advices from payment runs ([7.4](../07-finance-accounts/7.4-accounts-payable.md)) go to "Accounts"; return/RMA requests go to "Returns"; performance scorecards (6.4) go to the account manager or primary contact. Without designated contacts, documents go to a generic inbox and die there.

Contacts also carry the relationship memory that supplier reviews rely on: which rep attended the last quarterly review, who agreed the settlement discount, whose promises about lead times keep not materialising.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Buyer | Add/edit contacts on suppliers they buy from |
| Purchasing Manager | Full add/edit/remove on all suppliers |
| Accounts Payable Clerk | Add/edit contacts with `responsible_for = Accounts`; set remittance recipients |
| Branch Manager | View all |
| System Admin | Bulk import/export contacts |

## Workflow

Main process: capturing contacts and routing documents through them.

```
Supplier onboarded (6.1) with one primary contact
        │
Roster filled in over time:
  ├─ Sales Rep        (responsible_for: Orders)   ← from rep's visit
  ├─ Internal Sales   (responsible_for: Orders)
  ├─ Returns Clerk    (responsible_for: Returns)  ← from first RMA
  └─ Accounts         (responsible_for: Accounts) ← from first statement
        │
Document routing uses the roster:
  PO issued            → email contact(s) for Orders
  Payment run remit    → email contact(s) for Accounts
  Return/RMA request   → email contact(s) for Returns
  Scorecard (6.4)      → primary contact
        │
Bounce / no such person ──> routing exception on supplier profile
        │
Contact updated ──> routing self-heals
```

1. **Seed at onboarding.** Supplier creation (6.1) requires at least one contact, marked primary.
2. **Enrich in use.** Each workflow that touches the supplier (first PO, first return, first invoice query) prompts to capture the relevant contact if none exists for that responsibility.
3. **Route automatically.** Outbound documents select recipients by `responsible_for`; multiple contacts per responsibility all receive the document.
4. **Handle failures.** Email bounces write a routing exception to the supplier profile and notify the owning buyer; documents are never silently unsent.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Supplier detail — Contacts tab | `suppliers.show` | Roster for one supplier with responsibilities and routing status |
| Contact form | within Contacts tab | Add/edit a contact |
| Routing exceptions | via `suppliers.index` filter | Suppliers with bounced/missing routing contacts |

**Primary screen — Contacts tab on supplier detail (`suppliers.show`):**
- **Header:** contact count, primary contact name, routing health chips (Orders ✓ / Returns ✗ missing / Accounts ✓).
- **Main area:** card per contact — name, position, responsibility badges, email, phone, mobile, primary star, last-bounce indicator; ordered primary-first.
- **Actions:** Add Contact, Edit, Set Primary, Mark Left Company, Send Test Email, Log Interaction (note on supplier record).

## Fields & Validation

Contact add/edit form:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Name | text(100) | Yes | Min 2 chars |
| Position | text(100) | No | e.g. "Sales Rep", "Account Manager" |
| Email | email(255) | Conditional | RFC-valid; required if the contact holds any document-routing responsibility (Orders/Returns/Accounts) |
| Phone | text(20) | Conditional | 7–20 digits; at least one of email/phone required |
| Mobile | text(20) | No | 7–20 digits |
| Is primary | boolean | Yes | Exactly one primary per supplier; setting demotes the previous |
| Responsible for | multi-select | No | One or more of `Orders`, `Returns`, `Accounts`, `Technical`, `Management` |
| Notes | text | No | e.g. "only reachable mornings", "covers Coastal region" |

## Business Rules

1. Every active supplier must retain at least one contact and exactly one primary; the last contact cannot be deleted — the supplier would become unreachable by the routing engine.
2. Document routing requires a contact with the matching responsibility **and** a valid email; where none exists, the sending screen falls back to manual entry and writes a "missing routing contact" exception.
3. Contacts are marked "left company" rather than deleted when they move on — historic POs and correspondence reference them, and the audit trail must keep resolving.
4. A contact flagged left-company is removed from all routing immediately; if they were primary or sole holder of a responsibility, the supplier lands on the routing-exceptions list until replaced.
5. Multiple contacts may share a responsibility; all of them receive routed documents (deliberate redundancy for order confirmations).
6. Email changes to a contact holding the `Accounts` responsibility trigger a notice to the AP clerk — remittance-redirect fraud uses the same playbook as bank-detail fraud (6.1), and remit destinations changing quietly is a red flag.
7. Bulk import (Admin) validates per-row and never removes existing contacts — it only adds or updates matched-by-email rows.
8. Contact data is used strictly for supplier business correspondence; it is excluded from any customer-marketing lists by construction (separate tables, no shared campaign tooling).

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **PO emailed, order contact has left, nobody told us:** the bounce writes a routing exception and notifies the buyer; the PO status stays "issued — delivery unconfirmed" so a bounced order is visibly not the same as a received one.
- **Rep covers 40 of our suppliers (a buying-group rep):** the same person appears as a contact on each supplier record independently — duplication is accepted; a change of rep is handled per supplier as each relationship actually changes hands.
- **Two contacts both marked Returns, one insists RMAs go only to her:** remove the responsibility from the other contact; routing follows the roster, and the roster is the single source of truth — no side-channel overrides.
- **Supplier's generic inbox (orders@) plus a named rep:** both can hold `Orders`; capture the generic address as its own contact named "Orders Desk" so bounces and test-sends are trackable per address.
- **Contact asks for WhatsApp-only communication:** mobile is stored, but system routing is email-based; the notes field records the preference and the buyer handles it manually — the PO email still sends for the paper trail.
- **Accounts contact email changed the day before a payment run:** rule 6 notice fires; the AP clerk verifies by phone before the run sends remittance advices to the new address.
- **Supplier merge (6.1):** contact rosters merge; duplicate emails collapse to one contact keeping the union of responsibilities, and exactly one primary survives (from the surviving supplier record).
