# 6.4 Supplier Performance

> Rolling 12-month KPIs — delivery, fill rate, returns, lead time, invoice accuracy — per supplier. Part of [Module 6: Supplier Management](../06-supplier-management.md).

## Purpose

Price is only half of what a supplier costs. A distributor 5% cheaper who delivers late and short-ships every third order costs more in lost counter sales ("sorry, the filter didn't arrive") and buyer time than the dearer reliable one. Supplier performance turns the purchasing data the system already captures — POs, GRNs, returns, invoice matches — into five comparable KPIs per supplier, so review meetings and ASL re-ranking decisions (6.3) run on evidence instead of the buyer's most recent bad experience.

The KPIs are deliberately calculated, not captured: nobody fills in a scorecard. On-time delivery compares GRN dates to PO expected dates; fill rate compares received quantities to ordered quantities per line; return rate counts supplier-return lines against received lines; lead-time accuracy compares actual PO-to-GRN elapsed days against the quoted lead time; invoice accuracy counts supplier invoices that matched their GRN without a price or quantity dispute. All are computed over a rolling 12 months so seasonal blips wash out but trends show.

The output drives three actions: annual/quarterly supplier reviews (with the printout on the table), ASL re-ranking when a preferred supplier's numbers slide, and negotiation leverage ("your fill rate is 88% against a 95% target — why should you keep rank 1 on filters?").

## Workflow

Main process: quarterly supplier review driven by KPIs.

```
Purchasing calendar: quarterly review due for supplier
        │
Open performance report ──> 12-month KPI panel vs targets
        │
Drill down on any red KPI
  ├─ on-time %  → list of late POs with days-late
  ├─ fill rate  → short-shipped lines by part
  ├─ returns    → return lines with reason codes
  ├─ lead time  → actual vs quoted distribution
  └─ invoice acc.→ disputed invoices and outcomes
        │
Export review pack (PDF/Excel) ──> meeting with supplier rep (6.5)
        │
Outcomes recorded: targets agreed, corrective actions,
ASL re-rank decisions (6.3), notes on supplier profile
```

1. **Monitor.** The performance dashboard ranks all active suppliers by a composite score; anyone below target on any KPI shows amber/red.
2. **Drill.** Every KPI figure is clickable through to the underlying documents — a late-delivery percentage is only useful if the buyer can see *which* POs were late and by how long.
3. **Review.** The exportable review pack fixes the numbers as at the review date for the meeting record.
4. **Act.** Decisions feed back into the system: ASL re-ranks (6.3), lead-time corrections (6.1/6.3), or in the extreme, deactivation.

## Fields & Validation

There is no data-entry form for KPIs (they are computed). The configurable inputs:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| KPI targets (per KPI) | decimal | Yes | On-time > 0–100 (default 90); fill rate default 95; return rate default max 3; lead-time ratio default max 1.10; invoice accuracy default 98 |
| Target scope | select | Yes | Global default or per-supplier override |
| Review period | date range | Yes | Default rolling 12 months; min 1 month (small samples flagged) |
| Minimum order threshold | int | Yes | Suppliers below N orders in period (default 5) show "insufficient data" instead of misleading percentages |
| Scorecard email recipient | FK select | If emailing | A `supplier_contacts` row with valid email (see 6.5) |

KPI formulas (fixed, from the parent doc): on-time = delivered on/before expected ÷ total orders; fill rate = lines fully received ÷ lines ordered; return rate = lines returned ÷ lines received; lead-time accuracy = actual ÷ quoted lead time; invoice accuracy = invoices matched without dispute ÷ total invoices.

## Business Rules

1. KPIs are calculated views over purchasing documents — never stored per-record scores (parent rule); recalculation with the same inputs always reproduces the same numbers, and exported review packs snapshot values as-at export.
2. A PO counts as on-time against its **expected date at confirmation**; supplier-requested date changes accepted by the buyer move the expected date (and are counted separately as "reschedules"), unilateral slips do not.
3. Fill rate counts a line as filled only when fully received within the PO's receiving window; partial receipts count the line as unfilled until completed.
4. Return lines with reason `our_error` (wrong part ordered by us) are excluded from the supplier's return rate — the KPI measures their quality, not our ordering.
5. Invoice accuracy counts an invoice as accurate only if it matched GRN quantity and PO price without a dispute record; disputes resolved in the supplier's favour are retroactively counted accurate.
6. Suppliers with fewer than the minimum order threshold in the period display "insufficient data" — no red flags on two orders.
7. Branch filtering recomputes KPIs on branch-scoped documents so a supplier serving one branch badly and another well is visible.
8. Target changes apply prospectively to display only — historic review packs keep the targets in force at their export date.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **GRN captured days after physical delivery (backlog at receiving):** on-time uses the GRN's *delivery date* field (editable at capture with permission), not the capture timestamp; the report footnotes lines where they differ by > 3 days.
- **Blanket/standing order delivered in agreed monthly drops:** scheduled-release POs measure each release against its own expected date rather than damning the whole order as one late line.
- **Supplier disputes the scorecard:** every figure drills to document lists exportable to Excel; the meeting argument becomes "which of these POs was not late?" instead of duelling spreadsheets.
- **Part on ASL of two suppliers, one order each — 0% vs 100% on-time:** the insufficient-data threshold suppresses percentage display, showing raw counts (1/1, 0/1) instead.
- **Return of goods received 11 months ago lands in this period:** return rate numerator uses the return's date, denominator the period's received lines — noted in the KPI definition tooltip; large old-return distortions are flagged when returns reference receipts outside the period.
- **Currency-driven price disputes with import suppliers:** invoice mismatches caused solely by agreed exchange-rate differences use dispute reason `fx_agreed` and do not count against invoice accuracy.
- **Supplier merged (6.1):** the survivor's KPIs recompute over the combined document history automatically — a side effect of KPIs being calculated, not stored.
