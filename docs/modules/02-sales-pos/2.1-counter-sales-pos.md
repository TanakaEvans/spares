# 2.1 Counter Sales (POS)

> High-speed walk-in sales screen with barcode scanning, split tender, and shift controls. Part of [Module 2: Sales & POS](../02-sales-pos.md).

## Purpose

The POS screen is the busiest screen in the business: a queue of walk-in customers, each wanting parts identified, priced, paid, and bagged in under 90 seconds. Speed comes from scan-first design — a barcode scan on an NGK `BKR6E-11` box adds the line instantly — backed by layered search for the harder cases: internal number, OEM number, cross-reference, description words, or browsing category tiles when the customer only knows "a fan belt for a 2012 Corolla".

The screen carries the fitment intelligence of the catalogue to the counter. Capturing the customer's vehicle (registration lookup or make/model pick) filters and flags search results so a junior cashier sells the right Hilux oil filter without knowing Toyota part numbers. Pricing resolves automatically from the customer's price list, promotions apply themselves, and trade customers can buy on account against their credit limit.

Operationally, POS is also the cash-control point: cash drawer integration, split tenders, suspend/recall for the customer who forgot a part, same-shift voids with reasons, and X/Z reads that reconcile each cashier's drawer at shift end. Every shortcut exists because seconds per transaction multiply across a few hundred sales a day.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Cashier | Create sales, suspend/recall, tender payments; discounts up to own limit |
| Senior Cashier | Above + price override within tolerance, X-read |
| Supervisor / Manager | Void transactions, approve over-limit discounts, refunds at POS, Z-read, open drawer without sale |
| Branch Manager | Above + on-account sale beyond credit limit (with note), shift reassignment |

## Workflow

```
Open shift (float count) 
        │
        ▼
[Per sale]  Scan / search part ──► line added (price from customer price list)
        │            ▲
        │            └── set customer (default CASH) / set vehicle (fitment filter)
        ▼
Adjust qty, line discounts (within authority)
        │
        ├──► SUSPEND (customer steps away) ... RECALL later
        ▼
PROCESS PAYMENT → tender(s): cash / card / EFT / on account (split allowed)
        │
        ▼
Invoice posts (stock OUT, GL, AR if account) → receipt prints → drawer opens
        │
        ▼
[Shift end]  X-read (any time) → Z-read (locks shift, declares cash, variance logged)
```

1. Cashier opens a shift with a counted float; all sales attach to the shift.
2. Each scan resolves barcode → part; unknown barcodes fall through to cross-reference and supersession search before showing "not found".
3. Selecting a trade customer switches the price list and enables ACCOUNT tender; selecting a vehicle activates the fitment filter/badges.
4. Payment can split across tenders (e.g. $50 cash + remainder card). Cash tender computes change; card/EFT capture a reference.
5. Completion posts a tax invoice ([2.4](2.4-tax-invoices.md)) — stock ledger `SALE` entries, GL postings, receipt print.
6. Z-read closes the shift: declared cash vs expected cash, variance recorded and reported.

**Primary screen — POS (`Sales/POS/Index.jsx`):** Header bar: branch, cashier, current customer (default CASH), vehicle chip, shift status. Left pane: scan-focused search box (keyboard focus always returns here), recent parts and favourites tiles, category browse. Right pane: the cart — lines with qty/price/discount inline-editable, running subtotal, VAT, total; beneath it large tender buttons (CASH, CARD, ACCOUNT), SUSPEND, CLEAR, and PROCESS PAYMENT. Layout per the wireframe in the [parent doc](../02-sales-pos.md#pos-screen-layout). All key actions have keyboard shortcuts; the screen is operable without a mouse.

## Fields & Validation

Main form: the sale (cart + tender).

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| customer_id | FK | Yes | Defaults to CASH customer; account tender requires a real customer |
| vehicle | ref | No | From customer's vehicles or manual pick; drives fitment flags |
| lines[].part_id | FK | Yes | Active part; serial-tracked parts require serial pick |
| lines[].qty | decimal(10,2) | Yes | > 0; ≤ available unless negative stock allowed; decimals only for fractional UoM |
| lines[].unit_price | decimal(15,2) | Yes | Auto from price resolution; edits below list minimum need authority |
| lines[].discount_pct | decimal(5,2) | No | 0–100; capped by cashier's discount authority |
| tenders[].method | FK | Yes | Active payment method |
| tenders[].amount | decimal(15,2) | Yes | > 0; sum of tenders ≥ total; only cash may exceed (change) |
| tenders[].reference | string(100) | Cond. | Required when method `requires_reference` (card auth, EFT ref) |

## Business Rules

1. Availability is re-checked at payment time, not just when the line was added — the shelf may have been sold from another till.
2. On-account tender checks the customer's credit limit and account status; over-limit needs branch-manager approval and is logged.
3. Discounts above the cashier's authority prompt a supervisor PIN; the approver is recorded on the line.
4. Selling a flagged non-fit part for the selected vehicle requires an override reason stored on the line ([1.6](../01-inventory-management/1.6-parts-fitment-guide.md)).
5. Suspended sales hold no stock reservation; recall re-validates price and availability and highlights any changes.
6. Voids are same-shift only, require a reason and supervisor authority, and reverse stock and GL by posting reversal entries — the original document is never deleted.
7. The cash drawer opens only on cash tender, cash refund, or supervisor no-sale (logged).
8. Z-read locks the shift: no further sales or voids on it; cash variance beyond tolerance raises a management alert.
9. Change is only computable on cash; card/EFT tenders cannot exceed the amount due.
10. Every completed sale is a posted tax invoice — POS has no non-fiscal "quick sale" path.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Barcode not found**: falls through internal number → OEM → cross-refs → supersession; final miss shows "not found" with options: create special order or call inventory clerk.
- **Superseded part scanned** (old stock on shelf): line adds with a supersession notice; both old and new stock remain sellable.
- **Negative stock attempt** (available 0, branch disallows negative): line blocked with per-branch availability shown and a one-click transfer request option.
- **Two tills sell the last unit**: the later payment fails re-validation; cashier sees "just sold at till 2" and removes/back-orders the line.
- **Card terminal times out mid-tender**: tender marked pending; cashier retries or switches method; the sale never half-posts (payment and posting are one transaction).
- **Printer offline at receipt time**: sale still posts; receipt queues for reprint and screen shows the invoice number for manual reference.
- **Power/browser crash mid-cart**: cart state is persisted locally per terminal; on reload the cashier recovers the unpaid cart.
- **Customer pays with mixed cash currencies/rounding**: cash rounding rule applies to the cash portion only, logged as rounding, not discount.
- **Suspend used to hold stock for a mate**: suspensions auto-expire at Z-read and are listed on the shift report.
