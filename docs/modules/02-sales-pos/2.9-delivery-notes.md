# 2.9 Delivery Notes

> Delivery documentation for goods sent to customers rather than collected at the counter, with proof of delivery. Part of [Module 2: Sales & POS](../02-sales-pos.md).

## Purpose

When a garage orders twenty sets of Hilux brake pads and four batteries for delivery, the goods leave the building without the customer standing at the counter — and that gap between dispatch and receipt is where disputes are born. The delivery note is the document that closes it: a numbered list of exactly what left the warehouse, on which vehicle, for which order or invoice, signed by whoever received it. When the customer later claims two batteries short, the answer is the POD (proof of delivery): the signed note either shows 4 received or documents the shortage recorded at the doorstep.

Delivery notes are deliberately non-fiscal — no prices need appear (trade customers often insist their driver must not see pricing), no GL posting occurs, and VAT is untouched. They are the physical-movement counterpart to the invoice: an invoice says what was charged; the delivery note proves what was handed over. For partially fulfilled orders the two diverge, and the note is what keeps `qty_delivered` on the order lines honest.

The sub-module also carries light logistics: grouping the day's deliveries into runs per driver/vehicle, printing notes in drop sequence, and capturing delivery outcomes — delivered in full, short, refused, or failed (nobody at the address) — each with its follow-up path.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Dispatch Clerk | Create delivery notes from orders/invoices, plan runs, assign drivers |
| Storeman / Packer | Confirm packed quantities against the note |
| Driver | Mobile view: mark delivered/short/refused/failed, capture signature and photo |
| Sales Supervisor | Amend before dispatch, cancel notes, resolve failed deliveries |
| Branch Manager | Approve pricing shown on notes (exception), POD dispute review |

## Workflow

```
Source: sales order (picked/packed) or posted invoice
        │
        ▼
Create delivery note (lines = quantities physically going out)
        │  status: draft
        ▼
Pack check: packer confirms items vs note ──► status: ready
        │
        ▼
Assign to delivery run (driver, vehicle, drop sequence) ──► print notes (2 copies)
        │  status: in_transit  (order lines: dispatched)
        ▼
At customer:
   ├─ delivered in full ──► signature + name + time captured ──► status: delivered
   ├─ delivered short  ──► short qtys recorded on POD ──► status: delivered_short
   │        └── follow-up: second delivery or credit note (2.5)
   ├─ refused          ──► reason recorded ──► goods return to branch
   └─ failed (no one)  ──► status: failed ──► re-delivery scheduled
        │
        ▼
POD filed against the note ──► order qty_delivered updated ──► invoicing proceeds (2.4)
```

1. The dispatch clerk generates the note from a packed order (normal path) or a posted invoice (deliver-after-sale path); quantities default to what was packed, never more than ordered/invoiced.
2. The packer physically checks cartons against the note before it can go `ready` — the last catch point for pick errors.
3. Runs group notes per driver and vehicle with a drop sequence; notes print with bin-free, price-free layout (prices optional per customer setting).
4. The driver records the outcome per drop; signature, recipient name, timestamp, and optional photo become the POD.
5. Outcomes write back: `qty_delivered` on order lines updates, shortages open follow-ups, refused/failed goods are checked back into stock on return to branch.

## Fields & Validation

Main form: delivery note.

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| source document | FK | Yes | Confirmed order (packed) or posted invoice; one source per note |
| delivery_address_id | FK | Yes | From customer addresses; free-text override allowed with supervisor note |
| contact_name / phone | string | Yes | Pre-filled from customer; phone validated |
| lines[].qty | decimal(10,2) | Yes | > 0; ≤ (packed − already on other notes) per source line |
| driver_id | FK | At dispatch | Active driver user |
| vehicle | string(50) | At dispatch | Registration of delivery vehicle |
| pod.recipient_name | string(100) | At outcome | Required for delivered / delivered_short |
| pod.signature | blob/image | At outcome | Required unless "contactless" flag with photo instead |
| pod.short_qtys | per line | Cond. | Required for delivered_short; ≤ line qty |
| outcome_reason | enum | Cond. | Required for refused (`wrong_goods`, `damaged`, `not_ordered`, `other`) and failed (`closed`, `no_authorised_person`, `address_wrong`) |

## Business Rules

1. A delivery note line can never exceed the undelivered balance of its source line; multiple notes per order are allowed (partial deliveries) but their sum is capped.
2. Delivery notes are non-fiscal: no GL or VAT effect. Stock OUT is posted by the invoice ([2.4](2.4-tax-invoices.md)); for invoice-first flows the goods are already written out, and the note only evidences movement.
3. Prices print only when the customer profile enables priced delivery notes; default is unpriced.
4. `qty_delivered` on order lines updates from the POD outcome, not from dispatch — dispatching proves intent, the signature proves delivery.
5. Short deliveries auto-open a follow-up that must resolve as either a second delivery note for the balance or a short-delivery credit note ([2.5](2.5-credit-notes-returns.md)); it cannot be closed silently.
6. Refused and failed deliveries require the goods to be checked back in at the branch (return-to-branch scan) before the note can close; discrepancies raise an investigation.
7. A note cannot be cancelled once an outcome is recorded; corrections flow through follow-ups.
8. POD records (signature, name, time, photos) are immutable once saved and retained for the document retention period.
9. Serial-tracked items list their serials on the note; the POD binds those serials to the delivery.
10. Every printed copy after the first carries a "COPY" watermark, mirroring invoice reprint rules.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Customer signs but counts later, then claims short**: POD shows "received in full" with signature; the claim routes to manager review per [2.5](2.5-credit-notes-returns.md) — the note is evidence, not a barrier to genuine claims.
- **Driver's phone dies mid-run**: paper copy signatures are captured; the dispatch clerk enters outcomes from the signed paper on return, scanning the paper POD as the photo attachment.
- **Wrong address on file** (customer moved): driver marks failed/`address_wrong`; supervisor fixes the address on the customer record and reschedules — the note keeps the attempted address for the record.
- **Partial refusal at the door** (accepts pads, refuses batteries "not ordered"): outcome is delivered_short with refused lines flagged; refused goods return to branch and the follow-up decides credit vs re-delivery.
- **Goods damaged in transit**: refused/`damaged`; on check-in the storeman books them to quarantine, and a write-off or supplier-carrier claim proceeds — customer gets a replacement run.
- **Note created from an order whose stock was meanwhile sold** (invoice-later flow): packing confirmation fails against real stock; the note cannot go ready, surfacing the shortage before the truck leaves.
- **Two notes accidentally created for the same order lines**: the per-line cap (rule 1) blocks the second note's quantities; screen links to the existing note.
- **Driver app offline in low-signal areas**: outcomes queue locally and sync when connectivity returns; timestamps are capture-time, not sync-time.
