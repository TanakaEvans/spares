# 2.7 Promotions & Discounts

> Time-limited promotional pricing: percent/fixed discounts, buy-X-get-Y, free items, and bundles. Part of [Module 2: Sales & POS](../02-sales-pos.md).

## Purpose

Promotions move stock that price lists alone won't: the winter battery push, the supplier-funded "20% off all Bosch in July", clearance on filters for a discontinued model, or the service-bundle deal (Castrol 5W-30 5L + oil filter for $25 instead of $32). Where price lists are standing structure, promotions are campaigns — date-bounded, targeted at a brand, category, or specific part set, and often restricted to certain customer types (a trade-only deal must not fire for walk-ins, and vice versa).

The engine's job is to apply deals automatically and correctly at the moment of sale. Cashiers should never memorise the leaflet: scan four oil filters and the fifth rings up free; add the oil and filter together and the bundle price replaces the line prices, with the receipt describing the deal so the customer sees the saving. Automatic application also closes an abuse hole — hand-keyed "promo discounts" that outlive the promotion.

Because campaigns overlap, the engine needs deterministic conflict handling: a priority number decides which promotion wins when several match the same line, and an `is_stackable` flag says whether a deal may combine with others. Every applied promotion is recorded on the line, so supplier-funded deals can be claimed back with transaction-level evidence.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Cashier | Sees promotions apply automatically; cannot create or force them |
| Sales Supervisor | Pause/resume a live promotion at branch level (e.g. stock ran out) |
| Marketing / Pricing Clerk | Create and edit draft/scheduled promotions |
| Sales Manager | Approve and activate promotions; set priority and stacking |
| Finance | Promotion cost reports, supplier claim-back extracts |

## Workflow

```
Plan campaign ──► Create promotion (type, dates, targets, customer types)
        │            status: draft
        ▼
Manager approval ──► status: scheduled
        │
        ▼  (start_date reached — automatic)
status: active
        │
        ▼
[Every transaction line] engine evaluates:
   qualify? (part/category/brand + customer type + min qty + dates)
        │
        ├─ multiple matches → highest priority wins; stackables combine
        ▼
   discount / free line / bundle price applied + promotion recorded on line
        │
        ▼  (end_date passes — automatic)
status: expired ──► performance report (uses, discount value, margin impact)

   [any time] Pause ⇄ Resume (manual, e.g. promo stock exhausted)
```

1. The clerk defines the deal: type (`percent_discount`, `fixed_discount`, `buy_x_get_y`, `free_item`, `bundle_price`), date window, applicability (`all`, `category`, `brand`, `specific_parts`), qualifying minimums, and eligible customer types.
2. Manager approval moves it to `scheduled`; activation and expiry are clock-driven, never manual toggles at the till.
3. At sale time the engine evaluates per line (and per cart for bundles/BXGY), applies the winner, and prints the promotion description on receipt and invoice.
4. Free-item and BXGY lines are added at zero/reduced price with a linked reference to the qualifying lines.
5. After expiry, the performance report totals redemptions, discount given, and margin impact — and feeds the supplier claim for funded promotions.

## Fields & Validation

Main form: promotion.

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| name | string(255) | Yes | Unique among non-expired promotions |
| description | text | Yes | Printed on receipts — customer-facing wording |
| type | enum | Yes | One of the five types; type locks after activation |
| start_date / end_date | date | Yes | start ≤ end; end in the future at creation |
| applies_to | enum | Yes | `all`, `category`, `brand`, `specific_parts` |
| customer_types | json | Yes | ≥ 1 of retail/trade/wholesale/vip |
| priority | int | Yes | 1–100; ties broken by newest-first with a warning |
| is_stackable | boolean | Yes | Default false |
| lines[].part_id / category_id / brand_id | FK | Cond. | Exactly one target per line, matching `applies_to` |
| lines[].min_qty | decimal(10,2) | Cond. | Required > 0 for BXGY and qty-gated discounts |
| lines[].discount_value | decimal(15,2) | Cond. | Percent type: 0–100; fixed: > 0 and ≤ item price |
| lines[].free_part_id + free_qty | FK + decimal | Cond. | Required for `buy_x_get_y` / `free_item`; free part must be active and stocked |

## Business Rules

1. Activation and expiry are date-driven; `paused` is the only manual state for a live promotion and is fully audit-logged.
2. When multiple non-stackable promotions match a line, the highest `priority` wins; stackable promotions combine but total line discount is capped at a configurable ceiling (e.g. 50%).
3. Promotions apply after price-list resolution — the discount computes off the customer's resolved price, so a trade customer gets trade price then promo, unless the promotion is flagged "off retail only".
4. Customer-type gating is strict: the engine checks the transaction's customer type, and CASH sales count as `retail`.
5. BXGY qualification counts quantities within one transaction only; the free line is added automatically and cannot be price-edited.
6. Bundle pricing requires all bundle components on the cart; removing a component after the bundle applied re-prices the remaining lines to normal.
7. Every application writes the promotion id and discount value to the sale line — the basis for supplier claim-backs and the performance report.
8. Editing an active promotion is limited to end-date shortening and pausing; substantive changes require ending it and issuing a new one (protects redemption reporting).
9. A promotion cannot make a line price negative; fixed discounts floor at zero with the excess ignored.
10. Credit notes for promo-sold lines credit the promotional (paid) price; returning part of a BXGY set claws back the free item proportionally or reduces the credit accordingly.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Free-item part out of stock** (buy 4 filters, free filter unavailable): engine substitutes nothing silently — cashier is prompted: back-order the free item, offer equivalent (supervisor), or apply equivalent value discount.
- **Overlapping supplier promos** (Bosch 20% off and "brakes 10% off" both hit Bosch brake pads): non-stackable → priority decides; the Conflicts tab shows the overlap at scheduling time, before customers hit it.
- **Cart edited to game BXGY** (add 4 filters, take the free one, remove the 4): engine re-evaluates on every cart change; the free line vanishes when qualification breaks.
- **Promotion expires mid-transaction** (cart open across midnight): qualification is evaluated at payment time against the document date — expired means not applied, and the cashier sees the change flagged.
- **Suspended POS sale recalled after promo expiry**: recall re-prices and lists the removed promotions explicitly ([2.1](2.1-counter-sales-pos.md)).
- **Promo on a part that gets superseded mid-campaign**: `specific_parts` targeting does not auto-extend to the successor; the conflicts check flags the dead target so the clerk can add the new part.
- **Layered abuse attempt** (manual line discount on top of promo): manual discounts and promotions share the line-discount ceiling; exceeding it needs supervisor approval.
- **Quote issued during a promo, converted after it ends**: conversion re-pricing removes the promo with an explicit old/new comparison; honouring the promo price needs supervisor approval ([2.2](2.2-quotations.md)).
