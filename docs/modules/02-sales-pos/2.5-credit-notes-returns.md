# 2.5 Credit Notes & Returns

> Controlled reversal of posted invoices: goods returns, price corrections, short deliveries, and warranty claims. Part of [Module 2: Sales & POS](../02-sales-pos.md).

## Purpose

Returns are daily reality in the spares trade — more than in most retail, because parts get bought on guesswork. The customer's "mechanic said it's the round one" turns out to be the wrong oil filter for their Hilux; the alternator fails in week three under warranty; a trade delivery arrives two brake discs short of the invoice. Since posted invoices are immutable ([2.4](2.4-tax-invoices.md)), every one of these corrections flows through a credit note: a fiscal document that reverses part or all of an invoice, posts a negative AR/revenue entry, reverses VAT output, and — when goods physically come back — returns stock to hand.

Control is the point. An uncontrolled credit path is the easiest fraud channel in a parts business (credit a mate's invoice, pocket the cash refund). So every credit note must reference an original invoice, cannot exceed what that invoice has left uncredited, distinguishes whether stock actually returned, and gates cash refunds behind same-day/same-tender rules or manager approval.

The four credit types — `goods_return`, `price_correction`, `short_delivery`, `warranty` — carry different stock behaviour: a goods return puts sellable stock back, a warranty return puts the defective unit into quarantine for the supplier claim, and price corrections and short deliveries move no stock at all.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Cashier | Initiate return, create draft credit note; cash refund same-day/same-tender only |
| Counter Sales (senior) | Post goods-return credits within value limit; inspect condition |
| Supervisor / Manager | Approve cash refunds outside same-day rule, warranty credits, over-limit credits |
| Storeman | Receive returned goods into return/quarantine bin |
| Finance | View GL reversals, VAT credit reporting |

## Workflow

```
Customer presents part + proof of purchase
        │
        ▼
Find original invoice (scan receipt barcode / search customer + date)
        │
        ▼
Select lines + quantities to credit (system caps at uncredited balance)
        │
        ▼
Condition check (goods returns): resellable? ─ yes → stock RETURN_IN to bin
                                             └ no  → quarantine / warranty hold
        │
        ▼
Choose settlement: refund cash │ refund card reversal │ credit account │ exchange
        │
        ▼
Approvals (per rules) ──► POST credit note
        ├── GL: Dr Sales & VAT output · Cr AR/Cash
        ├── stock ledger RETURN_IN (if stock_returned)
        └── settlement executes (drawer opens / AR credited / exchange sale rung)
```

1. The original invoice is mandatory: staff locate it by receipt scan, invoice number, or customer purchase history.
2. Line selection shows, per line, invoiced qty minus already-credited qty — the only quantities available to credit.
3. For `goods_return`, staff record condition; resellable items book into the returns bin for putaway, non-resellable route to `warranty`/write-off handling.
4. Settlement follows the original tender where possible: card payments reverse to the card, account invoices credit the account. Cash refunds obey the same-day rule.
5. Exchange is a combined flow: credit note for the wrong part + immediate new sale for the right one, settling only the difference.
6. Warranty credits park the defective unit (serial recorded if tracked) pending the supplier claim; the customer receives replacement or credit per policy.

**Primary screen — Create credit note (`Sales/Credits/Create.jsx`):** Header: original invoice summary (number, date, customer, total, amount already credited). ## Fields & Validation

Main form: credit note.

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| original_invoice_id | FK | Yes | Posted, non-voided invoice; same branch group |
| type | enum | Yes | `goods_return`, `price_correction`, `short_delivery`, `warranty` |
| return_reason | enum | Yes | e.g. `wrong_part`, `does_not_fit`, `defective`, `changed_mind`, `overcharge`, `short_shipped` |
| lines[].qty | decimal(10,2) | Yes | > 0 and ≤ (invoiced − previously credited) per line |
| lines[].unit_price | decimal(15,2) | Auto | Copied from the invoice line — never re-priced |
| stock_returned | boolean | Yes | Forced true for `goods_return`, false for `price_correction`/`short_delivery` |
| return_bin_id | FK | Cond. | Required when stock_returned; must be a returns-type bin |
| serials[] | list | Cond. | Required per unit for serial-tracked lines; must match serials sold on that invoice |
| settlement_method | enum | Yes | `cash_refund`, `card_reversal`, `account_credit`, `exchange` |
| approved_by | FK | Cond. | Required when any approval rule triggers |

## Business Rules

1. No standalone credits: every credit note references an original invoice, and credited value per line can never exceed that line's uncredited balance (enforced cumulatively across multiple partial credits).
2. Credit pricing is inherited from the invoice line — including its discount and VAT rate snapshot — so the reversal exactly mirrors the original tax position.
3. `stock_returned = true` writes `RETURN_IN` ledger entries into the designated returns bin; sellable stock is only released to a picking bin after inspection sign-off.
4. Cash refunds require original tender cash AND same trading day; otherwise manager approval, and refunds above a threshold pay out by EFT rather than the drawer.
5. Serial-tracked returns must present a serial sold on that invoice; the serial transitions `sold → returned` ([1.7](../01-inventory-management/1.7-serial-batch-tracking.md)).
6. Warranty credits hold the unit in quarantine linked to a supplier claim; crediting the customer does not wait for the supplier's decision (customer-first policy), but the claim outcome is tracked against the credit.
7. `does_not_fit` return reasons raise a fitment review task ([1.6](../01-inventory-management/1.6-parts-fitment-guide.md)) with the sale's vehicle context.
8. Return windows are policy-configurable by category (e.g. electrical parts 7 days, no returns on cut-to-length hose); out-of-window returns need manager approval.
9. Credit notes use their own gapless number sequence (CN-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) and post GL and VAT reversals dated in an open period.
10. A posted credit note is immutable; an erroneous credit is corrected by a new invoice, never by editing.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **No receipt, cash customer**: staff search invoices by part, date range, and amount; if the invoice genuinely cannot be located, the return is refused (no invoice, no credit) — manager may authorise a goodwill discount on a future sale instead.
- **Return of a part bought before a price drop**: credit is at the invoiced (higher) price by rule 2 — the customer gets back what they paid.
- **Attempted double return** (same receipt presented twice): cumulative cap per line blocks the second credit; screen shows the earlier credit note.
- **Electrical part "returns"** (customer tested a coil pack by fitting it): category return policy flags electrical items as inspect-only; credit requires senior sign-off that the part is unused.
- **Exchange where the replacement is dearer**: combined flow charges only the difference; if cheaper, refunds the difference under the refund rules.
- **Original invoice in a now-closed VAT period**: credit note posts in the current open period with a cross-reference — history is never reopened.
- **Card reversal fails at the terminal**: settlement falls back to account credit or EFT with the failure logged; the credit note does not post until a settlement succeeds.
- **Short-delivery claim on a delivery signed "received in full"**: the POD on the delivery note ([2.9](2.9-delivery-notes.md)) is shown; approval requires manager review of the discrepancy.
