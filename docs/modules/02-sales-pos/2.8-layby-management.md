# 2.8 Lay-by Management

> Deposit-based payment plans: customer pays over time, goods release only when paid in full. Part of [Module 2: Sales & POS](../02-sales-pos.md).

## Purpose

Lay-by lets a cash-constrained customer secure a high-value part — a $180 battery for a Hilux, a shock absorber set, a reconditioned alternator — by paying a deposit now and instalments over weeks, collecting the goods only when fully paid. In markets where credit cards are rare and prices move with exchange rates, lay-by is a genuine sales channel: the customer locks today's price, and the business locks the sale.

The mechanics need care because a lay-by sits between a quote and a sale for weeks. The goods must be reserved (physically set aside in a lay-by holding bin and system-reserved so the counter can't sell them), payments must accumulate against the agreement with receipts per instalment, and the fiscal invoice only posts at completion when goods change hands. Meanwhile the agreement can go wrong: the customer stops paying (default), changes their mind (cancellation with a configurable forfeit/refund policy), or wants to swap an item mid-plan.

Discipline comes from a few hard rules — minimum deposit percentage before the agreement exists, no release below 100% paid, automatic overdue flagging for follow-up — so lay-by is a managed receivable-like exposure rather than a drawer of forgotten IOUs and dusty set-aside boxes.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Cashier | Create lay-by (deposit ≥ minimum), take instalments, print statements |
| Counter Sales | Above + item swaps within value (supervisor countersign) |
| Supervisor / Manager | Cancel lay-bys, approve refunds of deposits, extend completion dates |
| Storeman | Move goods to/from the lay-by holding bin |
| Branch Manager | Set policy: minimum deposit %, default period, forfeit rules; write off defaulted lay-bys |

## Workflow

```
Customer selects goods at POS ──► "Lay-by" instead of full payment
        │
        ▼
Agreement created: total, minimum deposit (e.g. 20%), expected completion date
        │
        ▼
Deposit taken (≥ minimum) ──► status: active
        ├── stock reserved (qty_reserved) + moved to lay-by holding bin
        └── receipt printed; agreement card/statement issued
        │
        ▼
Instalments over time (any amount, any tender) ──► paid_amount accumulates
        │
        ├── paid_amount = total ──► COMPLETE:
        │        invoice posts (2.4) → stock OUT → goods released → statement
        │
        ├── customer cancels ──► refund/forfeit per policy → stock back to shelf
        │
        └── overdue 30 days (config) ──► flagged for follow-up
                 └── no response → DEFAULTED → policy applies → stock back to shelf
```

1. The cart converts to a lay-by agreement; the system computes the minimum deposit from policy and blocks creation below it.
2. On activation, stock is reserved and physically moved to the lay-by holding bin with a ticket carrying the lay-by reference.
3. Each instalment is a receipted payment against the agreement — cash, card, or EFT — updating `paid_amount` and `remaining_amount`; the receipt shows the running balance.
4. At 100%, completion posts the tax invoice (prices as fixed at agreement start), writes stock out, and releases the goods.
5. Cancellations and defaults follow the configured policy: full refund, partial refund minus handling fee, or forfeit; goods return to sellable stock.

## Fields & Validation

Main form: lay-by agreement.

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| customer_id | FK | Yes | Real customer with phone number — CASH customer not allowed |
| lines (from cart) | list | Yes | ≥ 1 part line; special-order parts warn (goods must be on hand to set aside) |
| total_amount | decimal(15,2) | Auto | Cart total at agreement date; locked thereafter |
| minimum_deposit_pct | decimal(5,2) | Auto | From policy (e.g. 20%); manager may raise, not lower |
| deposit (first payment) | decimal(15,2) | Yes | ≥ total × minimum_deposit_pct |
| expected_completion | date | Yes | Defaults +90 days (policy); ≤ policy maximum |
| payments[].amount | decimal(15,2) | Yes | > 0 and ≤ remaining_amount (no overpayment) |
| payments[].payment_method_id | FK | Yes | Active method; reference required where method demands |

## Business Rules

1. No agreement exists until the minimum deposit is tendered; a failed deposit tender leaves only an ordinary abandoned cart.
2. Goods are reserved (`qty_reserved`) and bin-moved to lay-by holding for the life of the agreement; POS cannot sell them.
3. Release strictly requires `paid_amount = total_amount`; there is no partial release of some items (item-level release requires a formal split into two agreements, supervisor-approved).
4. Prices are locked at agreement date — later price rises or promotions do not alter an active lay-by.
5. The fiscal invoice posts at completion, not at deposit; instalments are held as customer deposits (liability), which Finance reports until conversion.
6. Cancellation settlement follows store policy: refund minus configurable handling fee, or forfeit after a grace period; all refunds follow the refund rules in [2.5](2.5-credit-notes-returns.md) (same-tender preference, manager approval for cash beyond limits).
7. Overdue = no payment for 30 days past schedule (configurable): flagged for follow-up with SMS/call task; defaulted after the policy's further window, with deposit handling per policy and goods returned to stock.
8. Extensions of `expected_completion` are supervisor actions, logged, and limited in count (e.g. max 2).
9. Item swaps re-total the agreement; a swap that lowers the total below `paid_amount` converts the excess to refund/credit under the cancellation rules.
10. Serial-tracked goods on lay-by keep status `in_stock` with a lay-by hold marker; the serial transitions to `sold` only at completion.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Final instalment overshoots** (owes $12.40, tenders $20 cash): payment capped at remaining; change given — `paid_amount` can never exceed total.
- **Customer loses the agreement card**: lookup by name/phone; statement reprints with full payment history and watermark.
- **Price of the goods drops mid-plan** (promo on that battery): price stays locked per rule 4; supervisor may apply a goodwill adjustment, which reduces total and is logged.
- **Reserved lay-by stock damaged in the holding bin**: write-off adjustment posts; replacement unit assigned to the agreement; customer unaffected.
- **Defaulted lay-by customer returns months later**: agreement is closed; policy amount (forfeit balance or refundable portion) is shown; any refundable amount pays out under refund rules — the goods are long since back on sale.
- **Customer wants goods early at 80% paid** ("I'll pay the rest Friday"): hard block on release; alternatives offered: pay balance on account terms (if a trade customer with credit) which completes the lay-by as an account invoice.
- **Branch closure/stock take freeze at completion time**: completion posting respects the freeze ([1.4](../01-inventory-management/1.4-stock-takes.md)); release waits until the freeze lifts.
- **VAT rate changes between deposit and completion**: the invoice posts at completion under the then-current rate; the gross agreement total is honoured, with the VAT split recomputed and the difference absorbed per fiscal policy — flagged to Finance either way.
