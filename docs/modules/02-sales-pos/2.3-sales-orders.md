# 2.3 Sales Orders

> Confirmed orders that reserve stock, manage back-orders, and drive picking and dispatch. Part of [Module 2: Sales & POS](../02-sales-pos.md).

## Purpose

A sales order is a commitment: the customer has said yes, and the business promises the stock. Unlike a quote it reserves inventory, and unlike a POS sale it separates commitment from fulfilment. That separation matters for three everyday situations: the trade customer who phones an order for collection tomorrow morning ("pick it tonight so my driver is in and out"), the part not currently in stock that must be back-ordered from the supplier, and the fleet order too large to assemble while the customer waits.

Reservation is the core mechanic. Confirming an order moves quantity into `qty_reserved`, so POS availability immediately reflects the promise — the counter cannot sell the last Hilux clutch kit out from under a confirmed order. Lines that cannot be covered by current stock become back-order lines, optionally raising purchase requisitions so the buyer sees demand-driven purchasing, not just reorder points.

Fulfilment runs a status pipeline — confirmed → picking → packed → dispatched → complete — with a printed picking list sorted by bin walk sequence, partial fulfilment for shipping what's available now, and invoicing of the delivered portion so revenue matches goods that actually moved.

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Counter Sales | Create and confirm orders within customer credit limits |
| Storeman / Picker | View pick lists, record picked/packed quantities |
| Sales Supervisor | Amend confirmed orders, approve over-credit confirmation, cancel orders |
| Dispatch Clerk | Mark dispatched, generate delivery notes |
| Branch Manager | Cancel with reservation release at any stage; back-order purchasing toggle |

## Workflow

```
Create order (customer, lines, expected dates, customer PO no.)
        │  status: draft — no stock effect
        ▼
Confirm ──► availability check per line
        │        ├─ covered → qty_reserved += qty
        │        └─ short   → line flagged back_order (optional auto-requisition)
        ▼
Picking: pick list printed (bin-sorted) → picker records picked qtys
        ▼
Packed → Dispatch ──► delivery note (2.9) ──► qty_delivered updated
        │                    │
        │                    └─ partial dispatch → remainder stays open/back-ordered
        ▼
Invoice fulfilled quantities (2.4) → stock OUT, reservation released for invoiced qty
        ▼
All lines delivered + invoiced → status: complete
```

1. Order capture mirrors the POS cart: fitment-aware part search, price-list pricing, customer PO reference, expected delivery date per line.
2. Confirmation is the commitment point: reservations post, credit exposure is checked (open orders count toward the limit), and back-order lines are identified.
3. The pick list prints in bin-walk order (primary bins from [1.3](../01-inventory-management/1.3-bin-locations.md)); pickers record actual picked quantities, surfacing shelf discrepancies early.
4. Dispatch generates the delivery note and updates `qty_delivered`; partial dispatches leave the order open for the balance.
5. Invoicing converts delivered quantities into a posted tax invoice; reservation for invoiced quantity is released as stock is written out of the ledger.

## Fields & Validation

Main form: sales order.

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| customer_id | FK | Yes | Active, not on credit hold (hold blocks confirmation, not draft) |
| customer_ref | string(100) | Cond. | Required if customer flagged "PO required" |
| delivery_address_id | FK | Cond. | Required when fulfilment method is delivery |
| lines[].part_id | FK | Yes | Active part |
| lines[].qty | decimal(10,2) | Yes | > 0 |
| lines[].unit_price | decimal(15,2) | Yes | Price-resolution; below-minimum needs approval |
| lines[].expected_date | date | No | ≥ today; defaults from supplier lead time for back-orders |
| lines[].qty_picked / qty_delivered | decimal(10,2) | Stage | 0 ≤ picked ≤ reserved; delivered ≤ picked |

## Business Rules

1. Confirming increments `qty_reserved` per covered line in `stock_levels`; draft orders never touch stock.
2. Cancelling a confirmed order (or removing a line) releases its reservation in the same transaction.
3. A back-order line generates an automatic draft purchase requisition when the branch setting is enabled; the requisition references the order so inbound stock can be allocated to it on receipt.
4. Reservation shortfall is never silent: if only 6 of 10 clutch plates are available, 6 reserve and 4 flag `is_back_order`.
5. Amending a confirmed order re-runs the availability and credit checks; quantity increases may split into new back-order portions.
6. Picked quantity cannot exceed reserved quantity; over-picks are rejected at entry.
7. Invoicing is limited to delivered (or collected) quantities; invoicing ahead of goods requires the proforma path instead ([2.4](2.4-tax-invoices.md)).
8. Orders untouched in `draft` for 14 days (configurable) are flagged stale; confirmed orders with no movement in 30 days appear on the aged-orders report.
9. When back-ordered stock arrives on a GRN, allocation to waiting order lines is oldest-order-first, and the salesperson is notified.
10. Credit check at confirmation includes existing AR balance plus all open confirmed orders, not just this order's value.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Picker finds fewer on the shelf than reserved** (bin says 4, system reserved 6): pick records 4, a spot-count task fires on the bin, and the shortfall converts to back-order rather than blocking dispatch of the 4.
- **Customer cancels after picking but before dispatch**: unpick returns stock to bins, reservation releases, cancellation reason logged; restocking fee policy applied via the order's charge line if configured.
- **Back-order arrives but the customer no longer wants it**: allocation notification includes a release action; released stock returns to free availability.
- **Two orders compete for the same inbound GRN stock**: oldest confirmed order wins allocation; the report shows allocation order so staff can override with supervisor authority.
- **Credit limit exceeded at confirmation** (order pushes exposure over): confirmation blocks with the exposure breakdown; supervisor override records approver and reason.
- **Partial dispatch then price list changes**: remaining balance invoices at the price captured on the order line, not the new list — order confirmation locks line pricing.
- **Customer PO number duplicated** (same customer_ref on two orders): warning with a link to the existing order to catch double-captured phone orders.
- **Serial-tracked line dispatched**: serials are chosen at picking, not confirmation, so reservation does not pin specific units prematurely.
