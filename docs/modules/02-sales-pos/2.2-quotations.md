# 2.2 Quotations

> Formal, expiring quotes with revisions, PDF email, and one-click conversion. Part of [Module 2: Sales & POS](../02-sales-pos.md).

## Purpose

Not every sale closes at the counter. A workshop pricing a Hilux front-brake overhaul (pads `BRK-PAD-0122`, discs `BRK-DSC-087`, wheel bearing kit) wants a written quote to put in front of their own customer; a fleet manager wants three service-kit options compared; an insurance job needs a documented price before authorisation. Quotations give these prospects a formal, numbered document (QT-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) with the customer's own price-list pricing, an expiry date, and a professional PDF.

Quotes are deliberately lightweight: they are non-binding, reserve no stock, and cost nothing to issue. Their value is in conversion — one click turns a won quote into a sales order (reserving stock) or straight into an invoice, copying every line without retyping. Revision tracking (REV1, REV2…) keeps a history when the customer negotiates, and win/loss reasons captured at closure feed sales analytics: are we losing on price, availability, or response time?

Expiry protects margins. Parts costs move with exchange rates and supplier increases; a quote honoured six months later at old prices is a loss-maker. Expired quotes must be re-quoted at current pricing rather than converted.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Counter Sales / Cashier | Create, edit own drafts, print/email, convert |
| Sales Rep | Full quote lifecycle for own customers; revisions |
| Sales Supervisor | Edit/convert any quote; approve below-minimum pricing on quotes |
| Branch Manager | Override expiry (re-validate + convert), view win/loss analytics |

## Workflow

```
Create quote (customer, vehicle, lines) ──► status: draft
        │
        ▼
Issue: print / email PDF ──► status: issued, expiry set (default +30 days)
        │
        ├── customer negotiates ──► new revision (REV2) supersedes REV1
        │
        ├── customer accepts
        │        │
        │        ▼
        │   Convert → Sales Order (stock reserved)  or → Invoice (immediate sale)
        │        │
        │        ▼
        │   status: won (conversion links parent quote)
        │
        ├── customer declines ──► status: lost + loss reason
        │
        └── expiry passes ──► status: expired (conversion blocked)
                                    │
                                    └──► "Re-quote" copies lines at current prices
```

1. Staff build the quote like a cart: part search with fitment filtering if a vehicle is set; prices resolve from the customer's price list.
2. Issuing fixes the quote number and starts the expiry clock; the PDF is printed or emailed from the system with a tracked timestamp.
3. Changes after issue create a revision — same base number, incremented REV; earlier revisions become read-only.
4. Conversion re-checks current prices and stock; differences are shown for confirmation before the order/invoice is created with `parent_id` linking back.
5. Closing a quote as lost requires a loss reason (`price`, `availability`, `lead_time`, `went_elsewhere`, `no_response`, `other`).

## Fields & Validation

Main form: quotation.

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| customer_id | FK | Yes | Active customer (CASH allowed with contact details captured) |
| document_date | date | Yes | Defaults today; not in the future |
| expiry_date | date | Yes | Defaults +30 days (configurable); must be after document_date |

| email_to | email | Cond. | Required for email action; validated format |
| loss_reason | enum | Cond. | Required when marking lost |

## Business Rules

1. Expired quotes cannot be converted; the re-quote action copies lines into a fresh quote priced at today's rates.
2. Quote prices are not locked: conversion re-resolves current pricing and displays a per-line old/new comparison the user must confirm. Honouring the quoted (lower) price beyond tolerance requires supervisor approval.
3. A quote never reserves stock; the availability column is informational and stamped "as at" the quote date.
4. Issued quotes are immutable — edits fork a new revision; only the latest revision is convertible.
5. Quote numbers are sequential per day (QT-YYYYMMDD-XXXX); revisions share the base number.
6. Converting marks the quote `won` and records the conversion document via `parent_id`; a quote converts at most once (partial conversion creates the order with the selected lines and closes the quote).
7. Discontinued parts on an old quote block conversion of those lines; the dialog offers superseding parts where they exist.
8. Win/loss data is mandatory at closure and drives the quote-conversion report (win rate by salesperson, loss reasons by value).
9. Automatic status sweep: quotes past expiry flip to `expired` nightly; a reminder task fires 5 days before expiry for quotes above a value threshold.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Price rise between issue and conversion** (supplier increase on Bosch lines): conversion comparison highlights changed lines in amber; user picks quoted vs current per line within authority.
- **Part superseded after quoting**: conversion swaps in the successor with a notice, keeping the quoted price if within tolerance.
- **Customer accepts an old revision** (has REV1 on paper, REV3 is latest): staff open the Revisions tab, compare, and either convert REV3 or re-issue matching REV1 with supervisor approval.
- **Quote emailed to a bouncing address**: email failure is surfaced on the Activity tab, not silently dropped.
- **Duplicate quotes for the same enquiry** by two staff: list view surfaces same-customer open quotes at creation ("3 open quotes for Mudzi Motors — continue?").
- **VAT rate changes before conversion**: conversion always applies the current rate; the comparison step shows the tax difference explicitly.
- **Conversion attempted while a line's stock is zero**: order conversion proceeds with the line flagged back-order ([2.3](2.3-sales-orders.md)); direct-to-invoice conversion blocks that line.
- **Expiry override request** (big fleet customer, quote lapsed Friday): branch manager uses re-validate + convert, which logs the override and re-checks pricing — there is no silent conversion of expired paper.
