# 10.4 Departments & Sections

> Organisational structure beneath branches — who works where in the business. Part of [Module 10: System Administration](../10-system-administration.md). **Status: existing implementation.**

## Purpose

Departments and Sections model the org chart: a two-level hierarchy (Department → Section) that users are assigned to alongside their branch. Where branches answer "which location", departments answer "which function" — Parts Sales, Workshop/Service, Purchasing/Procurement, Finance & Accounts, Warehouse/Stores, and Management, per the suggested list in the parent doc.

The structure earns its keep in three places. HR-ish administration: user lists filter by department, so "everyone in Warehouse/Stores" is one click. Reporting: staff-dimension reports (sales by salesperson, technician efficiency) can roll up by department. And workflow routing: approval chains and notifications can target a department ("notify Purchasing when a requisition is created") rather than hard-coding individuals, which survives staff turnover.

Sections give finer grain where a department is large — e.g. Parts Sales split into Counter Sales and Telesales, or Warehouse/Stores into Receiving and Dispatch.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Super Admin | Full CRUD on departments and sections |
| Owner | Full CRUD |
| Branch Manager | View; assign own-branch users to existing departments/sections |
| All other roles | None (their department shows on their profile) |

## Workflow

```
[Define departments]
        │
        ▼
[Parts Sales | Workshop/Service | Purchasing |
 Finance & Accounts | Warehouse/Stores | Management]
        │
        ▼
[Add sections where needed]
   e.g. Warehouse/Stores ─┬─ Receiving
                          └─ Dispatch
        │
        ▼
[Assign users (10.1) to department + section]
        │
        ▼
[Consume: user filters, report roll-ups,
 notification/approval targeting]
```

1. **Create departments** — usually once at setup, from the suggested list; add as the business grows.
2. **Create sections** under a department where the team is big enough to need it.
3. **Assign users** — each user record carries department (and optional section) alongside branch.
4. **Reorganise** — moving a section to another department, or a user between departments, is an edit; history of who sat where lives in the activity log.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Departments list | `admin.departments.index` | Departments with section and user counts |
| Sections list | `admin.sections.index` | Sections grouped by department |
| Create/edit forms | `admin.departments.*` / `admin.sections.*` | Simple name/description forms |

**Primary screen — Departments list**: table of department name, description, section count, user count, active status. Row expand lists its sections inline with their user counts. "Add Department" and per-row "Add Section" keep the hierarchy manageable from one screen.

## Fields & Validation

Department:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| name | text | Yes | max 100, unique |
| description | text | No | max 255 |
| is_active | boolean | Yes | default true |

Section:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| department_id | select | Yes | active department |
| name | text | Yes | max 100, unique within department |
| description | text | No | max 255 |
| is_active | boolean | Yes | default true |

## Business Rules

1. Sections always belong to exactly one department; a section cannot exist unparented.
2. A department cannot be deleted while sections or users reference it — deactivate instead; deactivation hides it from assignment dropdowns but preserves history.
3. Deactivating a department with active users prompts for their reassignment first.
4. Department names are unique company-wide; section names are unique within their department (two departments may each have an "Admin" section).
5. Users may have a department without a section, but a section only together with its owning department — the user form enforces the pairing.
6. Departments are company-wide, not per-branch: the Harare and Bulawayo branches share one "Parts Sales" department; the branch dimension comes from the user's branch.
7. Structure changes are activity-logged; reports that roll up by department use the assignment as at the reporting date where the data model supports it, otherwise current assignment (the report states which).

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Section moved to a different department** (reorg): allowed; users in the section follow it. The change is logged and dated.
- **User assigned a section whose department differs from their department field**: blocked by validation — the pair must be consistent.
- **Department renamed** ("Stores" → "Warehouse/Stores"): rename in place is safe; references are by id. Historic reports show the current name.
- **Empty department kept for future use**: allowed; zero-user departments simply show 0 and stay out of the way.
- **Duplicate creation race** (two admins adding "Telesales"): uniqueness constraint rejects the second with a clear message.
- **Workshop department at a non-workshop branch**: assignment is not blocked (departments are company-wide) but the anomaly is visible in the user list filters.
