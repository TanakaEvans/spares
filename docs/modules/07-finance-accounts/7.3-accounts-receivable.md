# 7.3 Accounts Receivable

> Customer invoices, receipts, and allocations — the sub-ledger behind Trade Debtors Control. Part of [Module 7: Finance & Accounts](../07-finance-accounts.md).

## Purpose

Accounts receivable tracks every dollar customers owe the business and every dollar they pay. It is the sub-ledger for the Trade Debtors Control account (1210): the sum of all open customer balances in AR must equal the control account balance in the GL at all times, because both move together — a credit invoice posts DR AR Control / CR Sales Revenue (+ CR VAT Output), and a receipt posts DR Bank / CR AR Control.

The daily work of AR is **receipting and allocation**. A garage pays one EFT of $14,730.50 covering four invoices less a credit note; the AR clerk captures the receipt (`customer_receipts`, split by payment method in `customer_receipt_payment_lines`) and allocates it line-by-line to the open invoices (`receipt_allocations`). Allocation is what makes the ageing truthful: an unallocated receipt reduces the balance but leaves specific invoices looking overdue, which is why unapplied credits must be cleared within 5 business days.

AR is also where Customer Management meets Finance: the ageing computed here drives credit checks and collections ([5.3](../05-customer-management/5.3-credit-management.md)), and the transactions recorded here are what statements ([5.5](../05-customer-management/5.5-statements.md)) print.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| AR Clerk / Cashier | Capture and allocate receipts; view customer ledgers |
| Accountant | Post receipts; reallocate; process refunds and small-balance write-offs |
| Credit Controller | Full view; dispute flags; drives collections from the ageing |
| Financial Manager | Approve refunds, write-offs above threshold, receipt reversals |
| Counter Sales | Take payment at POS (which creates receipt lines automatically); no back-office AR access |

## Workflow

Main process: receive and allocate a customer payment.

```
EFT appears on bank / customer pays at counter
        │
New Receipt: customer, date, total, bank reference
        │
Payment method lines (EFT / cash / card split)
sum must equal receipt total
        │
Allocation screen: open invoices listed oldest-first
        │
   auto-allocate oldest-first ──or── manual per remittance advice
        │
Allocated total ≤ receipt total
        │      └─ remainder stays "unapplied credit"
        ▼
Post ──> GL journal: DR Bank, CR AR Control
        │   customer balance ↓, ageing recalculated
        ▼
Unapplied credit? ──> on clerk's follow-up list,
must be allocated within 5 business days
```

1. **Capture.** The receipt header records who paid, when, how much, and the bank/deposit reference that ties it to the bank statement (feeds reconciliation, [7.5](7.5-cash-bank-management.md)).
2. **Split by method.** One receipt may combine tender types (cash + card at the counter); method lines must sum to the header total.
3. **Allocate.** Default is oldest-first auto-allocation; the clerk overrides to match the customer's remittance advice — customers get grumpy when their payment for invoice B is applied to disputed invoice A.
4. **Post.** Posting writes the GL journal and locks the receipt; the customer's balance and ageing update immediately, which can auto-release a credit hold ([5.3](../05-customer-management/5.3-credit-management.md)).
5. **Chase the unapplied.** Unallocated remainders appear on ageing as "Unapplied Credits" until resolved — allocated, refunded, or (tiny balances) written off.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| AR receipts | `finance.receipts.index` | Receipt register: drafts, posted, unapplied credits |
| Receipt capture/allocation | within receipts index | Header, method lines, allocation grid |
| Customer ledger | via customer profile / GL enquiry | All AR transactions for one customer |
| AR ageing report | via [7.7](7.7-financial-reporting.md) | Balances by bucket, drill to invoices |

**Primary screen — Receipt capture/allocation:**
- **Header:** receipt number (on post), customer search, receipt date, total amount, bank reference, status chip (draft/posted).
- **Main area:** payment-method lines block (method, amount, reference); allocation grid — open invoices with number, date, due date, original, outstanding, allocation input per row; footer totals: receipt total, allocated, unapplied remainder.
- **Actions:** Auto-Allocate Oldest First, Clear Allocations, Save Draft, Post, Print Receipt, Refund Unapplied (approval-gated).

## Fields & Validation

Receipt form:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Customer | FK search | Yes | Active customer (blacklisted customers can still pay — only sales are blocked) |
| Receipt date | date | Yes | In an open period; not future |
| Total amount | decimal(15,2) | Yes | > 0 |
| Reference | text(100) | Yes for EFT | Bank ref / deposit slip number |
| Method line: payment method | FK select | Yes | Active method; card lines require auth reference |
| Method line: amount | decimal(15,2) | Yes | > 0; Σ method lines = header total exactly |
| Allocation: amount applied | decimal(15,2) | No | 0 < amount ≤ invoice outstanding; Σ allocations ≤ receipt total |
| Allocation target | FK | — | Open (unpaid/partially paid) invoice of **this customer** only |

## Business Rules

1. A receipt can be split across multiple invoices (parent rule) and an invoice can be settled by multiple receipts — allocation is many-to-many via `receipt_allocations`.
2. Unallocated receipts appear on ageing as "Unapplied Credits" and must be allocated within 5 business days (parent rule); day 3 puts them on the clerk's follow-up list, day 5 escalates to the Credit Controller.
3. Posting a receipt creates DR Bank / CR AR Control (parent rule); the bank leg posts to the GL account linked to the deposit bank account, and cash tenders post to the till account until banked.
4. Reallocation after posting moves allocations between invoices without touching the GL (the money already moved); every reallocation is audit-logged with before/after and reason.
5. Receipt reversal (bounced EFT/cheque) requires Financial Manager approval, creates the opposite GL journal, de-allocates, restores invoice outstanding amounts — and triggers the automatic `returned_payment` credit hold ([5.3](../05-customer-management/5.3-credit-management.md)).
6. Overpayments may be refunded (approval-gated, paid via AP-style bank payment) or held as unapplied credit at the customer's request; small residuals under the write-off threshold (e.g. $1) may be written off to a rounding account by the Accountant.
7. Early-settlement discounts ([5.2](../05-customer-management/5.2-trade-accounts.md)) are effected as system-generated credit notes during allocation when the discount window is met — never by short-allocating silently.
8. AR-to-GL reconciliation runs nightly: Σ open customer balances vs Trade Debtors Control balance; any difference raises an alarm with the day's candidate journals, and period close ([7.8](7.8-period-management.md)) requires a zero difference.
9. Ageing buckets are computed from invoice **due date** (Current / 30 / 60 / 90+), consistent with [5.3](../05-customer-management/5.3-credit-management.md).

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Receipt allocated to the wrong invoice:** use reallocation (rule 4) — not reversal — since the cash was real; the customer's next statement simply shows corrected ageing. If the receipt was captured against the wrong **customer**, reverse and recapture; allocations cannot cross customers.
- **Customer pays an already-paid invoice (duplicate EFT):** allocation finds no outstanding amount; the amount parks as unapplied credit and the clerk contacts the customer — refund or hold against future invoices.
- **Payment received for exactly the invoice total less bank charges ($10,000 invoice, $9,985 received):** allocate 9,985, then either recover the $15 from the customer or write off to bank charges with approval — the allocation screen's shortfall helper offers both paths explicitly.
- **Period closed mid-posting:** the receipt post fails the period check atomically; re-date to the open period (the receipt date, not the money, is what moves — the bank rec matches on reference).
- **Cheque deposited then returned after allocation:** reversal flow (rule 5) restores everything; the ageing instantly shows the invoices overdue again and the hold fires.
- **Customer pays before the invoice exists (deposit on a special order):** captured as unapplied credit on the receipt; when the invoice posts, allocation clears it. Lay-by deposits are different — they post to the lay-by deferred-revenue account via the sales flow, not AR.
- **Two clerks allocate the same invoice concurrently:** allocation validates outstanding at commit; the second save re-loads with the reduced outstanding and the clerk adjusts.

## Key Tables

- `customer_receipts` — receipt headers with GL journal link (schema in [Module 7](../07-finance-accounts.md#73-accounts-receivable-ar)).

- `receipt_allocations` — receipt ↔ invoice application amounts.
