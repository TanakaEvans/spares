# 7.4 Accounts Payable

> Supplier invoices, payment runs, and allocations — the sub-ledger behind Trade Creditors Control. Part of [Module 7: Finance & Accounts](../07-finance-accounts.md).

## Purpose

Accounts payable tracks what the business owes its suppliers and pays it on the right day — not early (cash flow), not late (credit stops from the very distributors the counter depends on). AP is the sub-ledger for the Trade Creditors Control account (2110): supplier invoices arrive, are matched against GRNs and PO prices (three-way match), and post DR AP Accruals / CR AP Control; payments post DR AP Control / CR Bank. The sum of open supplier balances must always equal the control account.

The centrepiece is the **payment run**: rather than paying invoices ad hoc, the accountant periodically (typically monthly, aligned to supplier statement dates) selects due invoices across suppliers, reviews, generates a payment batch, exports it in the bank's EFT format, and posts. This concentrates approval into one controlled event, produces one clean audit trail, and lets the business time payments to terms — paying a Net 60 importer at day 59, and a 2.5%-settlement-discount local at day 7.

AP is also the fraud chokepoint: payments only go to verified bank details ([6.1](../06-supplier-management/6.1-supplier-profiles.md) dual control), and the run enforces that invoices are matched before they are payable — no GRN, no payment.

| Role | Access |

| Accountant | Approve matches with tolerances; build and post payment runs |
| Financial Manager | Approve payment runs (mandatory second approval); release the EFT export; approve manual/urgent payments |
| Purchasing Manager | Resolve price/quantity disputes with suppliers |
| Auditor (read-only) | Full view of invoices, matches, runs, and exports |

## Workflow

Main process: the payment run.

```
Accountant starts run: date range + supplier filter + bank account
        │
System lists outstanding invoices:
  due date ≤ cutoff, matched, not disputed,
  supplier bank details verified
        │
Review: select/deselect per invoice; take settlement
discounts where windows still open; credits netted
per supplier
        │
Batch generated: one payment per supplier
(PMT-YYYYMMDD-XXXX each), allocations to invoices recorded
        │
Financial Manager approval (four-eyes)
        │
Export bank EFT file (bank-specific CSV/XML)
──> upload to bank; bank processes
        │
Post payments ──> per payment: DR AP Control, CR Bank
        │
Remittance advices emailed to each supplier's
Accounts contact (6.5)
```

1. **Selection.** The run proposes everything due by the cutoff; blocked invoices (unmatched, disputed, unverified bank) are listed with reasons rather than silently excluded.
2. **Review.** The accountant curates — deferring an invoice for cash-flow reasons is a visible decision on the run record, not a forgotten piece of paper.
3. **Netting.** Supplier credit notes are offset within the same payment so the supplier receives the net and the remittance shows the arithmetic.
4. **Four-eyes.** The builder cannot release; the Financial Manager approves the batch total and spot-checks new/changed bank details.
5. **Export, pay, post.** The EFT file is generated once per batch (re-download allowed, re-generation versioned); posting writes one GL journal per payment and closes the allocated invoices.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| AP payments | `finance.payments.index` | Payment register: runs, single payments, statuses |
| Payment run | `finance.payment-run` | Build, review, approve, export a run |
| Supplier invoice capture/match | via purchasing/AP screens | Invoice entry and three-way match |
| Supplier ledger | via supplier profile | All AP transactions for one supplier |

**Primary screen — Payment run (`finance.payment-run`):**
- **Header:** run reference, cutoff date, paying bank account (with available balance), totals: proposed / selected / batch count; status stepper (Draft → Approved → Exported → Posted).
- **Main area:** supplier-grouped invoice grid — invoice number, date, due date, terms, gross, discount available (with expiry), credits applied, net to pay; blocked-invoice panel with reasons; bank-detail-change warnings highlighted.
- **Actions:** Select All Due, Take Available Discounts, Remove Invoice (reason), Submit for Approval, Approve (FM), Export EFT File, Post Payments, Email Remittances.

## Fields & Validation

Supplier invoice capture (the main entry form feeding AP):

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Supplier | FK select | Yes | Active supplier |
| Supplier invoice number | text(50) | Yes | Unique per supplier — duplicate blocks capture (double-payment guard) |
| Invoice date / due date | date | Yes | Due date auto-computed from terms; override logged |
| Currency / amounts | decimal(15,2) | Yes | Currency must equal supplier currency; gross = net + VAT within 1 cent |

| GRN reference(s) | FK multi | Yes for stock | Three-way match: invoice lines vs GRN quantities vs PO prices; tolerance (e.g. 2% or $50) auto-passes, beyond → dispute |
| Payment run fields: | | | |
| Cutoff date | date | Yes | Today or future |
| Bank account | FK select | Yes | Active account with EFT format configured; batch total ≤ available balance warning |
| Approval | workflow | Yes | Builder ≠ approver enforced |

## Business Rules

1. Only matched, undisputed invoices are payable; the match posts DR AP Accruals / CR AP Control (parent posting table), clearing the accrual raised at GRN.
2. `supplier invoice number + supplier` uniqueness is a hard block — the classic duplicate-invoice fraud/error is stopped at capture, with the original shown.
3. Payments only to suppliers with **verified** bank details ([6.1](../06-supplier-management/6.1-supplier-profiles.md)); a bank-detail change after run approval invalidates the approval for that supplier's payment.
4. Four-eyes on every run: builder and approver must be different users; single urgent payments outside a run require Financial Manager approval individually.
5. Settlement discounts are taken only when payment will reach the supplier within the window; taking one raises a system credit against the invoice, mirroring AR's treatment.
6. Payment posting is per payment: DR AP Control / CR Bank, with `payment_allocations` recording exactly which invoices each payment settles; partial payments leave invoices open for the balance.
7. A payment reversal (bank rejects the EFT line) reopens its allocated invoices, reverses the GL journal, and flags the supplier's bank details for re-verification.
8. Supplier statement reconciliation: monthly, the AP clerk ticks the supplier's statement against the ledger; unmatched statement lines (invoices we never received) generate chase tasks — protecting the credit relationship.
9. AP-to-GL reconciliation runs nightly (Σ supplier balances vs Creditors Control) and must be zero at period close ([7.8](7.8-period-management.md)).
10. Foreign-currency invoices post at transaction-date rate; settlement differences post to a realised-FX account automatically at payment.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Invoice arrives before the goods (import shipment on the water):** capture is allowed but the invoice stays `unmatched` and unpayable until the GRN lands; the blocked panel shows it with "awaiting GRN".
- **Price on invoice exceeds PO price beyond tolerance:** the line disputes automatically; the Purchasing Manager resolves (supplier credit note, or accept with reason); disputed invoices are excluded from runs and from the supplier's invoice-accuracy KPI resolution feeds ([6.4](../06-supplier-management/6.4-supplier-performance.md)).
- **EFT file exported but never uploaded to the bank (discovered at reconciliation):** payments posted but no bank movement — the bank rec ([7.5](7.5-cash-bank-management.md)) shows unmatched GL entries; the run screen supports "mark batch failed", reversing the postings cleanly for re-export.
- **Bank rejects one line of a 40-payment batch (closed account):** single-payment reversal (rule 7) without disturbing the other 39.
- **Supplier on credit stop demands immediate payment mid-month:** urgent single payment flow with FM approval; the payment still requires matched invoices and verified bank details — urgency never bypasses controls.
- **Credit notes exceed invoices for a supplier in the run:** net negative suppliers are excluded from the batch (banks don't collect on EFT); the credit balance carries forward or is claimed as a refund.
- **Period closed between approval and posting:** posting re-validates the period; the payment date re-dates to the open period with both dates kept (payment date vs posting date) so the bank rec still matches.
- **Same GRN matched to two invoices (supplier split-bills):** the match consumes GRN quantities cumulatively; over-consumption blocks with the earlier invoice referenced.

## Key Tables

- `supplier_payments` — payment headers with bank account and GL journal link (schema in [Module 7](../07-finance-accounts.md#74-accounts-payable-ap)).
- `payment_allocations` — payment ↔ supplier-invoice applications.
- Supplier invoices and match/dispute records — captured in Purchasing/AP tables; `suppliers.balance` is the cached AP balance ([6.1](../06-supplier-management/6.1-supplier-profiles.md)).
- Payment-run batch and EFT-export tables — run composition, approvals, export versions.
