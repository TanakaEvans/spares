# 7.2 General Ledger

> The double-entry journal engine — every financial event becomes a balanced journal here. Part of [Module 7: Finance & Accounts](../07-finance-accounts.md).

## Purpose

The general ledger is the single financial truth of the business. Every transaction with financial impact — a counter sale, a GRN, a stock write-off, a bank charge — ends life as a `gl_journals` header with balanced `gl_journal_lines` (SUM of debits = SUM of credits, enforced at the service layer). Reports (7.7) are queries over these lines; there is no second set of books.

Most journals are **automatic**: the sales module posts DR Cash / CR Sales Revenue / CR VAT Output when an invoice completes; purchasing posts DR Inventory / CR AP Accruals on GRN; COGS posts with each sale. The full mapping lives in the [posting architecture table](../07-finance-accounts.md#posting-architecture). Automatic journals carry `source_type`/`source_id` back to their business document, so any GL line drills to the invoice or GRN that caused it. **Manual journals** exist for what no module automates — depreciation, accruals, corrections — and are deliberately constrained: no posting to control accounts, no posting to closed periods, reversal instead of edit.

The GL is organised into financial years and periods (`gl_years`, `gl_periods`); the open/close lifecycle of those periods is managed in [7.8 Period Management](7.8-period-management.md), and the GL enforces it on every posting.

## Workflow

Main process: manual journal entry (e.g. monthly depreciation).

```
Accountant: New Journal (type `general`)
        │
Header: journal date, description, reference
        │  └─ system resolves period from date; closed period?
        │     → blocked, suggests re-dating to open period
        ▼
Lines: account + description + debit OR credit per line
        │  (control accounts absent from picker)
        ▼
Running totals shown: DR total / CR total / difference
        │
Save draft  ──> editable, not in TB
        │
Post ──> validation: balanced? period open? accounts active
        │  and postable? amount over threshold → FM approval
        ▼
Journal `posted`: JNL-YYYYMMDD-XXXX, immutable,
in trial balance immediately
        │
Mistake found later ──> Reverse ──> new opposite journal,
original marked `reversed`, both visible forever
```

1. **Header.** Date drives period resolution; the reference field links to any external paper (depreciation schedule, accrual memo).
2. **Lines.** Each line is one account with either a debit or a credit (never both); descriptions per line make the enquiry readable years later.
3. **Draft vs posted.** Drafts are workspace only. Posting is the moment of truth: balance check, period check, account checks, numbering, immutability.
4. **Reversal, never edit.** A posted journal is corrected by an automatic mirror journal (`reversed_journal_id` links both ways), then re-entering correctly. History is complete by construction.
5. **Recurring templates.** Standing monthly journals (depreciation, rent accrual) generate drafts on schedule for review and posting — automation of typing, not of judgement.

**Primary screen — GL enquiry (`finance.gl.index`):**
- **Header:** account selector (code/name search), period/date-range filter, journal-type filter, include-reversed toggle; opening balance, movement, closing balance for the selection.
- **Main area:** line grid — date, journal number, type, description, reference, debit, credit, running balance; each row drills to the journal detail, and automatic journals drill onward to their source document (`source_type`/`source_id`).
- **Actions:** Export to Excel, Print Account Ledger, Jump to Source Document, New Manual Journal.

## Fields & Validation

Manual journal form:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Journal date | date | Yes | Must resolve to an **open** period; future dates beyond the open period blocked |
| Journal type | select | Yes | Manual entry restricted to `general` (and `opening` during setup); other types are system-reserved |
| Description | text(255) | Yes | Min 5 chars |
| Reference | text(100) | No | External document reference |
| Line: account | FK select | Yes | Active, `allow_direct_posting = true`, not a control account |
| Line: description | text(255) | Yes | Defaults from header, editable per line |
| Line: debit / credit | decimal(15,2) | One of | Exactly one of the pair > 0 per line; other must be 0.00 |
| Lines (whole set) | — | ≥ 2 | SUM(debit) = SUM(credit) exactly (integer-cents comparison, no float tolerance) |
| Approval | workflow | Conditional | Journals with total DR over the configured threshold require Financial Manager approval before posting |

## Business Rules

1. Journals in a closed or locked period cannot be posted; the system suggests re-dating to the current open period (parent rule). Locked periods additionally block reversals dated into them — the reversal must be dated in an open period.
2. Reversals create a new journal with opposite debits/credits (parent rule); a journal can be reversed once only, and a reversal journal cannot itself be "reversed" — the correction is a fresh journal.
3. Control accounts accept postings only via sub-ledger posting (parent rule); the service layer enforces this even for code paths, not just the UI picker.
4. Posted journals are immutable in every field; drafts are freely editable and carry no journal number until posted.
5. `journal_number` (JNL-YYYYMMDD-XXXX) is issued at posting time, gap-free per day, and never reused — including for journals later reversed.
6. Automatic postings must carry `source_type` and `source_id`; a business document may not post twice — the service layer is idempotent per source, so a retried invoice posting cannot double-book revenue.
7. Multi-branch postings carry the branch dimension on lines so branch-filtered reporting (7.7) reconciles to the whole.
8. Recurring templates generate **drafts**, never auto-post, and skip generation into closed periods with a notification instead.
9. Every post, approval, and reversal records user and timestamp; the enquiry can always answer "who put this here and why".

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Period closed mid-posting (close races a draft being posted):** posting validates the period status inside the same transaction as the insert; the loser gets "period Jan 2026 was closed at 17:42 by J. Moyo — re-date to Feb 2026?" rather than a half-posted journal.
- **Unbalanced journal from a rounding split (e.g. 3-way allocation of $100):** the line editor shows the 1-cent difference and offers "post difference to rounding account" explicitly; it will not absorb it silently.
- **Automatic posting fails (unmapped account, closed period) during a sale:** the business document completes for the customer but enters a `posting_failed` queue monitored by the Accountant; the failure reason is stored and re-posting is one click after the mapping/period fix. Sales are never blocked at the counter by a GL configuration problem, and the queue must be empty before period close ([7.8](7.8-period-management.md)).
- **Reversal of an automatic journal:** allowed only from the source document's own reversal flow (credit note, GRN reversal) so the sub-ledger and GL stay in step; the GL screen's reverse button is disabled for journals with a source, pointing at the right flow.
- **Draft saved in a period that closes before posting:** posting fails the period check; the "re-date" helper updates the journal date and re-resolves the period in one step.
- **Two users post drafts simultaneously at day-end:** journal numbering is allocated under a lock at post time — no duplicate JNL numbers, no gaps from failed posts (number only issued on success).
- **Opening balances at go-live:** a one-time `opening` journal type, postable only while the first year's status allows it, establishes the seed trial balance; it is excluded from movement reports and P&L.
