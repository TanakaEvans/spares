# 7.8 Period Management

> Opening, closing, and locking financial periods — the discipline that makes the books final. Part of [Module 7: Finance & Accounts](../07-finance-accounts.md).

## Purpose

A ledger that anyone can post into at any date is never finished: last month's P&L changes retroactively every time a back-dated invoice slips in, statements no longer match what customers received, and the submitted VAT return diverges from the books beneath it. Period management imposes the boundary: the financial year (`gl_years`) divides into monthly periods (`gl_periods`), each moving one-way through `open` → `closed` → `locked`. Posting is only possible into open periods ([7.2](7.2-general-ledger.md) enforces this on every journal); closing a period is the declaration that its figures are final.

The month-end close is a checklist, not a button: all sales invoices posted, supplier invoices matched, bank accounts reconciled, AR/AP ageing reviewed and reconciled to their control accounts, depreciation posted, trial balance reviewed — then close. The system turns that checklist into enforced gates, because a close that skips reconciliation is just a lie with a timestamp.

Year-end extends the cycle: adjustments posted, full statements produced, the year **locked** (stronger than closed — not reopenable by ordinary means), and opening balances rolled into the new year, with the year's profit rolled into retained earnings. Locking is what lets the business hand statements to its bank and auditors knowing the underlying data cannot quietly shift.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Accountant | Run close checklist; prepare close; open next period |
| Financial Manager | Execute period close; reopen a closed period (with reason); execute year-end |
| Bookkeeper / Clerks | See period status (blocked postings explain themselves); no period actions |
| System Admin | Create financial years/calendars at setup; cannot close/reopen |
| Auditor (read-only) | Close history, checklist evidence, reopen audit trail |

## Workflow

Main process: month-end close (per the parent doc's sequence).

```
Period end approaches (e.g. 31 Jan)
        │
Close checklist opens for Period 01:
  [1] All sales invoices for the period posted
      (posting-failure queue empty ── 7.2)
  [2] All supplier invoices matched (no unmatched GRNs
      older than period end ── 7.4)
  [3] Bank reconciliations complete for all
      active accounts ── 7.5
  [4] AR ageing reconciles to Debtors Control ── 7.3
      AP ageing reconciles to Creditors Control ── 7.4
      (queries resolved)
  [5] Depreciation journal posted (recurring template ── 7.2)
  [6] Trial balance reviewed ── 7.7
        │  each gate: auto-checked where possible,
        │  sign-off recorded where judgement is involved
        ▼
All gates green ──> Financial Manager closes period
        │            status → `closed`; snapshot taken
        ▼
Next period opened (if not already)
        │
─── Year end (after Period 12 closes) ───
  Post year-end adjustments (audit adjustments)
  Run full financial statements
  Lock the year: all periods → `locked`
  Roll forward: opening balances into new year,
  P&L result → Retained Earnings
```

1. **Gates, auto-checked.** Items 1–4 are queries the system runs live: the checklist shows red counts with drill-through (e.g. "3 unposted documents") until each hits zero.
2. **Gates, signed off.** Items 5–6 record who reviewed and when — judgement steps leave a named trail.
3. **Close.** One action, FM permission, snapshot of the trial balance stored for reproducibility ([7.7](7.7-financial-reporting.md)).
4. **Reopen (exception).** A closed — never locked — period can be reopened by the FM with a mandatory reason; every reopen and re-close is audit-logged, and affected downstream artefacts (statements, VAT) are flagged for review.
5. **Year-end.** Runs only when all 12 periods are closed; produces closing entries, locks the year, and seeds the new year's opening balances via an `opening` journal.

## Fields & Validation

Period and close forms:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Financial year name | text(20) | Yes | e.g. "FY2026"; unique |
| Year start / end | date | Yes | Contiguous with previous year (no gap/overlap); 12 monthly periods generated |
| Period status | workflow | — | `open` → `closed` → `locked` only; `closed` → `open` via reopen (FM + reason); `locked` is terminal |
| Close action | action | FM | Enabled only when: all checklist gates green, all **prior** periods closed (no closing March before February) |
| Reopen reason | textarea | Yes | Min 20 chars; stored permanently with user/timestamp |
| Sign-off (judgement gates) | confirm | Yes | Named user confirmation per gate; cannot be the same action as the close itself |
| Year-end run | action | FM | Requires all periods closed, TB in balance, and confirmation of the retained-earnings roll amount |

## Business Rules

1. Journals cannot post into closed or locked periods ([7.2](7.2-general-ledger.md) parent rule); the block applies equally to automatic postings — a back-dated business document is re-dated or rejected, never silently posted into a closed month.
2. Periods close strictly in sequence; a period cannot close while any earlier period in the year is open.
3. Only one period per year is normally open for posting; the FM may hold two open briefly during month-end transition (old month finishing, new month trading), shown prominently as a temporary state.
4. Closing takes a trial-balance snapshot; closed-period reports must reproduce it ([7.7](7.7-financial-reporting.md)).
5. Reopening is allowed for `closed` only, requires FM + reason, and automatically flags: statements already sent for that period ([5.5](../05-customer-management/5.5-statements.md)), and VAT returns covering it ([7.6](7.6-vat-management.md)) — the correction rules of those modules then apply.
6. Locking (year-end) is one-way; corrections to locked years are posted as current-year adjustments with disclosure, matching accounting practice.
7. Year-end closing entries zero the revenue and expense accounts into Current Year Profit/Loss, then roll into Retained Earnings; the new year's `opening` journal carries only balance-sheet accounts.
8. The checklist's auto-gates cannot be waived; judgement gates can be signed off but never skipped — a close with an unsigned gate is impossible, not discouraged.
9. Every close, reopen, and year-end action records user, timestamp, and (for reopens) reason, retained permanently.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Period closed mid-posting elsewhere:** the racing journal fails atomically with the closed-at detail and a re-date suggestion ([7.2](7.2-general-ledger.md)); nothing half-posts across the boundary.
- **Trading continues on the 1st while month-end drags:** the two-open-periods transition (rule 3) lets February trade while January's checklist completes — the standing pressure metric ("January open 9 days into February") keeps the transition short.
- **Supplier invoice for January arrives on 20 February, January closed:** the invoice posts into February with its true document date preserved; VAT and ageing handle prior-dated documents by design ([7.6](7.6-vat-management.md) rule 2, [7.4](7.4-accounts-payable.md)) — reopening is not the tool for routine stragglers.
- **Reopen requested to "fix" a figure after statements went out:** allowed with reason, but the flags (rule 5) force acknowledgement that customer statements and possibly the VAT return are affected; the correction may belong in the current period instead, and the checklist says so.
- **Year-end run with Period 12 closed but a bank rec exception carried forward:** the year-end gate re-validates all close gates at year level; carried-forward exceptions block the lock until resolved or FM-accepted with disclosure notes.
- **Checklist gate flickers (new failure after sign-off, before close):** gates re-evaluate at the close action itself; a gate that regressed re-opens with the new failure listed — sign-offs don't grandfather stale states.
- **New installation mid-year:** the first year is created with an opening-balances period; `opening` journals ([7.2](7.2-general-ledger.md)) are permitted only while the year carries its initial status, then the normal cycle takes over.
- **Calendar year vs financial year confusion:** the year record's start/end drive everything; a March-February financial year renders Period 01 = March — screens always show period names with dates to prevent off-by-one-month postings.
