# 5.3 Credit Management

> Enforces credit limits, tracks aged debt, and manages holds and blacklists. Part of [Module 5: Customer Management](../05-customer-management.md).

## Purpose

Credit management is where the business protects its cash. A spares shop with 200 garage accounts can easily carry $500k of debtors' book; without disciplined limits and ageing follow-up, slow payers turn into bad debt. This sub-module runs the automated credit check on every on-account sale, maintains the aged debt analysis that drives collections, and controls the on-hold and blacklist mechanisms that stop the bleeding when an account goes bad.

The credit check must be fast and unavoidable: it runs inside the POS/invoicing flow, not as an after-the-fact report. Counter staff see a clear pass/fail with the reason ("R12,400 over limit", "invoice INV-2231 is 71 days overdue"), and the escalation path — supervisor override PIN — is deliberate and audited, never a silent bypass.

Ageing is the collections engine: the Credit Controller works the 60- and 90-day buckets daily, logging every promise-to-pay in the communication log (5.8), and the system escalates automatically — soft warnings at 80% utilisation, auto-hold at 110%, credit-sale block at 90+ days overdue.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Counter Sales | Sees credit check result at POS; cannot override |
| Supervisor | Override PIN for soft failures (over soft limit, minor ageing breach) |
| Branch Manager | Override hard failures; approve temporary limit increases ≤ 7 days |
| Credit Controller | Full: set/remove holds, adjust limits (with approval workflow), manage disputes, run ageing |
| Financial Manager | Approve permanent limit increases, blacklisting, and bad-debt write-offs |
| Accounts Clerk | View ageing, print dunning letters; no changes |

## Workflow

Main process: credit check at point of sale.

```
On-account sale started for trade customer
        │
┌───────┴────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Hold check:   on_hold = false?      │
│ 2. Balance check: balance + sale       │
│                   ≤ credit_limit?      │
│ 3. Ageing check:  no invoice overdue   │
│                   > 60 days?           │
└───────┬────────────────────────────────┘
   all pass│                fail
        │            ┌──────┴──────┐
   Sale proceeds     │ Reason shown │
                     │ at POS       │
                     └──────┬──────┘
              Supervisor/Manager override PIN?
                 yes │            │ no
        Sale proceeds,       Sale converted to
        override logged      cash/card or abandoned
```

1. **Hold check.** If `customers.on_hold = true`, the sale is blocked with the `hold_reason` displayed. Only Branch Manager override applies.
2. **Balance check.** Current AR balance plus the new sale total (VAT-inclusive) must not exceed `credit_limit`. Soft limits warn; hard limits block.
3. **Ageing check.** Any open invoice more than 60 days past due blocks new credit sales.
4. **Override.** A supervisor enters their PIN; the override is written to the audit log with the sale reference, reason code, and approver, and appears on the daily exceptions report.
5. **Post-sale.** The customer balance updates in real time so back-to-back sales at two counters cannot both slip under the limit.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Credit management dashboard | `customers.credit` | Book-wide ageing, holds, over-limit accounts, exceptions |
| Customer profile — Credit tab | `customers.show` | Single-account ageing, limit history, disputes |
| Aged debt report | via `customers.credit` | Printable/exportable ageing by customer, branch, salesperson |

**Primary screen — Credit management dashboard (`customers.credit`):**
- **Header:** total debtors' book, totals per ageing bucket (Current / 30 / 60 / 90+), count of accounts on hold, count over limit.
- **Main area:** sortable account grid — customer, limit, balance, utilisation %, oldest open invoice age, last payment date, account manager — with quick filters (90+ only, on hold, over limit, dormant).
- **Actions:** Place/Release Hold, Adjust Limit (opens approval flow), Log Collection Call (writes to 5.8), Print Dunning Letters, Export to Excel.

## Fields & Validation

Limit adjustment / hold form:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| New credit limit | decimal(15,2) | Yes | ≥ 0; increase requires approver per threshold; decrease below current balance allowed but flags account over-limit |
| Adjustment type | select | Yes | `permanent` or `temporary` |
| Expiry date | date | Temp only | ≤ 7 days from today for temporary increases |
| Reason | textarea | Yes | Min 10 chars; stored on audit trail |
| Supporting document | file | Increases > $5,000 | Financials, bank letter, or credit report |
| Hold reason | select + text | On hold | One of `over_limit`, `overdue`, `returned_payment`, `dispute`, `manual`; text required for `manual` |
| Override PIN | pin(4–6) | On override | Must belong to a user with override permission; 3 failed attempts locks override for that sale |

## Business Rules

1. Customers with any invoice 90+ days overdue cannot make credit sales without Branch Manager approval (expands parent rule: the block applies even if the balance is within limit).
2. Credit limit increases require approval — Branch Manager to a configured ceiling, Financial Manager above it — with reason and documentation stored.
3. **Auto-hold:** the system places the customer on hold when balance exceeds 110% of credit limit (overnight job plus real-time trigger at posting). Auto-holds release automatically when the balance drops back under 100%; manual holds require manual release.
4. A returned/bounced payment (receipt reversal) immediately places the account on hold with reason `returned_payment`.
5. Disputed invoices can be flagged by the Credit Controller; disputed amounts are shown separately on ageing and excluded from the 60-day sale block and penalty interest, but still count toward the balance for limit checks.
6. Overrides never change the limit — they authorise one sale only. Repeat overrides (3+ in 30 days for one account) appear on the Financial Manager's report as a limit-review trigger.
7. Blacklisting requires Financial Manager approval and a recorded reason; blacklist blocks all sale types (see 5.1) and removal requires the same authority.
8. Ageing is computed from invoice **due date**, not invoice date, so Net 60 customers are not penalised prematurely.
9. Bad-debt write-off moves the balance to the Allowance/Bad Debts expense via a Finance journal ([7.3](../07-finance-accounts/7.3-accounts-receivable.md)); the customer is simultaneously blacklisted unless explicitly excepted.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Customer exceeds credit limit at POS with a queue behind them:** the block screen offers three one-tap exits — supervisor override, convert to cash/card payment, or park the sale. No dead ends at the counter.
- **Two counters sell to the same account simultaneously:** balance updates are transactional; the second sale re-runs the check at commit time and fails cleanly if the first consumed the headroom.
- **Payment receipted but not yet allocated:** unallocated receipts reduce the balance used by the limit check (the customer has paid), but do **not** clear the 60-day ageing block until allocated to the overdue invoice — encourages same-day allocation (see [7.3](../07-finance-accounts/7.3-accounts-receivable.md)).
- **Limit decreased below current balance:** allowed (risk decision), but the account immediately shows over-limit; no new sales until paid down or overridden.
- **Temporary increase expires with balance above the permanent limit:** no forced hold — the account is simply over-limit for new sales; the expiry is reported to the Credit Controller.
- **EOM statement run while account on hold:** statements still generate and send; hold blocks sales, not correspondence.
- **Dispute resolved against the customer:** the flag is removed and the invoice re-enters ageing at its true age — which may instantly trip the 90+ block; the controller is warned before confirming.

## Key Tables

- `customers` — `credit_limit`, `balance`, `on_hold`, `hold_reason`, `status`, `blacklist_reason` (see [Module 5](../05-customer-management.md#51-customer-profiles)).
- Credit audit/override log tables — every override, limit change, hold, and dispute event.
- Ageing is a calculated view over open AR documents ([7.3](../07-finance-accounts/7.3-accounts-receivable.md)), not a stored table.
