# 5.6 Loyalty Programme

> Points and tier-based rewards for retail customers. Part of [Module 5: Customer Management](../05-customer-management.md).

## Purpose

Retail spares buying is fiercely price-compared — a car owner needing wiper blades will phone three shops. The loyalty programme gives the retail customer a reason to come back to *this* counter: points on every purchase, redeemable against future purchases, with tiers (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum) that reward the heavy DIY customer with earn multipliers and standing discounts. Trade customers are explicitly excluded — they already get trade pricing, and stacking points on top would double-discount the same margin.

Mechanically, the programme is an earn/burn points ledger per customer. Points are earned at a configurable rate (default 1 point per $1 spent on the VAT-inclusive total, adjustable per tier and product category), and redeemed at a fixed conversion (e.g. 500 points = $5 off) as a tender line at POS. Every movement — earn, redeem, expiry, manual adjustment, promotional bonus — is a row in `loyalty_transactions`, so the balance is always reconstructible and auditable, exactly like a mini sub-ledger.

Because redeemed points are a real cost, Finance treats the outstanding points balance as a provision; the programme's rules (expiry after 12 months, minimum redemption threshold, no earning on promotional discounts) exist to cap that liability and prevent gaming.

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|

| Branch Manager | Manual adjustments up to a configured cap (e.g. 1,000 points) with reason |

## Workflow

Main process: earn and redeem at POS.

```
Retail sale at POS, customer identified
        │
Enrolled? ──no──> Offer enrolment (name + mobile) ──> joined at Bronze
        │yes
Sale totalled
        │
Redemption requested? ──yes──> balance ≥ threshold?
        │no                      │yes: points tender line added,
        │                        │     total reduced
        ▼                        │no:  "needs 500 pts min" message
Payment completed
        │
Earn calculation: eligible lines × rate × tier multiplier
(promo-discounted lines excluded)
        │
`earn` transaction written ──> balance + tier re-evaluated
        │
Receipt prints points earned, new balance, expiry warning
```

1. **Enrolment.** A 10-second capture at the counter: name and mobile number (unique programme key). Email optional for balance statements.
2. **Earn.** On completion of a retail (non-account) sale, points accrue on eligible lines. Category rules can boost (oil promotions) or exclude (gift cards, fitment labour) specific categories.
3. **Redeem.** Points convert to a discount tender at the fixed rate, capped at the sale total; partial redemption allowed above the minimum threshold.
4. **Tier evaluation.** After every earn, lifetime-window points are compared to `loyalty_tiers.min_points`; upgrades apply immediately and print on the receipt.
5. **Expiry.** A nightly job expires points 12 months after their earn date (FIFO), writing `expire` transactions; customers with email get a 30-day expiry warning.

## Fields & Validation

Manual adjustment form (the main data-entry form beyond automatic earn/redeem):

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Customer | FK search | Yes | Enrolled retail customer; trade accounts rejected |
| Adjustment type | select | Yes | `adjust` (correction) or `bonus` (goodwill/campaign) |
| Points | int | Yes | Non-zero; negative allowed for corrections; abs value ≤ approver's cap |
| Reason | textarea | Yes | Min 10 chars; shown on customer's transaction history |
| Reference | text(100) | No | Original invoice/complaint reference |
| Approver PIN | pin | > cap | Financial Manager PIN for amounts above Branch Manager cap |

Tier configuration: `min_points` must be strictly increasing across tiers; `earn_multiplier` 0.5–5.0; `discount_pct` 0–15.

## Business Rules

1. Points are earned only on retail purchases — never on trade/credit-account sales (parent rule), and also never on: gift-card purchases, redemption-funded portions of a sale, or lines already carrying a promotional discount (double-dipping prevention).
2. Points expire 12 months after earning, consumed FIFO so redemptions always burn the oldest points first (minimising breakage disputes).
3. Minimum redemption threshold applies (default 500 points = $5); redemptions below it are blocked at POS with the shortfall shown.
4. Refunds/credit notes claw back the points earned on the returned lines (`adjust` transaction); if the balance has since been spent, the balance may go negative and future earns repay it first.
5. Redemption value reduces the payment due, not the sale price — VAT is still calculated on the full selling price, and the redemption posts as a marketing expense in Finance, not a revenue reduction.
6. Tier downgrades are evaluated only at the annual review date, not continuously — customers keep a tier for at least 12 months after reaching it.
7. Balances are non-transferable between customers except by a Manager-approved merge of genuine duplicate profiles (5.1).
8. Every balance is derivable as `SUM(points)` over `loyalty_transactions`; the cached `customers.loyalty_points` is reconciled nightly and discrepancies alarmed.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Customer returns a purchase that triggered a tier upgrade:** the claw-back may drop lifetime points below the tier threshold; the tier stays until annual review (rule 6), but the points balance reflects the reversal immediately.
- **Redemption attempted on an account (trade) sale:** blocked with "loyalty applies to retail purchases only" — even if the person standing there is enrolled personally.
- **Offline POS during earn:** the sale queues its earn transaction; on reconnection points post with the original sale timestamp so expiry dates stay honest.
- **Customer claims points missing from an unlinked cash sale:** with the till slip, a Manager can post a backdated `adjust` referencing the invoice number; without a slip, no adjustment.
- **Negative balance from claw-back never recovers (customer gone):** negative balances older than 12 months are written off to zero by the nightly job with an `adjust` transaction.
- **Two profiles, points split between them:** profile merge (5.1) re-parents both transaction sets; the merged balance is the sum, and tier re-evaluates on merged lifetime points.
- **Bonus campaign misconfigured to 10× instead of 1.5×:** campaigns have a per-transaction earn cap and a total-budget cap; breaching the budget cap pauses the campaign and alerts Marketing rather than continuing to mint points.

## Key Tables

- `loyalty_tiers` — tier definitions: thresholds, multipliers, discounts (schema in [Module 5](../05-customer-management.md#56-loyalty-programme)).
- `loyalty_transactions` — the points ledger: earn / redeem / expire / adjust / bonus rows.
- `customers` — cached `loyalty_points` and `loyalty_tier_id` (see 5.1).
