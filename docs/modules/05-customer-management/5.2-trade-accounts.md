# 5.2 Trade Accounts

> Credit accounts for garages, mechanics, panel beaters, and fleets — buy now, pay on terms. Part of [Module 5: Customer Management](../05-customer-management.md).

## Purpose

Trade accounts are the commercial heart of a spares business. A garage fitting brake pads for its own customer cannot stop work to pay cash at the counter for every part; it opens an account, collects parts all month against invoices, and settles on statement. In return the business earns loyalty from workshops who route all their parts buying through one counter, and can price at trade rates that reflect volume.

This sub-module manages the lifecycle of the account itself: the credit application, the payment terms assigned, the account manager relationship, and the settings that drive statements (5.5) and credit checks (5.3). It sits between the customer profile (5.1), which holds identity, and credit management (5.3), which enforces limits day to day.

Terms matter commercially: COD accounts get trade pricing but no credit exposure; Net 30 is standard for established garages; End of Month + 30 suits fleets that batch-process supplier invoices; early-settlement discounts (e.g. 2.5% if paid within 7 days) improve cash flow, while late-payment penalties (e.g. 2%/month) discourage using the spares shop as a bank.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Sales Rep | Initiate credit application for a customer; view account status |
| Counter Sales | View terms and available credit at POS; cannot change anything |
| Credit Controller | Approve/decline applications, set terms and limits, suspend accounts |
| Branch Manager | Approve applications up to a configured limit (e.g. $5,000); assign account manager |
| Financial Manager | Approve any limit; approve early-settlement discount terms |
| Accounts Clerk | Maintain statement delivery contacts; view ageing |

## Workflow

Main process: opening a trade account.

```
Customer profile exists (5.1)
        │
Credit application captured
(trade references, bank details, requested limit, signed T&Cs)
        │
Credit Controller review ──> Decline ──> Customer stays COD/cash
        │approve
Set: credit limit, payment terms, statement contact, account manager
        │
Account activated ──> credit_limit > 0, payment_terms assigned
        │
First on-account sale runs credit check (5.3)
```

1. **Application.** Sales Rep captures the application against an existing `business`/`fleet`/`dealer` profile: requested limit, two trade references, banking details, and an uploaded signed credit agreement (T&Cs include the penalty and interest clauses).
2. **Vetting.** Credit Controller checks references and any credit bureau report, then records the decision with reasons.
3. **Terms assignment.** On approval, the controller sets `credit_limit`, selects a `payment_terms` record (COD, Net 30, Net 60, EOM+30), and flags whether the limit is hard (blocks sale) or soft (warns, allows override).
4. **Account manager.** A salesperson is assigned as account manager; they own the relationship, follow-ups, and quarterly reviews.
5. **Activation.** The customer can now transact on account; every invoice inherits the terms for due-date calculation, and the customer joins the monthly statement run (5.5).

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Credit management dashboard | `customers.credit` | All trade accounts: limits, balances, ageing, holds |
| Customer profile — Credit tab | `customers.show` | Single account view: terms, limit, utilisation, application docs |
| Account statement | `customers.statement` | Statement view/print for the account (see 5.5) |

**Primary screen — Customer profile, Credit tab:**
- **Header:** credit limit, current balance, available credit (limit − balance), utilisation bar (amber ≥ 80%, red ≥ 100%), terms name, on-hold indicator.
- **Main area:** ageing mini-table (Current / 30 / 60 / 90+), application history with decisions and documents, terms history (who changed what, when), account manager.
- **Actions:** Request Limit Change, Change Terms (Credit Controller), Suspend Account, Generate Statement.

## Fields & Validation

Credit application / account settings form:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Requested credit limit | decimal(15,2) | Yes | > 0; approval routing: ≤ manager threshold → Branch Manager, else Financial Manager |
| Payment terms | FK select | Yes | Active `payment_terms` record; `prepaid`/`cod` types skip credit vetting |
| Limit type | select | Yes | `hard` or `soft` |
| Trade reference 1 & 2 | text(255) | Yes | Business name + phone; both required for limits above $1,000 |
| Bank name / account | text | Yes | For debit-order or verification purposes |
| Signed agreement | file upload | Yes | PDF/JPEG, max 10 MB; date-stamped |
| Statement contact | FK select | Yes | Must be a `customer_contacts` row with valid email if delivery = email |
| Account manager | FK select | Yes | Active user with sales role |
| Early payment discount % | decimal(5,2) | No | 0–10; Financial Manager approval required if > 0 |
| Late payment penalty % | decimal(5,2) | No | 0–5 monthly; must match signed agreement clause |

## Business Rules

1. A customer must be type `business`, `fleet`, or `dealer` to hold a trade account; `individual` customers stay cash/card (loyalty instead — see 5.6).
2. `credit_limit = 0` means COD-only: the customer gets trade pricing from their group but no on-account sales.
3. Due date = invoice date + `payment_terms.days` for `net_days`; for `end_of_month` type, due date = last day of invoice month + `days`.
4. Terms changes take effect on **new** invoices only; existing open invoices keep their original due dates.
5. Early-settlement discount is applied at receipt time as a credit note, only if the full invoice is paid within the discount window — partial payments do not qualify.
6. Late-payment penalty is raised as a monthly interest debit journal on balances 30+ days overdue, but only after the Credit Controller confirms the penalty run (no silent charges).
7. Every limit or terms change is written to an audit trail: old value, new value, user, timestamp, reason.
8. Accounts with no movement for 12 months are flagged dormant; dormant accounts revert to COD until reviewed.
9. One customer = one account; franchises wanting split billing per branch open separate customer profiles linked by a shared group.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Garage hits its limit mid-month with a vehicle on the hoist:** hard limit blocks the sale at POS; the workflow allows a one-off supervisor override (5.3) or an emergency temporary limit increase valid for 7 days, logged and reported to the Financial Manager.
- **Customer pays early expecting discount but short-pays:** system computes discount only on full settlement; the receipt allocation screen shows "discount not earned — short paid by $X" instead of silently writing off the difference.
- **Terms changed while a statement run is in progress:** the statement run snapshots terms at run start; the change applies from the next cycle.
- **Application approved but agreement upload missing:** activation is blocked — the account stays `pending` and appears on the Credit Controller's exceptions list.
- **Dealer account requests limit above insurer cover:** if credit insurance is configured, limits above the insured amount require Financial Manager sign-off with an explicit "uninsured exposure" acknowledgement.
- **Account manager deactivated:** accounts are auto-flagged for reassignment; statements still send (they are not blocked by an orphaned account manager).
- **Penalty raised on a disputed invoice:** invoices marked "in dispute" (5.3) are excluded from the penalty calculation until the dispute is resolved.
