# 5.4 Customer Vehicles

> Registered vehicles per customer, powering fitment lookup and service history. Part of [Module 5: Customer Management](../05-customer-management.md).

## Purpose

In a motor spares business, the vehicle is the real unit of work — parts are bought *for a vehicle*, not in the abstract. Registering a customer's vehicles (make, model, year, engine code, VIN, registration plate) lets counter staff answer the only question that matters: "will this part fit?" When a customer profile is pulled up at POS, selecting one of their vehicles filters the catalogue to compatible parts via the fitment data in Inventory, eliminating the most expensive error in the trade: selling the wrong part, taking it back used, and losing the resale.

For workshop customers the vehicle record is also the anchor for job history: every workshop job, part fitted, and service performed links to a specific vehicle, building a service file that supports warranty claims ("we fitted that water pump 4 months ago, job JOB-0871") and repeat business ("your Hilux is due for a cam belt at 90,000 km").

Fleets multiply the value: a transport company with 40 trucks registers each one, and orders reference the fleet number, so invoicing and history stay per-vehicle even on one consolidated account.

| Role | Access |

## Workflow

Main process: vehicle-driven part sale at the counter.

```
Customer identified at POS
        │
Select vehicle from profile ──none──> Quick-add vehicle
        │                             (plate → lookup, or
        │                              make/model/year/engine)
        ▼
Catalogue filtered by fitment (Inventory module)
        │
Compatible parts shown; incompatible greyed with warning
        │
Sale lines added ──> vehicle_id stamped on each line
        │
Sale completes ──> vehicle service/purchase history updated
```

1. **Identify the vehicle.** Staff pick from the customer's registered vehicles or quick-add a new one. Registration-plate entry can pre-fill make/model where a lookup source exists; otherwise selection is by make → model → year → engine variant.
2. **Fitment filter.** The parts search is scoped to the selected vehicle using fitment data owned by Inventory; staff can toggle the filter off, but off-fitment sales carry a confirmation.
3. **Stamp the sale.** Each sale line records the vehicle, feeding purchase history per vehicle.
4. **History.** The vehicle detail view accumulates parts purchased, workshop jobs, and odometer readings over time.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Customer profile — Vehicles tab | `customers.show` | List of the customer's vehicles with quick actions |
| Vehicle detail | within `customers.show` | Full spec, service and purchase history, documents |
| POS vehicle picker | within sales flow | Fast select/quick-add during a sale |

**Primary screen — Vehicles tab on customer profile:**
- **Header:** customer name; count of registered vehicles; "Add Vehicle" button.
- **Main area:** card per vehicle — plate, make/model/year, engine code, VIN (masked to last 6), last odometer, last visit date; expanding a card shows purchase history and workshop jobs for that vehicle.
- **Actions:** Add Vehicle, Edit, Transfer to Another Customer (Manager), Deactivate (sold/written off), Start Sale for This Vehicle.

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| Registration plate | text(15) | Yes | Uppercased, trimmed; unique per **active** vehicle system-wide (duplicate prompts transfer flow) |
| Make | select/text | Yes | From vehicle make reference list |
| Model | select/text | Yes | Filtered by make |
| Year | int | Yes | 1950 – current year + 1 |
| Engine code / variant | select/text | No | Strongly recommended — drives fitment accuracy; warning banner if blank |
| VIN | text(17) | No | Exactly 17 chars when provided; excludes I, O, Q; check-digit validated where possible |
| Fuel type | select | No | `petrol`, `diesel`, `hybrid`, `electric` |
| Colour | text(30) | No | |
| Fleet number | text(20) | Fleet only | Unique within the owning customer |
| Odometer | int | No | Must be ≥ last recorded reading (override with reason for clock replacement) |
| Notes | textarea | No | e.g. "aftermarket suspension", "runs LPG conversion" |

## Business Rules

1. A vehicle belongs to exactly one active customer at a time; ownership changes use the **transfer** action, which preserves the vehicle's full history under the new owner while flagging the ownership change date.
2. Plate uniqueness applies to active vehicles only — a re-registered plate on a deactivated vehicle does not block a new record, but the UI surfaces the old record for possible transfer instead.
3. Fitment filtering is advisory at POS by default: off-fitment sales are allowed with confirmation, and the confirmation is stored on the sale line (protects the business in "you sold me the wrong part" disputes).
4. Odometer readings are append-only history, captured at every workshop job and optionally at counter sales; a lower-than-last reading requires a reason (`clock_replaced`, `data_error`).
5. Deactivating a vehicle (sold, scrapped, written off) hides it from pickers but keeps all history intact and reportable.
6. Vehicles cannot be hard-deleted once referenced by any sale line or workshop job.
7. Fleet customers may bulk-import vehicles from CSV (plate, make, model, year, engine, fleet number); import validates row-by-row and reports rejects rather than failing the batch.
8. The `customer_vehicles` table is **owned by the Workshop module** ([Module 4](../04-workshop-management.md)); this sub-module is the CRM-facing surface over it — schema changes are coordinated there.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Customer sells their car to another existing customer:** the transfer action re-parents the vehicle; both customers' histories show the vehicle for their ownership window only.
- **Same plate entered for a second customer:** save is blocked; the UI shows the current owner (name partially masked for privacy for counter staff) and offers "request transfer", which a manager completes.
- **Part sold off-fitment comes back:** the return flow shows the original off-fitment confirmation, supporting a restocking-fee or refusal decision per returns policy.
- **VIN entered with I/O/Q or 16 chars:** field-level error before save; staff can save without VIN rather than saving a bad one.
- **Two vehicle records for the same physical car (created at different branches):** Manager merge combines history onto the surviving record and deactivates the duplicate with a cross-reference note.
- **Engine variant unknown at the counter:** sale proceeds with fitment filter at model level; the vehicle is flagged `fitment_incomplete` for later enrichment, and safety-critical categories (brakes, steering) force an explicit "verify fitment manually" confirmation.
- **Fleet CSV import with 3 bad rows out of 40:** 37 import, 3 rejects listed with row number and reason; nothing partial or silent.
