# 1.5 Reorder Management

> Min/max levels, reorder points, ABC analysis, and automatic purchase requisitions. Part of [Module 1: Inventory Management](../01-inventory-management.md).

## Purpose

A spares counter lives or dies on availability of fast movers. If the shop is out of oil filters for a Toyota Hilux or NGK `BKR6E` plugs, the customer drives to the competitor and may not come back. Reorder management watches available stock per part per branch and raises the flag the moment `qty_on_hand - qty_reserved` drops to the reorder point, before the shelf is actually empty.

The sub-module does more than alert. It generates draft purchase requisitions sized to the configured reorder quantity, nets off stock already on open purchase orders so the buyer is not double-ordering, and gives the buyer decision support: ABC classification (A = fast movers counted and reordered aggressively, C = slow movers ordered conservatively), an EOQ helper to balance order cost against holding cost, and a safety-stock calculator driven by average daily usage and supplier lead time.

The business outcome is a weekly buying routine instead of firefighting: the buyer opens the reorder report, reviews suggested quantities against supplier minimums and promotions, and converts approved lines to purchase orders in the Purchasing module.

## Workflow

```
Stock movement posts (sale / adjustment / transfer)
        │
        ▼
Service event checks: available ≤ reorder_point ?
        │ yes
        ▼
Part appears on Reorder Report
        │
        ▼
Nightly job creates/updates DRAFT purchase requisition per supplier
  (qty = reorder_qty, netted against open PO qty)
        │
        ▼
Buyer reviews report → adjusts qtys → approves lines
        │
        ▼
Convert to Purchase Order (Purchasing module) → qty_on_order increases
        │
        ▼
Part drops off report (covered qty ≥ reorder point)
```

1. Every posted movement re-evaluates the part's availability against its reorder point for that branch.
2. Parts at or below reorder point appear on the reorder report with suggested quantity, preferred supplier, last cost, and 12-month sales velocity.
3. A nightly job groups suggestions into draft requisitions by preferred supplier; existing drafts are updated, not duplicated.
4. The buyer reviews, adjusts quantities (e.g. rounding to supplier pack size of 12 for oil filters), removes lines, and approves.
5. Approved requisitions convert to POs; `qty_on_order` rises, which removes the part from the alert list for that quantity.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Reorder report | `inventory.reorder` | All parts below reorder level with actions |
| Part reorder settings | `inventory.parts.show` (stock tab) | Per-branch reorder point/qty/max editing |
| ABC analysis | `inventory.reorder.abc` | Velocity classification review |

**Primary screen — Reorder report (`Reorder/Index.jsx`):** Header has branch selector, supplier filter, ABC class filter, and bulk actions (approve selected, export). Main area is a table: part, description, ABC class, available, reorder point, suggested qty, on open POs, preferred supplier, last cost, 90-day usage sparkline. Rows where a draft requisition already covers the shortfall show a "covered" badge. Row actions: edit suggested qty, snooze alert (with reason and until-date), view usage history. Footer totals the suggested spend per supplier.

## Fields & Validation

Main form: per-part, per-branch reorder settings (stored on `stock_levels`).

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| reorder_point | decimal(10,2) | Yes* | ≥ 0; must be < max_level when max set (*required for stocked, non-special-order parts) |
| reorder_qty | decimal(10,2) | Yes* | > 0; warning if not a multiple of supplier pack size |
| max_level | decimal(10,2) | No | > reorder_point when present |
| safety_stock_days | int | No | 0–90; feeds the safety stock calculator |
| preferred_supplier_id | FK | No | Active supplier with this part on their price file |
| snooze_until | date | No | Requires a reason note; max 60 days ahead |

## Business Rules

1. Alert condition is `qty_on_hand - qty_reserved ≤ reorder_point`; `qty_on_order` and inbound `qty_in_transit` count toward coverage, not toward the trigger.
2. Auto-generated requisitions are always `draft` — a buyer must review and convert; nothing is ordered automatically.
3. Quantity already on open POs (and inbound transfers) is netted off the suggested quantity; if coverage meets or exceeds the shortfall, the part shows as "covered" instead of raising a new suggestion.
4. Parts flagged `is_special_order` or `is_discontinued` never appear on the reorder report.
5. ABC classification recalculates monthly from 12-month rolling sales: A = top 70% of sales value, B = next 20%, C = last 10% (thresholds configurable). Class changes are logged.
6. The safety-stock helper suggests `avg_daily_usage × (lead_time_days + safety_stock_days)` as the reorder point but never overwrites values without clerk confirmation.
7. Snoozed alerts resurface automatically at `snooze_until`, or immediately if available stock reaches zero.
8. Reorder settings are per branch; a bulk-apply tool can copy one branch's settings to another but requires manager approval.
9. New parts with fewer than 90 days of sales history are marked "insufficient history" — the EOQ/safety-stock helpers refuse to suggest values for them.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Seasonal spikes** (battery sales jump in winter): the usage sparkline shows the trend; the calculator uses a 90-day window by default, but the clerk can pin a manual reorder point that the helper will not touch.
- **Supplier pack size mismatch**: suggested qty 5 for filters sold in boxes of 12 shows a rounding hint; buyer decision, not auto-rounding.
- **Part on report but PO overdue** (ordered 3 weeks ago, lead time 7 days): row shows an "overdue PO" badge linking to the PO rather than suggesting a second order.
- **Reorder point set above max level** on import: validation rejects the row and lists it on the import exceptions report.
- **A-class part hits zero available**: escalated notification to the buyer and branch manager immediately, independent of the nightly cycle.
- **Preferred supplier deactivated**: requisition generation falls back to "no supplier" grouping and flags the lines for buyer assignment.
- **Same part below reorder at two branches**: two branch-level suggestions are raised; the buyer may consolidate into one PO with split delivery, or trigger an inter-branch transfer instead if one branch is overstocked.
- **Dead stock overlap**: a part below reorder point but with zero sales in 180+ days is flagged "review before reorder" instead of suggesting a buy.
