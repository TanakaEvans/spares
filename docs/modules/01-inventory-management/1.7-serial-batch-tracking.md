# 1.7 Serial & Batch Tracking

> Individual serial-number tracking for high-value units and batch/lot tracking for dated stock. Part of [Module 1: Inventory Management](../01-inventory-management.md).

## Purpose

For most parts, one oil filter is interchangeable with the next — quantity tracking is enough. But high-value and warranty-sensitive items are not interchangeable: a reconditioned 1GD-FTV engine, a gearbox, an alternator, or a battery each carries a serial number, its own warranty clock, and its own history. Serial tracking records each unit individually from goods receipt through sale to the specific customer, so a warranty claim eighteen months later can be answered with facts: this exact serial, received on this GRN, sold on this invoice, warranty expires on this date.

Batch tracking solves a different problem for consumables with shelf lives — engine oils, brake fluid, coolant, adhesives. Stock is received in lots with an expiry date; the system tracks how much of each batch remains, drives FEFO (first-expired, first-out) picking, and warns before selling expired brake fluid — a genuine safety issue, not just housekeeping.

Both mechanisms ride on top of normal stock control: quantities still flow through the stock ledger, while serial/batch records add the identity layer. Whether a part is tracked is set by flags on the part record (`has_serial_tracking`, `has_batch_tracking`).

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Storeman | Capture serials/batches at GRN; scan serials at dispatch |
| Cashier | Select/scan serial at POS sale; view batch expiry warnings |
| Inventory Clerk | Correct serial records (pre-sale), manage batch data |
| Inventory Manager | Scrap serials, write off expired batches, edit warranty dates |
| System Admin | Merge duplicate serials, reassign mis-captured records |

## Workflow

```
GRN receipt of tracked part
        │
        ├─ serial part → scan/enter one serial per unit (status: in_stock)
        └─ batch part  → enter batch no. + expiry + qty received
        │
        ▼
In stock: serials listed per branch; batches show qty_remaining + expiry
        │
        ▼
Sale at POS / dispatch on order
        │
        ├─ serial part → cashier scans the specific unit sold
        │       └─ serial → status: sold, linked to customer + invoice,
        │          warranty_expiry set from part warranty months
        └─ batch part  → system suggests earliest-expiry batch (FEFO)
        │
        ▼
Aftermarket events:
   return → serial status: returned → inspect → back to stock or scrapped
   warranty claim → lookup by serial → full unit history
   batch expiry reached → block sale, write-off workflow
```

1. At GRN, receiving a serial-tracked part forces one serial entry per unit received — a receipt of qty 3 needs 3 serials before posting.
2. Batch-tracked receipts capture batch number, expiry date, and quantity; the same part can hold several open batches.
3. At sale, the cashier scans or picks the exact serial handed over; for batches, the FEFO suggestion pre-selects the earliest-expiring batch with sufficient quantity.
4. The sold serial is stamped with customer, invoice line, and computed warranty expiry.
5. Returns re-open the serial (`returned`); after inspection it is restocked (`in_stock`) or scrapped (`scrapped`) — never silently resold without an inspection step.

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|

| Serial detail | `inventory.serials.show` | Full unit history: GRN → moves → sale → warranty |

**Primary screen — Serial register (`Serials/Index.jsx`):** Header has a scan-first search box (full or partial serial), part filter, branch filter, status filter (`in_stock`, `sold`, `on_order`, `returned`, `scrapped`). Main area is a table: serial, part number, description, branch, status pill, received date/GRN, sold date/invoice, customer, warranty expiry (red when lapsed). Row click opens the serial timeline. Bulk action: print serial labels.

## Fields & Validation

Serial capture form (at GRN or correction):

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| serial_number | string(100) | Yes | Unique per part; trimmed, uppercased; min 3 chars |
| part_id | FK | Yes | Part must have `has_serial_tracking` |
| branch_id | FK | Auto | From the receiving GRN/transfer |
| status | enum | Auto | Lifecycle-managed, not free-set |
| warranty_expiry | date | Auto/edit | Computed sale date + part warranty months; manager may override |

Batch capture form:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| batch_number | string(100) | Yes | Unique per part + received_date |
| expiry_date | date | Cond. | Required for parts flagged expiry-managed (oils, fluids); must be future at receipt |
| qty_received | decimal(10,2) | Yes | > 0; must equal the GRN line quantity |
| qty_remaining | decimal(10,2) | Auto | Decremented by sales/issues; never below 0 |

## Business Rules

1. A GRN line for a serial-tracked part cannot post until the number of captured serials equals the received quantity.
2. Serial numbers are unique per part (two different parts may legitimately share a serial string; the same part may not).
3. Selling a serial-tracked part requires selecting an `in_stock` serial at that branch; the invoice cannot post without it.
4. Serial status transitions are constrained: `in_stock → sold`, `sold → returned`, `returned → in_stock | scrapped`, `in_stock → scrapped`. No transition skips inspection on return.
5. Branch transfers of serial-tracked parts move the named serials; the receiving branch confirms each serial on receipt.
6. Batch issues follow FEFO by default; overriding to a later-expiring batch requires a reason.
7. Batches past expiry are blocked from sale; remaining quantity enters the write-off workflow (`expiry` adjustment reason, [1.2](1.2-stock-control.md)).
8. Expiry warnings surface at 90/30/7 days before expiry on the batch register and the reorder report.
9. Turning on tracking for a part with existing untracked stock requires a stock take on that part to capture identities ([1.1 rule 7](1.1-parts-catalogue.md)).
10. Warranty expiry edits are manager-only and audit-logged with before/after values.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Serial label unreadable at sale** (oil-stained engine tag): cashier searches by partial serial; if truly unreadable, manager reassigns the sale to the correct record after physical verification against the GRN list.
- **Duplicate serial scanned at GRN** (supplier reused a number or double-scan): second scan is rejected with a link to the existing record; genuine supplier duplicates get a branch-suffix appended with a note.
- **Customer returns a serial that was never sold to them** (bought elsewhere): lookup shows the true purchaser or "not our unit"; return refused at counter, escalation path to manager.
- **Sale attempted at branch B for a serial sitting at branch A**: blocked; screen offers an inter-branch transfer instead.
- **Batch part sold when only an expired batch remains**: sale blocked; manager may transfer stock from another branch or write off and lose the sale — no expired sale override.
- **Partial batch damaged** (dropped 5L oil drums): write-off adjustment reduces `qty_remaining` on that specific batch, keeping other batches intact.
- **Stock take counts more serials than the register shows**: unexpected serials are flagged individually; posting creates the serial records with an `ADJUSTMENT_IN` link rather than absorbing them anonymously.
- **Warranty lookup on a scrapped serial**: full history remains visible; scrapped units are never purged.
