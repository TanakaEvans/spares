# 3.4 Supplier Invoices (AP)

> Captures supplier tax invoices, enforces 3-way matching against PO and GRN, and raises the accounts payable liability. Part of [Module 3: Purchasing & Procurement](../03-purchasing-procurement.md).

## Purpose

When the Bosch distributor's invoice arrives — usually by email a day or two after the truck — the AP clerk must answer one question before the business owes anything: does the invoice agree with what we ordered and what we actually received? This sub-module encodes that discipline as 3-way matching: PO (agreed price and quantity) ↔ GRN (confirmed quantity) ↔ supplier invoice (billed amount), all within a configurable tolerance before the invoice can be approved.

Passing the match posts the invoice to the AP ledger: the supplier's balance increases, the GRN's accrual ("goods received not invoiced") clears, and the due date is computed from the supplier's payment terms (e.g. 30 days from statement). Failing the match parks the invoice as `disputed` with a written reason — a price higher than the PO, a quantity we never received, freight we didn't agree to — and escalates it so suppliers are queried while the delivery is still fresh.

For import suppliers, the invoice is captured in foreign currency at the invoice-date rate; the difference against the PO's locked rate posts as a foreign-exchange variance rather than distorting part costs.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| AP Clerk | Capture invoices, run matching, mark disputes, post within tolerance |
| Finance Manager | Approve out-of-tolerance invoices, resolve disputes, unpost (reversal journal) |
| Buyer | Read-only; consulted on price disputes against own POs |
| Procurement Manager | Read-only; dispute escalation recipient |

## Workflow

```
Invoice received (email/paper)
        │
        ▼
Capture header + lines against GRN/PO
        │
        ▼
3-way match: PO qty/price ↔ GRN qty ↔ Invoice qty/price
        │
   ┌────┴─────────────────┐
   ▼                      ▼
within tolerance     variance found
   │                      │
   ▼                      ▼
status: matched      status: disputed (reason mandatory)
   │                      │
   ▼                      ├── supplier credit note ──► re-match
Approve ──► Post          └── Finance Manager override ──► Approve ──► Post
   │
   ▼
AP ledger entry · GRN accrual cleared · due date scheduled
```

1. **Capture** — Clerk selects the supplier and GRN(s); invoice lines pre-populate from GRN accepted quantities at PO cost. The supplier's invoice number, date, and totals are keyed from the document.
2. **Match** — The engine compares, per line: invoice qty ≤ GRN accepted qty; invoice price vs PO price within tolerance; header total vs computed total.
3. **Resolve** — Variances either get a Finance Manager override (with reason), a supplier credit note (3.5), or the invoice stays disputed.
4. **Approve & post** — Posting writes the AP ledger entry, sets `payment_status = unpaid`, and stamps `posted_at`. The PO moves toward `invoiced`.
5. **Pay** — Payment happens in the Finance module; `payment_status` updates to `partial`/`paid` from allocations.

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| supplier_id | select | Yes | Must match the GRN's supplier |
| supplier_ref | text | Yes | Unique per supplier (duplicate-invoice block) |
| grn_id / po_id | lookup | Yes | Posted GRNs of this supplier not yet fully invoiced |
| invoice_date | date | Yes | ≥ GRN received date; not future-dated |
| due_date | date | Auto | Computed from supplier payment terms; editable by Finance Manager only |
| currency_id / exchange_rate | select / decimal | Yes | Defaults to PO currency; rate at invoice date |
| subtotal_excl / vat_amount / total_incl | decimal | Yes | total = subtotal + VAT (±0.02 rounding); VAT % must match VAT codes used |
| line: invoiced qty | decimal | Yes | ≤ GRN accepted qty not yet invoiced |
| line: invoiced price | decimal | Yes | Variance vs PO price computed against tolerance |
| dispute_reason | textarea | Conditional | Mandatory when status set to `disputed` |

## Business Rules

1. Total invoice value may not exceed GRN value by more than tolerance — the larger of 2% or a fixed amount (both configurable per company); anything above requires Finance Manager override, logged with reason.
2. Invoiced quantity can never exceed GRN accepted quantity, tolerance or not; the excess must come as a separate invoice after a further GRN, or be rejected back to the supplier.
3. Duplicate `supplier_ref` for the same supplier is blocked at save — the classic double-payment defence.
4. Invoice date cannot precede the GRN date; a supplier pre-dating invoices to shorten terms is surfaced, and the due date is computed from invoice date per terms regardless.
5. Disputed invoices require a written reason and appear on the Finance Manager's dispute queue; they cannot be paid while disputed.
6. FX difference between PO rate and invoice rate posts to the FX variance account, not to stock value.
7. One GRN may be invoiced by multiple invoices (split billing) and one invoice may cover multiple GRNs, but every invoice line must trace to exactly one GRN line.
8. Posting is reversible only via a Finance Manager reversal journal that reinstates the GRN accrual — the original invoice is never edited after posting.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Supplier invoices freight on the goods invoice but freight was a landed cost**: the freight line has no GRN line to match; the clerk moves it to the landed-cost record of the GRN (3.3) or disputes it.
- **Price increase the buyer verbally agreed but never revised on the PO**: match fails on price; correct path is a PO revision (3.2) then re-match — override is discouraged and reported on the override audit listing.
- **Invoice arrives before the GRN is posted**: it can be captured as `draft` against the PO but cannot match or post until a posted GRN exists; a "waiting for GRN" queue shows these.
- **Supplier invoices the rejected quantity**: match fails (invoiced > accepted); resolution is a supplier credit note (3.5) linked to this invoice, after which the *net* is matched.
- **Rounding differences on VAT** (supplier rounds per line, we round per total): differences within ±0.02 per document auto-accept as a rounding adjustment line.
- **Credit-hold supplier**: posting still allowed (liability is real) but the payment module blocks allocation until the hold clears.
- **Same delivery, two invoice copies keyed by two clerks**: second save hits the unique `supplier_ref` block with a link to the first capture.
- **Partial GRN then invoice for full PO quantity**: only GRN quantity matches; the remainder waits in the invoice's unmatched balance until the back-order GRN posts, or is disputed.
