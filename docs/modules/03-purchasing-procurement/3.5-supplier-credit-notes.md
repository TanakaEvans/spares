# 3.5 Supplier Credit Notes

> Captures credits issued by suppliers — for returns, price corrections, and rebates — and applies them against the AP balance. Part of [Module 3: Purchasing & Procurement](../03-purchasing-procurement.md).

## Purpose

Suppliers owe the business money more often than people expect. A carton of oil filters arrives crushed and goes back on the same truck; the NGK distributor billed the old price on plugs after a price decrease; the quarterly volume rebate lands because the workshop pushed 400 brake-pad sets through in three months. Each of these arrives as a supplier credit note, and until it is captured and applied, the AP balance overstates what the business owes and the month-end supplier statement reconciliation will not balance.

The credit note sub-module records the supplier's credit document, links it to its cause — an original invoice, a supplier return (3.6), or a warranty claim (Module 4.7) — and posts it as a negative entry in the AP ledger. From there, the payment run offsets credits against open invoices automatically, so the business never pays gross when it should pay net.

Credits are also evidence in supplier performance scoring: a supplier with a high credits-to-invoices ratio is either shipping damaged goods or billing carelessly, and the buyer sees this in the price-comparison and supplier review screens.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| AP Clerk | Capture credit notes, link to invoice/return/claim, post within tolerance |
| Finance Manager | Approve unlinked ("goodwill"/rebate) credits, reverse posted credits |
| Buyer | Read-only; notified when a requested credit is received |
| Procurement Manager | Read-only; credits ratio in supplier reviews |

## Workflow

```
Supplier issues credit note
        │
        ▼
Capture header + amounts
        │
        ▼
Link cause:
  ├── original supplier invoice (price correction / over-billing)
  ├── supplier return (3.6 — goods sent back)
  ├── warranty claim (4.7 — claim approved)
  └── none (rebate / goodwill — Finance Manager approval required)
        │
        ▼
Validate amounts vs linked document
        │
        ▼
POST ──► AP ledger credit ──► available for allocation in payment run
        │
        ▼
Linked return → status credit_received · claim → status credited
```

1. **Capture** — Clerk enters the supplier's credit number, date, reason, and amounts (subtotal, VAT, total) from the document.
2. **Link** — The credit is tied to its cause. Return- and claim-linked credits validate against the expected credit value; invoice-linked credits validate against the original invoice's remaining value.
3. **Approve** — Linked credits within value post directly; unlinked credits (rebates, goodwill gestures) always require Finance Manager approval.
4. **Post** — The AP ledger receives a credit entry; the supplier's balance drops; the source document (return/claim) is stamped with the credit.
5. **Allocate** — In the payment run, credits offset open invoices oldest-first unless manually allocated.

**Primary screen — Capture credit note.** Header: supplier, supplier's credit number, credit date, reason category (`return`, `price_correction`, `rebate`, `warranty`, `goodwill`) and free-text reason. Main area: cause-link panel — searching open returns awaiting credit, posted invoices, and approved warranty claims for the supplier; selecting one shows the expected credit value beside the keyed amount with a variance chip. ## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| supplier_id | select | Yes | Active supplier |
| credit_number | text | Yes | Supplier's reference; unique per supplier |
| credit_date | date | Yes | Not future-dated; ≥ linked document date |
| reason (category) | select | Yes | `return` / `price_correction` / `rebate` / `warranty` / `goodwill` |
| original_invoice_id | lookup | Conditional | Required for `price_correction`; must be a posted invoice of this supplier |
| linked return / claim | lookup | Conditional | Required for `return` / `warranty` categories |
| subtotal / vat_amount / total | decimal | Yes | total = subtotal + VAT (±0.02); total > 0 |
| total vs expected | — | — | Variance beyond 2% of linked document value requires Finance Manager approval |

## Business Rules

1. A credit note posts as a negative AP entry; it can never flip the supplier's overall balance treatment — an AP account in net credit is flagged for refund request rather than silently carried.
2. A `return`-category credit closes the loop on a supplier return: posting it sets the return's status to `credit_received` and stores `credit_note_id` on the return.
3. A `warranty` credit updates the linked claim to `credited` and records `credit_amount`; a partial credit leaves the claim `approved` with the shortfall visible.
4. A `price_correction` credit cannot exceed the un-credited remainder of the original invoice.
5. Unlinked credits (rebate/goodwill) always require Finance Manager approval, whatever the amount.
6. VAT on the credit must use the same VAT treatment as the original transaction (a zero-rated export invoice cannot be credited with standard-rate VAT).
7. Posted credits are immutable; corrections are made by a reversing debit adjustment, keeping the supplier statement audit trail intact.
8. Credits older than the configurable statement-cutoff still post, but are flagged for the reconciliation report of the period they arrive in.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Supplier credits less than the return value** (they dispute two of ten damaged filters): capture at the supplier's figure; the variance keeps the return in a "short-credited" list for the buyer to chase; a follow-up credit can link to the same return.
- **Credit received for goods never returned** (supplier error): capture as `goodwill` with Finance Manager approval, or hold — posting an unexplained credit invites a clawback invoice later; the approval dialog states this.
- **Duplicate credit number keyed twice**: unique per-supplier constraint blocks the second save with a link to the first.
- **Credit in a different currency than the original invoice** (rare, consolidator statements): captured in the credit's currency at credit-date rate; FX difference posts to FX variance, mirroring invoice handling in [3.4](3.4-supplier-invoices.md).
- **Rebate credit spanning multiple branches' purchases**: captured once at head office and apportioned to branch cost centres by purchase value — apportionment lines generated automatically, editable before post.
- **Original invoice already fully paid**: the credit still posts and sits as an unallocated credit for the next payment run, or Finance requests a refund.
- **Supplier sends a credit *and* a replacement shipment for the same damage**: the receiving clerk's replacement GRN must reference a zero-value replacement PO; the system warns if both a credit and a replacement GRN link to the same return.
