# 3.7 Import Management

> Tracks internationally sourced stock from proforma to cleared delivery, managing currency, customs, and landed costs. Part of [Module 3: Purchasing & Procurement](../03-purchasing-procurement.md).

## Purpose

A meaningful share of motor spares is imported: OEM filters and gaskets from a Dubai consolidator, aftermarket suspension kits from a Guangzhou factory, genuine Toyota parts routed through a South African distributor. An import is not a bigger PO — it is a months-long project with its own document chain (proforma, commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill, customs entry), its own costs (freight, marine insurance, customs duty, clearing agent fees), and its own risks (currency movement between order and payment, demurrage when clearance stalls).

This sub-module gives each import a shipment record that tracks status from `ordered` to `delivered`, stores the transport documents and references, and accumulates the landed costs that will be allocated across the received lines at GRN time. Because sea freight from China can be 8–12 weeks, the shipment's ETA feeds availability promises: a counter salesman quoting a customer on a back-ordered Hilux 2.8 GD-6 clutch kit can see it is on the water, ETA in 19 days.

Financially, the shipment is the container for true unit cost. A shock absorber invoiced at $11.40 FOB may land at $16.05 once freight, duty at the tariff rate, and clearing fees are allocated — and selling prices calculated off FOB instead of landed cost destroy margin invisibly.

## Workflow

```
Supplier proforma invoice
        │
        ▼
Foreign-currency PO (3.2) ──► deposit / LC payment (Finance)
        │
        ▼
Create Import Shipment ── link PO(s)
        │
   status: ordered
        ▼
Freight booking ──► goods ship ──► capture BL/AWB number
        │
   status: shipped ──► in_transit  (ETA maintained)
        ▼
Arrival ──► Customs entry lodged (entry number, duty assessed)
        │
   status: customs
        ▼
Cleared ──► delivery to branch
        │
   status: cleared ──► delivered
        ▼
GRN (3.3) with landed costs allocated ──► stock at landed cost
        │
        ▼
Supplier + freight + clearing invoices matched (3.4) ──► shipment closed
```

1. **Order** — A foreign-currency PO is raised from the proforma; deposit terms (e.g. 30% TT in advance) are handled in Finance.
2. **Create shipment** — The shipment record links one or more POs (a consolidator may combine POs in one container), sets transport mode and origin.
3. **Track** — Milestones update status; BL/AWB number, vessel/flight, and ETA are maintained; ETA changes notify buyers with affected special orders.
4. **Clear** — The customs entry number and assessed duty are captured; the clearing agent's fee estimate is recorded.
5. **Deliver & receive** — Goods arrive at the branch; the GRN receives against the linked POs with the shipment's accumulated costs allocated by value or weight.
6. **Close** — Once all cost invoices are captured and the GRN posted, the shipment closes with an actual-vs-estimated cost summary.

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| supplier_id | select | Yes | Supplier flagged as import/foreign |
| linked PO(s) | multi-select | Yes | Open foreign-currency POs of this supplier |
| origin_country | select | Yes | ISO 3166 code |
| transport_mode | select | Yes | `sea` / `air` / `road` / `rail` |
| eta | date | Yes | Future at creation; every change stored with reason |
| bl_number | text | Before `in_transit` | Max 100; label switches BL/AWB by mode |
| customs_entry_number | text | Before `cleared` | Max 100 |
| customs_duty | decimal | Before `cleared` | ≥ 0; warning if > 40% of goods value |
| freight_cost / insurance / clearing_agent_fee | decimal | No | ≥ 0; each with estimate → actual lifecycle |

## Business Rules

1. Status must advance sequentially through the milestone chain; skipping a step (goods arrive before docs updated) requires the clerk to backfill the skipped milestone dates.
2. A shipment's costs allocate to the GRN only when the GRN is created *from the shipment*; a normal GRN against an import PO is blocked with a "receive via shipment" message.
3. Duty is calculated per tariff line where tariff codes are maintained on parts; the entered assessed duty is reconciled against the calculation, with variances flagged for a tariff-code review.
4. FOB values remain at the PO's locked exchange rate; landed costs entered in local currency add on top — the final landed unit cost is base-currency.
5. Estimated costs may be used for GRN allocation when the actual invoice is outstanding; the later actual triggers a revaluation of remaining on-hand stock (see 3.3 late landed-cost rule).
6. An ETA slip of more than 7 days triggers notifications to owners of any special-order requisition lines in the shipment.
7. Closing requires: GRN posted, all cost lines marked actual, and all linked cost invoices matched — otherwise close is blocked with a checklist.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Container inspected and held at customs**: status stays `customs` with a hold flag and daily-cost note field (demurrage accrues as an `other` landed cost); the hold surfaces on the imports board in red.
- **Partial container release** (customs releases 80% of lines, holds the rest): the shipment supports split delivery — a first GRN for released lines with proportional cost allocation, remainder received on a second GRN when released.
- **Short-shipment discovered at receiving** (packing list says 40 cartons, 38 arrive): GRN records the shortfall; the marine insurance claim is logged against the shipment as a negative `insurance` cost line when settled.
- **Exchange control / payment delay blocks the supplier releasing docs**: shipment can sit at `shipped` with a "docs not released" flag; the GRN is blocked because receiving without a bill of lading is impossible.
- **Duty rate disputed by clearing agent** (tariff heading disagreement on sensor assemblies): assessed duty is captured as charged; a duty-refund tracker note keeps the dispute visible until resolved by amendment or refund credit.
- **One PO split across two shipments** (factory ships available stock air, back-order by sea): a PO may link to multiple shipments; each shipment receives only its own quantities and the PO tracks the combined `qty_received`.
- **Currency of freight differs from goods** (goods USD, freight EUR): each cost line carries its own currency and converts at its own invoice-date rate.
