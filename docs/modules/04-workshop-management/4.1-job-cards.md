# 4.1 Job Cards

> The central workshop document — one per vehicle visit — carrying fault, diagnosis, parts, labour, and status through to invoice. Part of [Module 4: Workshop Management](../04-workshop-management.md).

## Purpose

Every vehicle that rolls into the workshop gets exactly one job card per visit, and everything that happens to that vehicle hangs off it. When a customer brings in a Toyota Hilux 2.8 GD-6 complaining of a whining noise under acceleration, the job card records the reported fault in the customer's words, the pre-inspection condition (existing scratches, fuel level, loose items — the workshop's defence against "you damaged my car" claims), the technician's diagnosis (failing hub bearing, right front), the parts drawn from stores, the labour applied, and the customer's authorisation when the scope grows.

The job card is simultaneously an operational document (what should the technician do next, when did we promise the vehicle back), a legal document (customer authorisation for work and for additional cost), and a financial document (the accumulator that becomes the invoice). Its status machine — from `open` through `in_progress`, `awaiting_parts`, `quality_check`, `completed`, `invoiced`, `closed` — is what the workshop board (technician allocation view) and the promised-time alerting run on.

Nothing bills without a job card, and no part leaves the store for the workshop without one: that single rule is most of what separates a profitable workshop from one that leaks parts and hours.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Service Advisor | Open/edit job cards, capture fault & authorisations, set promised time, invoice |
| Workshop Foreman | Allocate technicians, change status, approve extra-work scope, quality check |
| Technician | View own jobs; write diagnosis/work performed; request parts; clock time |
| Storeman | Issue parts against a job card (see 4.4) |
| Workshop Manager | Cancel jobs with issued parts; override holds; all-branch visibility |

## Workflow

```
Walk-in / booking
     │
     ▼
Open job card (customer + vehicle mandatory) ── pre-inspection + odometer in
     │
     ▼
allocated ── Foreman assigns technician (board)
     │
     ▼
in_progress ── diagnose ──► write diagnosis
     │              │
     │              ├─ parts needed ──► awaiting_parts (4.4)
     │              └─ extra work found ──► awaiting_customer ── authorised? ──┐
     │                                                        └─ declined ─┐  │
     ▼                                                                     │  │
work performed ◄───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴──┘
     │
     ▼
quality_check (foreman) ── fail ──► back to in_progress
     │ pass
     ▼
completed ──► invoiced (4.8) ──► paid ──► closed
```

1. **Open** — Service advisor finds/creates the customer and vehicle (4.5), records reported fault, pre-inspection notes, odometer in, job type and priority, and a promised time.
2. **Allocate** — Foreman assigns the primary technician from the board, matching skill specialisation to job category.
3. **Diagnose & work** — Technician clocks on (4.2/4.3), writes the diagnosis, requests parts (4.4), performs work; labour lines accumulate.
4. **Authorise extras** — Any material scope increase halts work in `awaiting_customer` until the customer's approval is captured (name, time, channel).
5. **Quality check** — Foreman road-tests/inspects; failures return to `in_progress` with QC notes.
6. **Complete & invoice** — Odometer out captured; job converts to a tax invoice (4.8); payment closes the card and writes service history (4.5).

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Job cards list | `workshop.jobs.index` | Live board-style list by status, promised-time alarms |
| New job card | `workshop.jobs.create` | Customer/vehicle capture, fault, pre-inspection |
| Job card detail | `workshop.jobs.show` | Tabs: overview, labour, parts, authorisations, costing, documents |
| Technician board | `workshop.board` | Drag-and-drop allocation across technicians |

**Primary screen — Job card detail.** Header: job number, status chip, priority badge, customer + vehicle summary (registration, VIN, odometer in), promised time with countdown (amber at −60 min, red past due). Main area, tabbed: **Overview** (reported fault, diagnosis, work performed, pre-inspection), **Labour** (labour lines grid, clock status per technician), **Parts** (issued/requested lines with issue status), **Authorisations** (extra-work approvals trail), **Costing** (4.6 margin summary — foreman/manager only). Footer actions are status-driven: *Allocate*, *Start*, *Request authorisation*, *Send to QC*, *Complete*, *Invoice*, *Cancel*.

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| customer_id | lookup | Yes (past `open`) | Active customer; credit-hold customers warn at open, block at invoice |
| customer_vehicle_id | lookup | Yes (past `open`) | Vehicle of this customer (or transfer-ownership prompt) |
| job_type | select | Yes | `repair` / `service` / `diagnostic` / `warranty` / `quote_only` |
| priority | select | Yes | `normal` / `urgent` / `vip` |
| reported_fault | textarea | Yes | Min 10 chars — "noise" alone is not a fault description |
| pre_inspection_notes | textarea | Yes | Required before status `allocated` |
| odometer_in | int | Yes | ≥ last recorded odometer for the vehicle (override with reason: clock replaced) |
| promised_at | datetime | Yes | Future; within branch working hours |
| authorised_by / authorised_at | text / auto | Conditional | Both required to leave `awaiting_customer` |
| odometer_out | int | At completion | ≥ odometer_in |

## Business Rules

1. A job card must have customer and vehicle before leaving `open`; `quote_only` jobs may complete without parts issues but can never be invoiced (they convert to a quotation in Sales).
2. Adding parts reserves stock; the `JOB_CARD_OUT` ledger entry posts at issue, and returning unused parts credits the job (4.4).
3. Scope growth above the configured percentage of the original estimate forces `authorisation_required`; labour clocking and further parts issues are blocked until authorisation is captured.
4. Cancelling a job with issued parts is supervisor-only and requires all parts returned (or written off with reason) first.
5. Promised-time alert fires to technician and service advisor 30 minutes before `promised_at` when the job is not yet in `quality_check`.
6. `warranty` job types default all lines `is_warranty = true` (not customer-charged) and require a linked warranty claim (4.7) before completion.
7. Status transitions are constrained to the lifecycle graph; skips (e.g. `open` → `completed`) are blocked, and every transition is written to a status audit trail with user and timestamp.
8. Re-opening a `completed` job (QC missed something) is foreman-only and voids any draft invoice.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Customer declines the extra work**: the job resumes with original scope; declined items are recorded on the authorisations tab and printed on the invoice as "recommended — declined" (evidence for later disputes and a sales follow-up list).
- **Vehicle abandoned** (customer unreachable after completion): job parks in a `completed`-aged list; storage-fee lines may be added per branch policy after N days, with the notification log as evidence.
- **Same vehicle, second fault while in the workshop**: additions within the same visit go on the same card as authorised extras — a second open card for the same vehicle is blocked while one is active.
- **Walk-in with a vehicle registered to another customer** (car just bought): the advisor triggers vehicle-ownership transfer (4.5) rather than creating a duplicate vehicle.
- **Technician finds the reported fault is intermittent and unreproducible**: job completes as `diagnostic` outcome with "no fault found" work summary; diagnostic labour still bills per the labour line's rate type.
- **Odometer lower than last visit**: hard warning; override requires a reason (instrument cluster replaced) and both values are kept in history.
- **Customer wants the old parts back**: a "return old parts" flag on the card prints on the picking slip; warranty-claim parts (4.7) are excluded — those must go back to the supplier.
- **Power/network outage mid-shift**: paper fallback job numbers are reserved per branch range and keyed in later; the audit trail marks back-captured entries.

## Key Tables

- `job_cards` — the visit header: customer, vehicle, status, fault/diagnosis/work text, authorisation, timestamps, odometers.
- `job_card_labours` — labour lines: labour code, technician, rate type, hours (estimated/actual/billable), totals, warranty flag.
- `job_card_parts` — parts lines: part, bin, qty, cost and sell, warranty flag, issue audit.
