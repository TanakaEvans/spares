# 4.5 Vehicle Registry

> The master record of every vehicle the business has ever touched, with identity, specification, and permanent service history. Part of [Module 4: Workshop Management](../04-workshop-management.md).

## Purpose

The vehicle registry answers the two questions every workshop conversation starts with: *which exact vehicle is this*, and *what have we done to it before*? A registration plate and a 17-character VIN pin down the identity; make/model/variant, year, engine code, fuel type, and transmission pin down the specification — the difference between ordering brake pads for a Hilux 2.4 GD and a Hilux 2.8 GD-6 is exactly this data, and the parts-fitment filters in 4.4 run off it.

Service history is the registry's second half and its commercial engine. Every closed job card writes a history entry — date, odometer, work summary, technician, cost — creating the timeline that lets a service advisor say "you're 9,000 km past your last oil change" the moment the plate is typed. History drives service-due reminders, supports resale-value printouts customers ask for, and protects the workshop in disputes ("the brakes were advised worn and declined on 14 March — here is the record").

Vehicles outlive customers: cars are sold, owners change, but the vehicle's history stays with the vehicle. Ownership transfer relinks a vehicle to a new customer without breaking the timeline, and history is retained permanently even when a customer or vehicle record is soft-deleted.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Service Advisor | Create/edit vehicles, transfer ownership, view full history |
| Workshop Foreman / Technician | Read vehicle spec and history; append via job cards only |
| Counter Sales | Vehicle lookup for parts fitment; read-only spec |
| Workshop Manager | Merge duplicate vehicles, change status (`sold` / `written_off`), delete (soft) |
| Marketing (read-only) | Service-due lists for reminder campaigns |

## Workflow

```
Vehicle arrives / phone booking
        │
        ▼
Lookup by registration or VIN
        │
   ┌────┴─────┐
   ▼          ▼
 found     not found ──► Create vehicle
   │                       reg · VIN · make/model/variant · year ·
   │                       engine code · fuel · transmission · colour
   ▼
Owner matches? ── no ──► Ownership transfer (old owner keeps past history view)
   │ yes
   ▼
Attach to job card (4.1)
        │
        ▼
Job closed ──► vehicle_service_history entry written
  (date · odometer · type · summary · technician · cost)
        │
        ▼
History timeline grows ──► service-due engine ──► reminders
        │
        ▼
Vehicle sold / scrapped ──► status sold / written_off (history retained)
```

1. **Lookup** — Plate or VIN search first, always; creating without searching is the main source of duplicates and the create button sits behind the "no results" state.
2. **Create** — Spec captured from the vehicle reference catalogue (make → model → variant cascade), which auto-fills engine code, fuel, and transmission for a chosen variant.
3. **Transfer** — When the person in front of the counter isn't the recorded owner, transfer reassigns `customer_id` with an effective date; prior jobs remain attributed to the previous owner.
4. **Accumulate** — Closing a job card appends the history entry automatically; manual entries (work done elsewhere, declared by customer) are allowed but flagged `external`.
5. **Retire** — `sold` (left our customer base) or `written_off` ends active use; the record and history stay queryable forever.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Vehicle lookup | `workshop.vehicles.search` | Plate/VIN/customer search, quick-create |
| Vehicle detail & history | `workshop.vehicles.show` | Spec, owner, timeline, service-due status |
| (Embedded) vehicle panel | `workshop.jobs.create` | Inline lookup/create during job opening |

**Primary screen — Vehicle detail & history.** Header: registration (large), VIN, make/model/variant with year, colour swatch, status chip, current owner with transfer action, warranty-expiry badge when in warranty. Main area: **Timeline** — reverse-chronological service history cards (date, odometer with delta since previous, service type, work summary, technician, total cost, link to the job card), interleaved with declined-work markers from job authorisations. Side panel: spec block (engine code, fuel, transmission, purchase date), service-due computation (last service date/odometer vs interval), and open-job alert if the vehicle is currently in the workshop. Footer: *New job card*, *Transfer ownership*, *Edit spec*, *Print history report*.

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| registration | text | Yes | Uppercased, max 20; unique among `active` vehicles per branch |
| vin | text | No | Exactly 17 chars when given; excludes I/O/Q per VIN standard; unique among active vehicles |
| customer_id | lookup | No | Nullable for unknown-owner walk-ins; required before a job can be invoiced |
| make_id / model_id / variant_id | cascade select | Make+model Yes | Variant from catalogue; free-spec fallback logs a catalogue-gap note |
| year | int | Yes | 1950 – current year + 1 |
| engine_code | text | No | Max 30; auto-filled from variant |
| fuel_type / transmission | select | Yes | From enum lists |
| purchase_date / warranty_expiry | date | No | warranty_expiry ≥ purchase_date |
| history: odometer | int | Yes | ≥ previous entry (override with reason — cluster replaced) |
| history: service_type | select | Yes | `service` / `repair` / `diagnostic` |

## Business Rules

1. VIN, when present, must be exactly 17 valid characters; blank is allowed for pre-1981 and some grey-import vehicles.
2. Registration + VIN uniqueness applies to `active` vehicles only — a re-issued plate on a new car is legal once the old vehicle is `sold`/`written_off`.
3. Service history is permanent: soft-deleting a customer or vehicle never deletes history rows, and history entries are immutable once their job is closed (corrections via linked amendment notes).
4. Ownership transfer preserves job attribution: jobs remain linked to the owner at the time of the work; the new owner sees the vehicle's technical history but the previous owner's invoices remain theirs.
5. Odometer entries must be monotonic per vehicle; overrides require a reason and both readings are retained (feeds mileage-fraud visibility on history printouts).
6. A vehicle can have only one open job card at a time (rule shared with 4.1).
7. Service-due is computed from the later of time interval or distance interval using the variant's service schedule where catalogued, else branch defaults; `sold` and `written_off` vehicles drop out of reminder lists.
8. Merging duplicates (manager-only) re-parents all jobs and history to the surviving record and tombstones the duplicate with a redirect.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Same plate, two vehicles** (personalised plate moved to the owner's new car): the old vehicle is marked `sold` first, freeing the plate; the search on that plate shows both, clearly dated, so history never cross-contaminates.
- **Walk-in who doesn't know their VIN and the plate is unregistered** (fresh import): vehicle created with plate "TBA-{seq}" placeholder flag; invoicing warns until a real registration is captured.
- **Customer disputes an odometer reading** from a previous visit: readings are immutable; a signed amendment note attaches to the entry — the audit story stays intact.
- **Duplicate created despite lookup** (typo'd plate on first visit): manager merge; the system suggests candidates by VIN match, and by same variant + near plate string.
- **Vehicle written off, then reappears** (rebuilt and re-registered): `written_off` vehicles can be reactivated by the manager with the insurer/rebuild note; history continuity is exactly the point.
- **Fleet customer with 40 bakkies**: bulk import of vehicles from a fleet spreadsheet is supported at customer level; per-vehicle service-due rolls up to a fleet service-planning view.
- **Catalogue lacks the variant** (rare JDM import): free-spec entry allowed with a catalogue-gap flag; fitment filtering (4.4) degrades to make/model level and warns accordingly.
