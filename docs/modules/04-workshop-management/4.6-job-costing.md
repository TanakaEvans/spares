# 4.6 Job Costing

> Real-time cost vs billed vs margin per job — labour at internal cost, parts at stock cost, against invoice value. Part of [Module 4: Workshop Management](../04-workshop-management.md).

## Purpose

A workshop can be busy every day and still lose money; job costing is how you find out before the accountant does. For every job card it accumulates the *cost* side — technician hours at each technician's internal `labour_cost_per_hr`, parts at their stock cost (FIFO/AVCO, as issued from the ledger) — and sets it against the *billed* side: labour lines at customer rates and parts at selling prices. The difference is gross margin per job, visible live on the job card's costing tab while the vehicle is still on the hoist.

The classic example from the parent doc: a minor service bills $137.00 (3.5 hrs labour at retail plus oil and filter), costs $60.50 (internal labour $42.00 + parts $18.50), for a margin of $76.50 (55.8%). Costing exposes the jobs that don't look like that — the flat-rate brake job that took a junior twice book time, the goodwill rework absorbing hours nobody billed, the fixed-price quote that underestimated parts.

Aggregated, the same data answers management questions: margin by job type, by technician, by customer, by labour category; the true cost of comebacks and warranty work; and whether the fleet discount rate is still profitable at actual efficiency levels.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Workshop Manager | Full costing on all jobs; margin reports; cost-rate configuration inputs |
| Workshop Foreman | Costing tab on jobs in own branch; low-margin alerts |
| Service Advisor | Billed side only (no internal costs) — protects cost confidentiality at the counter |
| Finance | Full read; month-end WIP valuation and margin journals |
| Technician | No access to costing |

## Workflow

```
Job card activity                       Costing accumulator (live)
─────────────────                       ──────────────────────────
Technician clocks time (4.3) ─────────► labour cost += actual_hrs × tech cost rate
Labour line billed (4.2) ─────────────► labour billed += billable_hrs × rate − discount
Part issued (4.4) ────────────────────► parts cost += qty × unit_cost (ledger)
                                        parts billed += qty × unit_price − discount
Part returned ────────────────────────► both sides reduced
Warranty line flagged (4.7) ──────────► moved out of customer billed;
                                        expected claim recovery tracked
        │
        ▼
Job completed ──► costing snapshot frozen alongside invoice (4.8)
        │
        ▼
Aggregation: margin by job / technician / job type / customer / period
        │
        ▼
Month-end: open jobs = WIP valuation (labour + parts cost to date)
```

1. **Accumulate** — Every clock event, labour line, parts issue, and return updates the job's cost and billed totals in real time; no batch step.
2. **Alert** — When projected margin falls below the configurable floor (e.g. 35%) while the job is open, the foreman gets a low-margin flag — while there is still time to seek authorisation for extras or correct a mispriced line.
3. **Snapshot** — At invoicing, the costing summary is frozen with the invoice so later cost-rate or price changes never restate history.
4. **Aggregate** — Reports slice frozen snapshots plus live WIP by technician, job type, customer, category, and period.
5. **Value WIP** — Open jobs' accumulated cost is the workshop's work-in-progress figure for month-end.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Costing tab (job card) | `workshop.jobs.show` (Costing tab) | Live per-job cost/billed/margin breakdown |
| Margin reports | `workshop.costing.reports` | Aggregated margin analysis with drill-down |
| WIP valuation | `workshop.costing.wip` | Open-job cost totals for month-end |

**Primary screen — Costing tab.** Header: margin headline — total cost, total billed, margin value and percentage, with a red/amber/green band against the margin floor. Main area: two-section breakdown table. **Labour**: one row per labour line — technician, book hrs, actual hrs, internal cost, billed value, line margin (a row goes amber when actual > book by 25%). **Parts**: one row per issued line — part, qty, stock cost, sell, line margin. Below: warranty section (cost carried, expected claim recovery from 4.7), and unbilled-cost warnings (clocked time with no labour line — the classic leak). Side note: costing basis (AVCO/FIFO) and snapshot status (live / frozen at invoice).

## Fields & Validation

Costing is computed, not keyed. Configurable inputs:

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| margin floor % | decimal | Yes | 0–100; per branch, optionally per job type |
| technician cost rate | decimal | Yes | Maintained in 4.3; effective-dated, > 0 |
| costing method | select | Yes | `FIFO` / `AVCO` — inherited from Inventory config, read-only here |
| overhead uplift % | decimal | No | Optional loading on labour cost for absorbed overheads; 0–100 |
| snapshot | — | Auto | Frozen at invoice; immutable thereafter |

Computation rules: labour cost = Σ(actual clocked hrs × technician effective cost rate × (1 + overhead uplift)); parts cost = Σ(ledger cost of issued − returned); billed values come from labour/parts lines net of line discounts; warranty lines excluded from billed, included in cost, offset by expected claim value.

## Business Rules

1. Cost and billed sides are always computed from source records (time logs, ledger issues, lines) — never stored as editable totals; the costing tab is a view over facts.
2. The invoice-time snapshot is immutable; retrospective cost-rate corrections generate a variance report entry rather than restating closed-job margins.
3. Clocked time not attached to any labour line ("unbilled hours") stays in cost and is flagged — it can be zero-billed deliberately (goodwill) but never silently lost.
4. Warranty work shows a *net* margin: cost minus expected supplier recovery; when the claim settles short (4.7), the shortfall posts back to the job's final margin variance.
5. Internal jobs (workshop's own courtesy car) bill at zero and report in a separate internal-work category, excluded from customer-margin averages.
6. Comeback rework jobs link to the original job; their cost can be attributed against the original job's margin in the comeback-cost report (foreman attribution, per 4.3).
7. Access control is strict: selling-side users never see internal cost rates or stock costs — the API returns the billed side only for the advisor role.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Flat-rate job done in half book time** (senior tech, 0.6 actual vs 1.2 book): billed stays at book — margin improves; the efficiency gain is visible per line, which is the flat-rate incentive working as designed.
- **Flat-rate job blows out** (seized caliper bolts, 3.8 actual vs 1.5 book): billed unchanged, margin per line goes red; the foreman sees it live and can seek authorised extra labour (corrosion clause) instead of discovering the loss at month-end.
- **Two technicians at different cost rates on one line**: labour cost sums each technician's hours at their own rate; no averaging.
- **Part price-list update between issue and invoice**: both cost and sell were frozen at issue (4.4 rule); costing is unaffected — deliberate consistency.
- **Return of a part after the costing snapshot** (customer refunds a part post-invoice): handled as a credit note in Sales; the snapshot stands and the credit carries its own margin effect.
- **Technician cost rate missing an effective period** (new hire clocked before rate set): those hours cost at zero and the job is flagged "incomplete costing" — blocked from margin reports until the rate is backfilled and recomputed (allowed pre-snapshot only).
- **Negative margin on a warranty job before claim settlement**: expected — the net view with claim recovery is the honest number; the report separates "awaiting recovery" from truly negative jobs.
- **Month-end with a job mid-clock** (technician clocked on at cutoff): WIP includes the open clock valued to the cutoff timestamp; the WIP report notes open-clock jobs.

## Key Tables

Job costing owns no primary tables — it computes over:

- `job_card_labours`, `job_card_parts` (4.1) — billed values and warranty flags.
- `technician_time_logs`, `technicians.labour_cost_per_hr` (4.3) — labour cost side.
- Stock ledger issue entries (Inventory) — parts cost side.
- `job_costing_snapshots` — the single owned table: frozen per-job summary at invoice time.
