# 4.3 Technician Management

> Technician profiles, skills, availability, time clocking, and the efficiency/utilisation KPIs that run the workshop floor. Part of [Module 4: Workshop Management](../04-workshop-management.md).

## Purpose

The workshop's capacity is its technicians' hours, and this sub-module makes those hours visible and allocatable. Each technician has a profile extending their employee record: a technician code (printed on job cards), a skill level (`apprentice` to `master`), specialisations by category (a diesel specialist gets the Hilux 2.8 GD-6 injector job; the auto-electrician gets the parasitic-drain diagnosis), an internal cost rate for margin calculation, and a live availability state driven by their current job.

Clocking is the heartbeat. A technician clocks onto a specific labour line of a specific job and off again when done or interrupted; the resulting time logs feed three things: actual hours on the job card (billing for `actual_time` work), job costing (internal cost × actual hours, 4.6), and the KPI set every workshop manager lives by — **efficiency** (book hours sold ÷ clock hours worked, target > 100%), **utilisation** (productive hours ÷ attended hours, target > 85%), and **comeback rate** (vehicles back within 30 days for the same fault).

The foreman's board view sits on top of this: who is free, who is buried, which jobs are stalled with nobody clocked on, and which promised times are at risk.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Workshop Manager | Create/edit technician profiles, cost rates, skill levels; view all KPIs |
| Workshop Foreman | Allocate jobs on the board; correct clock entries (with reason); view team KPIs |
| Technician | Clock on/off own jobs; view own assignments and own KPIs only |
| HR (via Employees module) | Underlying employee record; leave feeding availability |
| Service Advisor | Read-only availability for promise-time estimation |

## Workflow

```
Employee record exists (HR)
        │
        ▼
Create technician profile
  code · skill level · specialisations · internal cost/hr
        │
        ▼
Available on foreman board
        │
        ▼
Job allocated (4.1) ──► technician clocks ON to a labour line
        │                       │
        │                  interruption? ──► clock OFF (reason: waiting parts,
        │                       │             helping colleague, break)
        │                       ▼
        │                 clock ON again / next line
        ▼
Clock OFF at task completion ──► duration written to time log
        │
        ▼
Time logs roll up:
  actual hrs per labour line ──► billing (actual_time) + costing (4.6)
  daily/weekly KPIs ──► efficiency · utilisation · comeback rate
```

1. **Profile** — Manager creates the technician against an employee, sets skill level, specialisation categories, and the internal `labour_cost_per_hr` (fully loaded cost, not wage).
2. **Allocate** — Foreman drags jobs to technicians on the board; the system warns when the job's category is outside the technician's specialisations or above their skill level.
3. **Clock on** — Technician selects the assigned job's labour line (touchscreen/scan of the job barcode) and clocks on; `current_job_id` updates and availability flips.
4. **Clock off** — On completion or interruption; interruptions carry a reason so idle time is classified, not lost.
5. **Review** — Foreman corrects mis-punches with a mandatory reason (audit-logged); KPIs recompute nightly and on demand.

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|

| Technician list | `workshop.technicians.index` | Profiles, skill levels, active status |

**Primary screen — Technician board.** Header: branch selector, date, shift totals (jobs in progress, awaiting allocation, at-risk promised times). Main area: one column per technician — avatar, skill-level badge, availability dot; job cards showing job number, vehicle, status, clocked-on indicator with running timer, promised-time countdown. An "Unallocated" first column holds incoming jobs; drag to assign. Card colour: green (on track), amber (promised −60 min), red (overdue). Side panel on card click: job summary with *Reassign*, *View job* actions.

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| employee_id | lookup | Yes | Active employee, not already a technician |
| technician_code | text | Yes | Unique, max 20, immutable after first clocking |
| skill_level | select | Yes | `apprentice` / `junior` / `senior` / `master` |
| specialisations | multi-select | Yes | ≥ 1 category from labour categories |
| labour_cost_per_hr | decimal | Yes | > 0; visible to manager/foreman only |
| is_available | toggle | Auto/manual | Auto-false while clocked on; manual off for leave/sick |
| clock: job_labour_id | select | Yes | Labour line of a job allocated to this technician, job in workable status |
| clock: interruption reason | select | On early clock-off | `awaiting_parts` / `assisting` / `break` / `reassigned` / `other`+note |
| correction: reason | text | Yes | Mandatory on any foreman clock edit; original values retained |

## Business Rules

1. A technician can be clocked onto **one labour line at a time**; clocking onto a second line auto-clocks off the first with reason `reassigned` — parallel clocks are impossible by construction.
2. Apprentices cannot be the primary technician on `repair` jobs of categories flagged safety-critical (brakes, steering); they may clock assist time on a senior's job.
3. Time logs are append-only: foreman corrections create an adjusting entry linked to the original, never an overwrite.
4. `labour_cost_per_hr` changes apply from the effective date forward; historic time logs cost at the rate in force when worked.
5. Utilisation counts clocked productive time against attended time from the HR attendance feed; unclassified gaps above 15 minutes appear on the foreman's daily exception list.
6. Comeback detection: a new job for the same vehicle within 30 days sharing the same labour category as a prior job flags both jobs and attributes the comeback to the prior job's primary technician (foreman can override attribution).
7. A technician with `is_available = false` (leave) cannot receive board allocations; their in-progress jobs surface for reassignment.
8. Clock events more than 12 hours long auto-close at shift end with an exception flag — nobody replaces a clutch for 14 unbroken hours.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Technician forgets to clock off and goes home**: the 12-hour/shift-end auto-close fires; the entry lands on the foreman's exception list for correction to the true time — KPIs exclude flagged entries until corrected.
- **Technician clocks onto two jobs** (attempted): the second clock-on succeeds and auto-closes the first — the classic double-clock is converted into a clean handover with an audit trail instead of overlapping time.
- **Two technicians on one job** (seized suspension bolt needs a second pair of hands): both clock onto the same labour line; actual hours sum for costing, while billable flat-rate hours are unchanged — efficiency reflects reality.
- **Mid-job reassignment** (urgent VIP job arrives): foreman drags the job away; the current technician's clock closes `reassigned`; the job card keeps both technicians in its labour history.
- **Clock terminal offline**: paper punch fallback; foreman back-captures with the correction workflow, entries marked back-captured.
- **Skill-mismatch override**: foreman may allocate outside specialisation (short-handed Saturday) — the warning requires an acknowledgement note, and the override is reportable (quality correlation).
- **Employee leaves the company**: technician profile deactivates with the employee; open time logs must be closed and jobs reassigned before HR can complete offboarding.
- **KPI gaming** (clocking off during slow diagnosis to protect efficiency): utilisation drops when efficiency is protected this way — the KPI pair is designed to be read together, and the technician detail screen plots both.
