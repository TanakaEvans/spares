# 4.8 Workshop Invoicing

> Converts a completed job card into a tax invoice through the Sales module — labour and parts lines, warranty exclusions, VAT, and payment. Part of [Module 4: Workshop Management](../04-workshop-management.md).

## Purpose

Invoicing is where the workshop's work becomes the company's revenue, and the conversion must be mechanical, not creative: when a job card reaches `completed`, the invoice is *generated from the card*, never typed afresh. Every labour line becomes an invoice line of type `labour` (code description, billable hours, rate, discount), every issued part becomes a line of type `part` (SKU, description, quantity, price, discount), warranty-flagged lines are excluded entirely (the customer owes nothing for warranty work — the supplier does, via 4.7), and VAT is computed per line on the non-warranty balance.

Generating rather than retyping kills the classic disputes — "the invoice doesn't match the job card" cannot happen when one is a projection of the other — and enforces the authorisation chain: only work the customer approved (original scope plus captured authorisations, 4.1) can appear on the document, with declined recommendations printed as unpriced advisories.

The invoice itself is a Sales-module document: it takes a number in the sales sequence, posts to the AR ledger against the customer's account (or is settled immediately for cash customers), and drives the job card's final transitions — `invoiced` on generation, `closed` on payment — which in turn freezes the costing snapshot (4.6) and writes the vehicle's service history entry (4.5).

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Service Advisor | Generate invoice from completed jobs, apply approved discounts, take payment, email/print |
| Workshop Foreman | Verify job-to-invoice content pre-generation; cannot invoice |
| Branch Manager | Discounts above advisor limit; credit-limit overrides; re-open invoiced jobs |
| Finance (AR) | Ledger view, allocations, credit notes against workshop invoices |
| Cashier | Take payment against generated invoices |

## Workflow

```
Job card status: completed (QC passed, odometer out captured)
        │
        ▼
Pre-invoice review (advisor)
  lines from job card · authorisation coverage check · discounts
        │
        ├── account customer? ──► credit limit / credit hold check
        │                              │ blocked ──► manager override or payment up front
        ▼                              ▼
Generate invoice (Sales module)
  labour lines + parts lines − warranty lines · VAT per line
        │
        ▼
Job card: invoiced · costing snapshot frozen (4.6)
        │
        ▼
Present to customer ──► payment (cash/card/EFT/account)
        │
        ▼
Paid / allocated ──► Job card: closed ──► service history written (4.5)
        │
        └── dispute? ──► credit note via Sales (linked back to job)
```

1. **Review** — The advisor opens the pre-invoice screen: every billable line from the card with running totals, warranty lines shown greyed-and-excluded, and an authorisation-coverage check that every line traces to original scope or a captured authorisation.
2. **Credit check** — Account customers are validated against credit limit and hold status; failures require manager override or convert the sale to cash terms.
3. **Generate** — One action creates the Sales invoice; the job card locks (`invoiced`), stores `sales_document_id`, and the costing snapshot freezes.
4. **Settle** — Payment is taken at the counter or the invoice ages on the customer's account; full settlement (or account posting per policy) closes the job.
5. **After-sales** — Corrections post-generation are Sales credit notes referencing the invoice, keeping the job card immutable.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Pre-invoice review | `workshop.jobs.invoice` | Line review, discounts, credit check, generate |
| Invoice document | (Sales module) `sales.invoices.show` | The tax invoice, payment, print/email |
| Ready-to-invoice queue | `workshop.jobs.index?status=completed` | Completed jobs awaiting invoicing, aged |

**Primary screen — Pre-invoice review.** Header: job number, customer with account status chip (cash / account / on-hold), vehicle summary, completion date, aging since completion. Main area: invoice preview grid — labour lines (description, hrs, rate, discount, total) then parts lines (SKU, description, qty, price, discount, total); warranty lines rendered struck-through with "warranty — not charged" badges; declined-work advisories listed below as unpriced text. Totals panel: subtotal excl, VAT breakdown by rate, total incl, less deposit already taken. Footer: *Back to job*, *Apply discount* (limit-checked), *Generate invoice*, and for account customers the credit-status banner with override control (manager credential).

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| job (source) | — | Yes | Status must be `completed`; odometer_out captured; no open part requests |
| customer account state | auto | — | Credit limit incl. this invoice ≤ limit; no credit hold — else override path |
| line discounts | decimal | No | Per-line; advisor cap (e.g. 10%) then manager approval; reason code required |
| overall discount | decimal | No | Same caps; applied pro-rata across non-warranty lines |
| VAT | auto | — | Per line by VAT code; warranty lines excluded from base |
| payment method(s) | select | At settlement | Split tender supported; account posting only for account customers |
| deposit applied | auto | — | Deposits taken at booking auto-allocate to the invoice |

## Business Rules

1. Invoice content is a strict projection of the job card: all non-warranty labour lines → `labour` invoice lines, all non-warranty, non-returned part issues → `part` invoice lines; nothing can be added on the invoice that isn't on the card (missing item ⇒ fix the card, foreman-gated re-open).
2. Warranty lines never appear as chargeable lines; branch-configurable whether they print as zero-value informational lines ("replaced under warranty") or are omitted.
3. VAT is calculated per line by each line's VAT code (parts and labour can differ), on the discounted value; totals reconcile per rate on the tax invoice footer.
4. Generating the invoice freezes the job (`invoiced`) and the costing snapshot (4.6); any subsequent change is a Sales credit note, never an edit.
5. `quote_only` jobs cannot be invoiced (4.1 rule) — they convert to quotations; a quotation accepted later opens a fresh job card.
6. Credit-held account customers cannot post to account; the invoice may still be generated for immediate cash/card settlement.
7. Deposits taken at booking are recorded against the job and auto-allocate at invoicing; over-deposits produce a refund line item through Sales.
8. Job `closed` requires full payment for cash customers or successful account posting for account customers; closing writes the service-history row (4.5) with the invoice total.
9. Aging alert: jobs sitting `completed` > 48 hours uninvoiced escalate to the branch manager — finished cars in the yard are unbilled revenue and insurance risk.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Customer disputes a line at collection** ("I never approved the wiper blades"): the authorisation trail resolves it on-screen; if the workshop is wrong, the manager re-opens (foreman-gated), the part is returned via 4.4, and the invoice regenerates — pre-payment, this is clean; post-payment it is a credit note.
- **Vehicle collected before payment** (known account customer in a rush, cash policy): release-without-payment requires manager authorisation logged with the invoice; the job stays `invoiced`, appearing on the unpaid-released report until settled.
- **Split billing** (insurance pays panel-adjacent work, customer pays service items): line-level split to two invoices for two debtors from one job card, each invoice referencing the job; totals must cover all billable lines exactly.
- **Fleet customer requires PO number on invoice**: customer flag enforces a mandatory order-number field before generation; missing PO is the top cause of fleet non-payment.
- **Price disagreement between quote and invoice** (quoted from an estimate, prices moved): lines carry issue-time/line-creation prices (4.2/4.4 freeze rules); if a formal quotation exists, the quoted totals bind and the variance flags for manager attention before generation.
- **Payment gateway fails mid-settlement**: tender records are atomic — a failed card attempt leaves the invoice unpaid with the attempt logged; no partial ghost payments.
- **Warranty job with a chargeable extra** (warranty pump plus customer-requested wiper blades): one card, mixed lines; the invoice bills only the non-warranty lines — the common case the exclusion logic exists for.
- **Invoice generated, then warranty claim rejected** (4.7): the customer is not retro-billed; the unrecovered cost writes off to warranty expense (4.7 rule 7) — customer trust outranks recovery.
