# 4.4 Parts Requisition

> Controls the issue of parts from the spares counter to workshop jobs — reservation, pick, issue, and return of unused parts. Part of [Module 4: Workshop Management](../04-workshop-management.md).

## Purpose

The wall between the parts store and the workshop floor is where spares businesses bleed margin. This sub-module enforces the rule that no part crosses that wall except against a specific job card: the technician requests, the storeman picks and scans out, the stock ledger records `JOB_CARD_OUT`, and the part appears on the job's parts tab at both cost (for margin) and selling price (for the invoice).

The flow is two-phase by design. A **request** places a reservation — `qty_available` drops immediately so the counter cannot sell the last Hilux 2.8 GD-6 fuel filter out from under a booked service — and an **issue** converts the reservation into a physical stock movement when the storeman hands the part over. If the part is not in stock, the request automatically raises a purchase requisition (Module 3.1) linked back to the job card, and the job flips to `awaiting_parts` so the board shows why nothing is happening.

The reverse path matters just as much: fitted the gasket set, didn't need the second bottle of sealant — unused parts return to the store as `RETURN_IN`, the job's parts cost drops, and the customer isn't billed for shelf stock.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Technician | Raise parts requests on own jobs; return unused parts |
| Service Advisor | Raise requests during quoting/booking; view issue status |
| Storeman | Pick, issue (scan out), accept returns, substitute with approval |
| Workshop Foreman | Approve substitutions and over-quantity requests; cancel requests |
| Workshop Manager | Write-off authority for damaged-in-fitting parts; issue-audit reports |

## Workflow

```
Technician on job card ──► request part(s) + qty
        │
        ▼
In stock? ──── no ──► auto purchase requisition (3.1, linked to job)
   │ yes                       │
   ▼                           ▼
Reservation placed        job status: awaiting_parts
(qty_available ↓)              │
   │                    goods arrive (GRN 3.3) ──► reservation fulfilled, notify
   ▼                                                       │
Pick list at spares counter ◄──────────────────────────────┘
        │
        ▼
Storeman picks ──► scans part + job barcode ──► ISSUE
        │
        ▼
Stock ledger: JOB_CARD_OUT · job_card_parts line (cost + sell)
        │
        ▼
[unused after work] ──► return to store ──► inspect ──► RETURN_IN
                                             │
                                   damaged in fitting? ──► write-off (manager)
```

1. **Request** — From the job card's parts tab (or scan at the counter), the technician requests part and quantity; fitment data from the vehicle's make/model pre-filters the part search.
2. **Reserve** — Stock is reserved at the branch; the request shows expected pick time. Out-of-stock lines raise the linked purchase requisition automatically.
3. **Pick & issue** — The storeman works the pick queue, scans the part and the job barcode; the issue posts the ledger entry, stamps `issued_at`/`issued_by`, and prices the line from the customer's price list.
4. **Fit** — Parts show as issued on the job; warranty-flagged lines carry `is_warranty` and are excluded from customer billing.
5. **Return** — Unused parts go back with a return action referencing the issued line; accepted returns post `RETURN_IN` and reduce the job's cost and billing.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Parts tab (job card) | `workshop.jobs.show` (Parts tab) | Request, track, and return parts for one job |
| Pick queue | `workshop.parts.queue` | Storeman's ordered list of open requests |
| Issue station | `workshop.parts.issue` | Scan-driven issue confirmation |

**Primary screen — Pick queue.** Header: branch, open-request count, oldest-request age, awaiting-stock count. Main area: request rows sorted by job priority then request time — job number, vehicle, technician, part (SKU, description, bin location), qty requested, stock state chip (`reserved` / `awaiting stock — PO ref` / `partial`). Row expands to show fitment note and substitution suggestions. Actions per row: *Pick* (opens issue station pre-loaded), *Substitute* (foreman approval flow), *Cancel request*. VIP/urgent jobs pin to the top with a red left border.

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| part_id | part search | Yes | Active part; fitment mismatch with job vehicle raises a confirm dialog |
| qty | decimal | Yes | > 0; > typical qty for the labour code (e.g. 5 brake pad sets) needs foreman approval |
| bin_location_id | select | Auto | Storeman may override at pick (bin discrepancy logged) |
| unit_price | decimal | Auto | From customer's price list at issue time; discount per line rules from Sales |
| is_warranty | boolean | Auto | Inherited from job type `warranty` or set with linked claim (4.7) |
| return: qty | decimal | Yes | ≤ issued qty minus already returned |
| return: condition | select | Yes | `unused` (restock) / `damaged_fitting` (write-off path) |
| substitution: alternative part | lookup | Yes | Must be a linked alternative/cross-reference of the requested part |

## Business Rules

1. Reservation on request, ledger movement on issue: `qty_available` falls at request time, `qty_on_hand` falls only at issue — the two-phase rule from the parent doc, and the reason counter sales can trust availability figures.
2. Out-of-stock requests always create the linked purchase requisition (never a silent backorder); the PR line carries the job card reference so goods receipt can notify the workshop and auto-fulfil the reservation.
3. Unused-part returns post `RETURN_IN` at the issued cost and remove the line value from the job — but only in `unused` condition; `damaged_fitting` returns require manager write-off and the cost stays on the job (charged to workshop expense, not the customer, per policy flag).
4. Issued lines cannot be deleted, only returned; cancelling an unissued request simply releases the reservation.
5. Substitution (requested part out of stock but a cross-referenced alternative available) requires foreman approval and reprices at the substitute's price; the original request is preserved for demand statistics.
6. Price at issue is frozen on the line; later price-list changes never reprice issued lines.
7. A job cannot pass `quality_check` with open (unissued, uncancelled) part requests.
8. Serial/batch-tracked parts capture the identifier at issue, linking the exact unit to the vehicle — required for warranty claims (4.7) and recalls.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Counter sale grabs the reserved part anyway** (manual override at POS): POS blocks selling into reservation except with manager override; the override notifies the foreman on the affected job immediately — the fight happens before the customer is promised, not after.
- **Technician requests against the wrong job** (two Hiluxes in the shop): unissued — cancel and re-raise; issued — a "transfer between jobs" action moves the line with both job numbers in the audit trail, rather than a return + reissue that would distort stats.
- **Part fits but fitment data says it doesn't** (catalogue gap): the confirm dialog lets the technician proceed with a "fitment override" note; overrides feed a fitment-data correction queue for the catalogue maintainer.
- **Partial stock** (need 4 shocks, 2 on hand): the request splits — 2 reserved for pick, 2 on the auto-PR; the job stays `awaiting_parts` until the balance arrives, with the technician able to start on what's issued.
- **Return of an opened-box electrical part**: policy-flagged categories (ECUs, sensors) block `unused` restock returns — electrical parts are notoriously non-returnable; the line stays on the job and the dialog explains why.
- **GRN arrives for a job-linked PR after the job was cancelled**: the receipt notification flags the orphaned special order; stock goes to the shelf and the buyer is prompted about returning it (3.6) if it is a non-stock line.
- **Scan mismatch at issue** (barcode reads a different SKU than picked): issue blocks; the storeman resolves bin mix-up before anything moves — mis-issues caught at the door, not at stocktake.
