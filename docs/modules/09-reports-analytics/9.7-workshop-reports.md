# 9.7 Workshop Reports

> Productivity, technician efficiency, job profitability, and quality metrics for the fitment workshop. Part of [Module 9: Reports & Analytics](../09-reports-analytics.md).

## Purpose

Where a branch runs a workshop (see the `is_workshop` branch flag), these reports answer whether the bays are making money. Workshop economics differ from counter sales: revenue is labour hours plus parts, cost is technician time plus parts at cost, and profit leaks through slow jobs, comebacks, and unbilled hours. Each report targets one leak.

The suite covers seven reports: Workshop Productivity (jobs opened/completed/invoiced, revenue, average job value), Technician Efficiency (flat-rate hours billed vs actual hours, utilisation), Job Profitability (per-job parts cost, labour cost, billed, margin), Comeback Rate (vehicles back within 30 days for the same fault, by technician), Outstanding Job Cards (open beyond X days), Parts Usage in Workshop (informs workshop-specific stock levels), and Warranty Claims (submitted/approved/rejected, value recovered vs claimed, by supplier).

## Workflow

```
[Daily]   [Outstanding Job Cards: open > X days, oldest first]
              │
              ▼
          [Foreman chases stalled jobs / parts holds]

[Weekly]  [Technician Efficiency: billed flat-rate hrs vs
           actual hrs; utilisation %]
              │
              ▼
          [Coaching / allocation decisions]

[Monthly] [Productivity + Job Profitability + Comeback Rate]
              │
              ▼
          [Pricing review: labour rate, flat-rate times,
           quality follow-up per technician]
```

1. **Track throughput** — Productivity counts job cards opened, completed, invoiced; workshop revenue and average job value for the period.
2. **Measure efficiency** — per technician: flat-rate hours billed ÷ actual hours worked (efficiency %), and productive time ÷ available time (utilisation %). Both are needed — a technician can be efficient but under-utilised.
3. **Check margin** — Job Profitability lists each job's parts cost, labour cost, billed amount, gross margin; loss-making jobs sort to the top.
4. **Watch quality** — Comeback Rate flags vehicles (e.g. a Hilux back within 30 days for the same brake shudder) grouped by original technician.
5. **Recover warranty** — Warranty Claims tracks the pipeline per supplier and the gap between value claimed and value recovered.

**Primary screen — Technician Efficiency**: period selector; one row per technician with columns: actual hours clocked, flat-rate hours billed, efficiency %, available hours, productive hours, utilisation %, jobs completed, comebacks. Rows expand to the underlying job cards. Technicians opening the report see only their own row.

## Business Rules

1. Efficiency % = flat-rate (billed) hours ÷ actual clocked hours × 100; jobs without time clocking are excluded and counted in a data-quality note, not assumed at 100%.
2. Utilisation % = productive clocked time ÷ available time (roster hours minus approved leave); the two percentages are never conflated.
3. A comeback = same vehicle returning within the window with the same fault/labour code as a completed job; the foreman confirms or dismisses each candidate before it counts against a technician.
4. Job Profitability values parts at cost when issued to the job and labour at the technician cost rate, not the charge-out rate; warranty jobs use the `warranty_labour_rate` setting.
5. Productivity's "invoiced" count uses invoice date, so a job completed in March and invoiced in April counts completed-March, invoiced-April — the report shows both counts to expose billing lag.
6. Outstanding Job Cards sorts oldest first and shows the blocking reason where recorded (awaiting parts, awaiting authority, awaiting payment).
7. Warranty recovery = credit/payment received from supplier ÷ value claimed; claims pending beyond a threshold age are flagged.
8. Internal jobs (workshop work on company vehicles) are excluded from revenue metrics and shown separately.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Job spanning the period boundary**: opened counts in its opening period, completed in its completion period; no job is double-counted within one metric.
- **Two technicians on one job**: labour splits by clocked hours per technician; efficiency attributes each their share of the flat-rate hours.
- **Comeback where the second visit is a different fault**: foreman dismisses the candidate; dismissal is logged so quality disputes are auditable.
- **Job invoiced before completion** (deposit/progress billing): profitability uses cumulative billed vs cumulative cost and marks the job "in progress".
- **Technician terminated mid-period**: appears with partial-period figures, marked inactive; utilisation uses rostered days only.
- **Parts issued to a job then returned to stock**: usage and job cost are net of returns.
- **Branch without a workshop selected**: hub hides workshop reports entirely rather than showing empty grids.
