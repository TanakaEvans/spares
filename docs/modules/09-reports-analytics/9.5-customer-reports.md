# 9.5 Customer Reports

> Customer activity, debt, loyalty, and lifecycle — for collections and sales growth. Part of [Module 9: Reports & Analytics](../09-reports-analytics.md).

## Purpose

Customer reports serve two masters. Accounts uses them defensively: the Customer Ageing (Debtors) report drives the weekly collections call list, and the Credit Limit Review catches accounts drifting toward risk before they default. Sales uses them offensively: Top Customers identifies who deserves priority treatment and negotiated pricing; Dormant Customers is a ready-made re-engagement call list; the New Customer Report shows whether marketing is bringing in first-time buyers.

The suite covers seven reports: Customer Ageing, Top Customers, Customer Purchase History, Dormant Customers, New Customer Report, Loyalty Points Report, and Credit Limit Review. Purchase History doubles as the customer-service screen — when a trade customer phones about "that invoice from March", this report answers without leaving the page.

## User Roles & Permissions

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| Super Admin / Owner | All reports |
| Accountant / Accounts Clerk | Ageing, Credit Limit Review, Purchase History |
| Sales Manager | All reports, margin columns visible |
| Cashier / Counter Staff | Purchase History lookup for a customer in front of them; no ageing |
| Branch Manager | All reports scoped to own branch's customers |
| Reports Only | Per role config |

## Workflow

```
[Weekly collections cycle]                [Monthly sales cycle]
        │                                        │
        ▼                                        ▼
[Customer Ageing: balances by          [Top Customers (spend/visits/
 current/30/60/90+]                     margin) + Dormant Customers]
        │                                        │
        ▼                                        ▼
[Flag high-risk: 60+ balances,         [Call lists / re-engagement
 broken promises]                       offers to sales team]
        │
        ▼
[Credit Limit Review: near/over
 limit, on hold, recommended
 adjustments]
        │
        ▼
[Actions in Customers module:
 hold account, adjust limit]
```

1. **Age the book** — Ageing lists every trade customer with a balance, bucketed current/30/60/90+, high-risk flagged.
2. **Work the list** — drill from an aged balance into Purchase History to see the unpaid invoices and payment pattern.
3. **Review limits** — Credit Limit Review shows near-limit/over-limit/on-hold accounts with recommended adjustments based on payment history.
4. **Grow** — Top Customers (MTD/YTD/12-month, ranked by spend, visits, or margin contribution) and Dormant Customers (no purchase in configurable 60/90/180 days) feed sales outreach.
5. **Track loyalty** — points balances, points expiring in 30/60 days, earned vs redeemed per period.

## Screens

| Screen | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Customer reports hub | `reports.customers.index` | Report list + filters (`Reports/Customers/Index.jsx`) |
| Ageing view | `reports.customers.index?report=ageing` | Bucketed debtor grid |
| Purchase history | `reports.customers.index?report=history&customer=...` | One customer's full transaction list |

**Primary screen — Customer Ageing**: as-at date selector; grid of customer, credit limit, total balance, then bucket columns current/30/60/90+, last payment date, and a risk flag. Rows sort by 90+ descending by default; row expand reveals the open invoices behind the balance. Footer totals reconcile to the AR control account.

## Fields & Validation

| Field | Type | Required | Validation rules |
|-------|------|----------|------------------|
| as_at date | date | Ageing | not in the future |
| period | select | Top/New/Loyalty | MTD / YTD / last 12 months / custom |
| dormancy window | select | Dormant | 60 / 90 / 180 days (configurable) |
| customer | search-select | Purchase History | required for history view |
| branch | select | No | permitted branches |
| rank by | select | Top Customers | total spend / number of visits / gross margin contribution |

## Business Rules

1. Ageing buckets and as-at logic match [9.4 Financial Reports](9.4-financial-reports.md) AR Ageing exactly; the sum always reconciles to the AR control account.
2. High-risk flag = any 90+ balance, or 60+ balance exceeding half the credit limit, or account already on hold.
3. Credit Limit Review recommendations are advisory: computed from average monthly spend and days-to-pay, they never change a limit automatically — changes happen in the Customers module with approval.
4. Dormancy is measured from last *invoice* date (quotes don't count as activity); cash-sale walk-ins without accounts are excluded.
5. Top Customers by margin requires the margin permission; without it the rank-by option hides.
6. Loyalty expiring-points figures use the `loyalty_expiry_months` setting; the expiring list is the source for reminder campaigns.
7. New Customer Report counts first registration in the period and joins first purchase details; registered-but-never-purchased customers show explicitly with a blank first purchase.
8. Purchase History shows every document (invoice, credit note, receipt) with status and payment allocation.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Credit balance customer** (overpaid or credited): appears in ageing with a negative balance in "current", clearly signed — omitting them would break control-account reconciliation.
- **Customer with multiple branches' activity**: branch filter scopes documents; the "all branches" total is only offered to consolidated roles.
- **Merged duplicate customers**: history follows the surviving record and notes the merge; ageing never double-counts.
- **Dormant customer who is also on hold for debt**: flagged in the dormant list so sales doesn't re-engage an account accounts is chasing.
- **Loyalty points on a returned sale**: earned points reverse with the credit note; the report's earned/redeemed columns are net of reversals.
- **Very long histories** (multi-year trade account): Purchase History paginates server-side with a date filter defaulting to the last 12 months.
