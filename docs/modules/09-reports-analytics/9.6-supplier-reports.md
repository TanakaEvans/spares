# 9.6 Supplier Reports

> Spend, performance, delivery, and landed cost — the buyer's negotiating evidence. Part of [Module 9: Reports & Analytics](../09-reports-analytics.md).

## Purpose

Supplier reports turn purchasing history into leverage. The Spend Report quantifies annual volume per supplier for rebate negotiations and vendor rationalisation ("we put $180k through you last year — where's our settlement discount?"). The Performance Scorecard makes delivery reliability measurable: on-time rate, fill rate, return/rejection rate, and invoice accuracy, trended over 12 months, so switching decisions rest on data rather than anecdotes.

The operational reports keep day-to-day buying honest: PO Status lists everything open and overdue; Landed Cost Analysis shows what imported stock *really* cost after freight, customs, and clearing — critical for a Southern African importer where landed cost can run far above the supplier invoice; and Price List Comparison puts the same part's cost, lead time, and MOQ across suppliers side by side.

## Workflow

```
[Quarterly supplier review]
        │
        ▼
[Spend Report: total per supplier] ──► [Rebate / rationalisation talks]
        │
        ▼
[Performance Scorecard: on-time %, fill %,
 rejects %, invoice accuracy, 12-mo trend]
        │
        ▼
[Decision: keep / renegotiate / dual-source / drop]

Daily buying loop:
[PO Status] → [Overdue POs flagged] → [Chase supplier / update ETA]
[Landed Cost Analysis per shipment] → [Verify sell prices still hold margin]
```

1. **Spend** — total purchasing per supplier for a period, with document drill-down.
2. **Score** — the scorecard computes: on-time delivery (received by promised date), fill rate (qty received ÷ qty ordered first delivery), return/rejection rate, invoice accuracy (supplier invoice vs PO/GRN price matches); each trended monthly.
3. **Chase** — PO Status lists open POs with expected dates; overdue (date passed, not fully received) sorts to the top.
4. **Cost** — Landed Cost Analysis breaks each shipment into product cost + freight + customs + clearing and shows the resulting unit-cost uplift vs the original PO cost.
5. **Compare** — Price List Comparison lays out one part across suppliers: cost, lead time, MOQ — the pre-order decision screen.

## Business Rules

1. Spend is measured on GRN-received value (goods actually received), with a toggle for invoiced value; the report states which basis is displayed.
2. On-time = fully received on or before the PO promised date (+ tolerance); a PO with no promised date is excluded from the on-time metric rather than counted either way.
3. Fill rate uses the *first* delivery against each PO line — later top-ups don't retroactively repair the score.
4. Rejection rate counts GRN lines rejected or returned-to-supplier ÷ lines received.
5. Invoice accuracy = supplier invoices matching PO/GRN within tolerance ÷ total invoices; mismatches link to the price-variance records in Purchasing.
6. Landed cost apportions freight/customs/clearing across shipment lines by the method recorded on the shipment (value or weight); the report never re-apportions.
7. Price List Comparison reads current supplier price lists plus last actual cost; stale price lists (older than a configurable age) are marked.
8. Overdue POs remain listed until fully received, cancelled, or closed short — closing short is a purchasing action, not a report action.

## Edge Cases & Error Handling

- **Supplier with < 5 POs in the window**: scorecard renders with a low-sample warning instead of presenting 100%/0% as meaningful.
- **Split shipments agreed upfront** (back-order arranged): buyer can mark the PO line "scheduled delivery", excluding it from fill-rate penalty.
- **Currency-mixed spend** (ZAR and USD suppliers): converted to base currency at document-date rates; the report notes the conversion basis.
- **Landed cost finalised after stock already selling**: the analysis shows provisional vs final apportionment and the cost adjustment posted on finalisation.
- **Same part, different pack sizes across suppliers** (e.g. glow plugs boxed 4 vs 10): comparison normalises to unit cost and shows pack/MOQ separately.
- **Supplier deactivated with open POs**: still appears in PO Status until documents close; excluded from comparison for new buying.
