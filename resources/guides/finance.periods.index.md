# About this page

Financial years and their periods. Postings are only accepted into an **open** period — this is what stops back-dating into a finalised month.

## Lifecycle
Open → **Close** (freezes the month; can be reopened) → **Lock** (permanent). At month-end, close the period once receipts, payments and reconciliations are done.

## Watch out
- A posting dated in a closed or locked period is rejected at the source (invoice, receipt, journal) with a re-date prompt.
