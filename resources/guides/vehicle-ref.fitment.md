# About this page

Answer the counter's most important question: **"what fits this car?"** Pick the vehicle — make first, then narrow by model, variant and year — and every compatible part appears below, grouped by category, with live stock.

## How it works
- Make-level fitments cover ALL models of that make; model-level cover all its variants.
- Green **in stock** means it is on the shelf right now; click any part to see where.
- Type a year to exclude parts whose fitment range does not cover it.

## Watch out
- A part missing here just means no fitment record yet — check [Cross-Reference](route:vehicle-ref.cross-ref) before telling a customer no.
- Fitments are maintained on each part's detail page (Fitments tab).
