# About this page

Capture a sales order directly — for phoned-in or counter orders that skip the quote stage.

## How it works
- Search the part the same way as the till: vehicle + part type ("hilux brake pad"), part number, or `3*CODE` for multiples.
- Pick the customer first — their price list sets every line's price, and changing the customer re-prices the lines.
- Amber **short** badges mean the line can't be fully reserved; fix the quantity before saving or the server will refuse it.

Saving **confirms** the order and reserves every line's stock at this branch. The draft survives a refresh until saved.
