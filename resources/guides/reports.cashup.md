# About this page

The daily cash-up (X/Z read) for a branch — every till taking and account receipt for the chosen day, summarised by payment method and by cashier. It reads straight from posted sales and receipts, so it always agrees with the books.

## Reading it
- **Banked** is cash + card + EFT — the money that should reach the bank/drawer. **On account** is credit extended (not money collected). **Cash in drawer** is the cash portion, net of the change given.
- **By payment method** splits each method into counter sales and AR receipts. **By cashier** attributes each taking to the person who rang it up (the tender's cashier, falling back to the salesperson).

## Doing the cash-up
Pick the date and branch, count your drawer against **Cash in drawer**, then **Print cash-up** for a signed slip with a counted-by / variance line.
