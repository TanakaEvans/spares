# About this page

The owner's-eye view — the KPIs that matter, computed live from the ledger every time you open it (no overnight batch, nothing stale).

## The tiles
Today's and month-to-date sales (net of credit notes), outstanding debtors and the 60+ day slice, stock value at cost, and open job cards. The charts show the 12-month sales trend (this year vs last), debtors ageing, and the top parts and categories for the month.
