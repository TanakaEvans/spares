# About this page

Customer returns and refunds. A credit note is always raised **against a posted invoice**, so quantities can never exceed what was sold.

## How it works
- Choose the invoice lines and quantities to credit (capped at the remaining creditable qty).
- **Restock** returns good stock at its original cost; leave it off for scrapped goods.
- VAT reverses at each line's **original rate**; refund as **cash** or **to the account**.
