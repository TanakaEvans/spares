# About this page

Manage the currencies the business trades in and capture the **daily exchange rate** against the base currency (USD).

## Every morning
1. Get today's rate.
2. Pick the currency, enter **buy** (what you pay for foreign cash) and **sell** (what you charge), and press **Capture Rate**.
3. The amber banner disappears once today's rates are in.

## Watch out
- **Sell must be at least buy** — the spread protects the business.
- Old rates are never changed: documents from last week reprint at last week's rate.
- Sales may fall back to the last known rate when today's is missing — or be blocked entirely, depending on the [Configuration Centre](route:admin.settings.index) setting.

## Related
- Dual-currency display (USD + ZWG on receipts) is switched on in the [Configuration Centre](route:admin.settings.index).
