# About this page

Capture the supplier's invoice against a goods received note — the **3-way match** (PO price × GRN quantity × invoice amount) runs when you save.

## How it works
- The left card shows exactly what arrived. Enter the supplier's invoice number, date and amounts.
- **Within tolerance** (max 2% or R10 of the GRN value): the invoice posts straight to Accounts Payable.
- **Outside tolerance**: it's captured as **disputed** — a buyer must resolve it before it posts.
- The due-date hint uses the supplier's payment terms; VAT defaults to the configured rate — fix it if the invoice differs.
