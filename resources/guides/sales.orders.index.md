# About this page

Confirmed sales orders awaiting fulfilment. An order **reserves stock** without moving it; the money and stock move only when you invoice it.

The chips at the top count the orders still to fulfil and their value, and how many were invoiced today.

## Lifecycle
Confirmed → **Fulfil & Invoice** (take payment, posts the tax invoice, releases the reservation) → Invoiced. **Cancel** releases the reservation without invoicing.

## Getting an order in
- **New Order** — capture a phoned-in or counter order directly. It confirms and reserves on save; a line that's short on stock is flagged before you commit.
- Convert a quotation from its detail page.
