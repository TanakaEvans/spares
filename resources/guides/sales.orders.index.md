# About this page

Confirmed sales orders awaiting fulfilment. An order **reserves stock** without moving it; the money and stock move only when you invoice it.

## Lifecycle
Confirmed → **Fulfil & Invoice** (posts the tax invoice, releases the reservation) → Invoiced. **Cancel** releases the reservation without invoicing.
