# About this page

One posted customer receipt. Receipts are **immutable** — the figures here are exactly what was posted (DR Bank / CR Debtors control) and the journal link opens the entry in the GL.

## Reading the page
- **Amount received** is the cash/card/EFT that came in; **Allocated** is how much was matched to invoices; **Unallocated** is credit sitting on the customer's account (it still reduces their balance, it just isn't matched to a specific invoice yet).
- **Allocations** list every invoice the receipt settled, fully or partly. Click an invoice number to open it.
- **Connected to** shows the chain Invoice(s) → Receipt and links the customer, journal and the user who captured it.

## Corrections
A receipt cannot be edited. If it was captured against the wrong customer or amount, post a reversing journal through Finance → Journals (or ask an accountant to), then capture the correct receipt.
