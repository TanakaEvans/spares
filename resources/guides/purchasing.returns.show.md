# About this page

One return to supplier — the parts going back, why, and where the money is: draft → **shipped** (stock leaves the shelf) → **credited** (the supplier's credit note reduces what you owe them).

## Working the return
1. Get an RMA number from the supplier, enter it and **Ship Return** — stock is reduced at that point.
2. When their credit note arrives, **Capture Credit** with its reference and amount; any difference from the stock value posts to purchase variances.

## Watch out
- Shipping needs the supplier's RMA — there is no way round it.
- Credits appear in [Supplier Credit Notes](route:purchasing.supplier-credits.index) once captured.
