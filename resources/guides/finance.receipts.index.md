# About this page

Money coming in from account customers, and how old the debtors book is. The ageing table buckets every open invoice by how far past its due date it sits (current / 30 / 60 / 90+).

## Capturing a receipt
New Receipt → find the customer → enter the amount and method → **allocate** it across their open invoices (or "auto-settle oldest first"). Posting does DR bank/till, CR Trade Debtors (1210). Anything left unallocated sits as a credit on the account.

## Watch out
- 90+ day balances are highlighted — chase them.
- A receipt never exceeds an invoice's outstanding; the rest stays unapplied.
