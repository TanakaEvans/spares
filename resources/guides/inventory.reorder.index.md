# About this page

The buyer's morning screen: every part at or below its reorder point, with a suggested order quantity and the preferred supplier. Tick, adjust quantities, and **Create Draft POs** — one PO per supplier, costs from their active price list.

## Setup
- Reorder points are set inline on [Stock Levels](route:inventory.stock.index).
- Preferred suppliers per part come from the approved-suppliers list.

## Watch out
- Parts without a preferred supplier can't be auto-ordered — set one first.
- Created POs are **drafts**: review and submit them from [Purchase Orders](route:purchasing.orders.index).
