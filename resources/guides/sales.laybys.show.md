# About this page

One lay-by: what the customer is paying off, how much they have paid, and what is still owed. Goods stay in the shop until the balance reaches zero.

## Taking a payment
1. Enter the amount in **Record a payment** (it cannot exceed the balance).
2. Pick the method — cash, card or EFT.
3. Press **Record payment**. The payment appears in the list and the balance drops.

When the balance reaches zero the lay-by flips to **completed** — hand over the goods.

## Watch out
- The minimum deposit is set in the [Configuration Centre](route:admin.settings.index) and enforced when the lay-by is created, not here.
- A completed or cancelled lay-by cannot take payments.
- Lay-by payments are a holding deposit, not a sale — the tax invoice is raised when the goods are released.
