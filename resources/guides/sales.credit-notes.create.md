# About this page

Post a credit note against this invoice — the only way to correct a posted sale.

## Three steps
1. **What is coming back** — set the quantity to credit per line (never more than sold minus already credited). Tick **Back to stock** to book resellable goods in at original cost; untick for damaged goods or a pure price correction.
2. **Why** — pick a reason; it prints on the credit note.
3. **How the customer is settled** — **Cash refund** is only proper when the sale was paid in cash *and* it's the same trading day; the screen warns when you're outside that rule. **To account** reduces what an account customer owes (walk-ins have no account).

The red total is exactly what posts: returned sales, VAT at the original rate, and the refund or account credit.
