# About this page

Supplier invoices matched against goods received — the classic **3-way match** (PO ↔ GRN ↔ invoice). Matched invoices post straight to accounts payable; mismatches beyond tolerance are held as **disputed**.

## Watch out
- A disputed invoice never reaches payment runs until a buyer resolves it.
- The due date calculates from the supplier's payment terms automatically.
