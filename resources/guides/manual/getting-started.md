# Getting Started

Welcome to the system. This page gets you from your first login to a business that's ready to trade. Work through it top to bottom the first time.

## 1. Sign in for the first time

1. Open the system in your web browser and go to the sign-in page.
2. Enter your **username** (or email) and **password**. The very first login uses the administrator account your installer set up.
3. The system will immediately ask you to **set a new password** — this is required, you can't skip it. Choose something only you know.
4. You're now on the **Dashboard**. This is home; you can always get back here by clicking the logo.

> Tip: press **F1** on any page to open that page's built-in help panel. This User Guide (the book icon in the menu) is the longer, step-by-step version.

## 2. Find your way around

- **Module cards** on the Dashboard are the ten big areas of the system — Sales, Inventory, Purchasing, Workshop, Finance, Customers, Suppliers, Vehicle Reference, Reports and System Administration. Click one to enter it.
- **The left sidebar** changes to show only the screens for wherever you are. The top of it switches between the area's sub-sections.
- **Search** — press **Ctrl+K** anywhere to jump to a part, customer, supplier or screen by name.
- **Everything is linked.** A part number, customer name, invoice number or supplier shown anywhere on screen is a link — click it to open that record. From a document you can trace its whole story (the "Connected to" card).

## 3. Set up the business (admin, once)

Do these in order before you start trading. They live under **System Administration** (see [system-admin guide](guide:system-admin) for detail).

1. **Company details** — your name, address, VAT and registration numbers, and **banking details** (these print on every invoice and statement). [(open)](route:admin.company.index)
2. **Check the settings** — VAT rate, document numbering, pricing rules. [(open)](route:admin.settings.index)
3. **Add your staff** as users and give each a role (Cashier, Buyer, Accountant, Workshop, Manager…). [(open)](route:auth.users.index)
4. **Workshop labour rates** — if you run a workshop, set your hourly rates. [(open)](route:workshop.labour.index)

## 4. Load your data

Now bring in your real parts, stock, suppliers and customers from Excel — see the full walkthrough in [data-upload guide](guide:data-upload). Then you're ready to trade: start selling in [sales guide](guide:sales).
