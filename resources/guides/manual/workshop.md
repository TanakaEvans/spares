# Workshop

Book a vehicle in, do the work, and turn it into an invoice — with the parts and labour tracked and costed.

## Open a job card

[**Workshop → Job Cards → New**](route:workshop.jobs.index).

1. Pick the **customer** and their **vehicle** (add the vehicle if it's new).
2. Record the **reported fault** and the odometer reading.
3. Save — the job card is created and lands on the **board**.

## Assign and start

1. Open the job and **assign a technician**.
2. Move it to **In progress** using the status buttons.

## Add labour and parts

- **Labour** — add a labour line, pick the operation (from your labour-code pack) and the hours. The rate comes from your pricing.
- **Parts** — request a part, then **issue** it. Issuing brings the part out of stock and books its cost to the job, so you can see the job's margin live.
- Fitted the wrong part? **Return** it and it goes back into stock.

## Complete and invoice

1. Move the job to **Quality check**, then **Completed**.
2. Click **Invoice job**. The system raises a tax invoice — parts and labour split onto the right accounts — for cash or the customer's account.
3. The job and its invoice stay linked; the "Connected to" card shows the whole story.

## Warranty claims

If a supplied part fails under warranty, raise a [**warranty claim**](route:workshop.warranty.index) against the supplier and track it to credit.

## See a technician's work

Open any [**technician**](route:workshop.technicians.index) to see their jobs, status counts and hours.
