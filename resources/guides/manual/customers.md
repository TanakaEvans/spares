# Customers

Your customer list, their credit, and their statements.

## Add a customer

1. **Customers → Add Customer** (route:customers.index).
2. Enter their name and type (individual, business, fleet, dealer).
3. For account customers, set the **payment terms** and **credit limit**, and the **price list** (retail or trade) they get.
4. Save.

> Loading lots of customers? Use (guide:data-upload) instead of typing them.

## The customer page

Open any customer (click their name anywhere) to see, all linked:

- **Account balance** and available credit.
- Their **invoices, credit notes, receipts and lay-bys**.
- Their **vehicles** and **job cards** (if you run a workshop).
- Quick links to their **statement** (PDF or CSV) and to **take a payment**.

## Set or change a credit limit

On the customer's page, edit the customer and set the **credit limit** and **terms**. The till enforces this automatically — a sale that would push them over is blocked.

## Put a customer on hold

On the customer's page, use **Place on hold** to stop further credit sales (for example when they're overdue). Release it the same way.

## Send a statement

On the customer's page, **Statement** gives you a PDF (or CSV) of their account activity and ageing to send them.

## Groups and communication

- **Customer Groups** (route:customers.groups.index) — attach a trade price list to a whole group.
- **Communication Log** (route:customers.comms.index) — record calls, visits and emails.
- **Loyalty** (route:customers.loyalty.index) — points, if you run a loyalty programme.
