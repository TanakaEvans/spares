# About this page

Every supplier you buy from — local distributors and importers. The **AP balance** column shows what you currently owe (posted invoices minus credits).

## From a supplier's page you can
- Raise a PO directly, pre-linked to them.
- **Import their price list** (CSV) — costs then pre-fill on every PO line.
- Manage contacts and see their order/invoice history.
