# About this page

The trial balance as at any date: every account with a movement, in debit and credit columns. Because the ledger is balanced by construction, total debits always equal total credits — the badge confirms it.

## Reading it
Pick a date (and optionally a branch). Each account shows its net balance on its natural side. From here the [Income Statement](route:finance.reports.income-statement) and [Balance Sheet](route:finance.reports.balance-sheet) present the same figures grouped for reporting.
