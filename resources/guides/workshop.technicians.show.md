# About this page

One technician's profile and workload: skill level, specialisations, cost rate and whether they are active, with the job cards they have been assigned and a simple productivity line.

## What you see
- **Job cards** — the 20 most recent jobs assigned to this technician. Every job number, customer, vehicle and invoice is a link, so you can walk straight from a technician to the work and the money.
- **Productivity** — jobs completed (completed, invoiced or closed) against all jobs ever assigned; **hours booked** is the sum of labour lines charged to this technician; **hours logged** comes from clock-on/off entries where they exist.
- **Connected to** — active jobs and the home branch.

## Changing details
Skill level, specialisations, cost rate, branch and the active flag are edited from the [Technicians](route:workshop.technicians.index) list. The cost rate drives job costing (labour cost = hours × cost rate) and so the margin shown on each job card.
