# About this page

What you owe suppliers, aged by due date, and the payments you've made. Posting a payment does DR Trade Creditors (2110), CR bank — clearing the AP sub-ledger.

## Paying
- **New Payment** — pay one supplier, allocate across their invoices ("pay all" settles everything owed).
- **Payment Run** — select outstanding invoices across many suppliers and pay them in one batch (one payment per supplier, shared batch reference).
