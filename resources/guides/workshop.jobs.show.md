# About this page

The job card — everything on one repair. Book labour and parts, move the job through its stages, watch the margin, and invoice it when done.

## The lifecycle
Open → Allocated (needs a customer + vehicle) → In Progress → (Awaiting Parts / Customer / On Hold) → Quality Check → Completed → **Invoiced** → Closed.

## Parts vs labour
- **Parts** are requested, then **issued** from stock — issuing books the cost to the job (stock leaves inventory then). Unused parts can be **returned**.
- **Labour** bills hours × rate; the rate pre-fills from the labour code (make-specific overrides apply). Warranty lines are excluded from the customer's invoice.

## Invoicing
A **completed** job invoices through Sales — parts revenue and labour revenue post separately (4100 / 4300). The costing panel shows cost vs billed and the margin.
