# About this page

Lay-bys: the customer pays a deposit now and the balance over time; the goods only leave once paid in full.

- The bar on each row shows progress to full payment; the balance column is what's still owed.
- **Payment** records an instalment from the list; open the lay-by for the full payment history.
- A new lay-by requires the minimum deposit percentage shown on the form.
