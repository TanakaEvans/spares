# About this page

Live stock across all branches: on hand, reserved, available, average cost (AVCO) and total value. This screen always agrees with the stock ledger — every number here traces to a posted movement.

## How it works
- **Available** = on hand − reserved (reserved = allocated to open orders).
- **AVCO** is the weighted-average cost, recalculated on every receipt.
- Tick **Below reorder point** to see what needs ordering.

## Watch out
- Stock cannot be edited here — it only changes through posted documents (receipts, sales, adjustments). That is what keeps the books true.
