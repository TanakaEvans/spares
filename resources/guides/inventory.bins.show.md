# About this page

One bin location and everything physically stored in it: each part's on-hand, reserved and available quantity, its average cost and the value sitting on that shelf.

## How it works
- A bin belongs to one branch; a part's stock level at that branch points at (at most) one primary bin.
- Totals at the top are the sum of the stock levels assigned here, valued at AVCO.
- Click a part to open its catalogue page; **Connected to** lists the branch and every part in this bin.

## Watch out
- This screen is read-only. Quantities only change through posted documents (receipts, sales, adjustments, transfers) — never here.
- A part with no bin assigned does not appear in any bin; set its primary bin on the stock level.
