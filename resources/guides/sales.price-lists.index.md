# About this page

Your **selling** prices (VAT-exclusive). The default retail list is the fallback for every sale; other lists attach to customers or customer groups.

## How pricing resolves
A part's price is taken from the customer's own list → their group's list → the **default retail** list. A part with no price anywhere cannot be sold.

## Watch out
- Keep the **unpriced parts** count on the default list at zero — those parts are blocked at the till.
- Supplier **cost** prices are separate; import those from the Suppliers module.
