# About this page

One delivery note: who the goods are going to, which invoice or order they belong to, who is driving, and whether they have arrived.

## Marking it delivered
- While the note is **dispatched**, press **Mark delivered** once the customer has signed for the goods.
- Delivered notes are read-only — raise a new note for a second trip.

## Watch out
- A delivery note moves goods; it does not bill them. The invoice (linked under **Source document**) is what the customer owes.
- No source document means the note was raised by hand — link it to the invoice next time so the chain is complete.
