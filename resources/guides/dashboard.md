# About this page

This is the home of SparesPro. Each card is a **module** — a major area of the business. Click a card to see that module's sub-modules and enter its screens.

## Where things live
- Selling at the counter, quotes and invoices → [Sales & POS](route:modules.show) *(open the Sales card)*
- Parts, stock levels and stock takes → Inventory Management
- Buying and receiving stock → Purchasing
- Users, roles, company setup and system configuration → System Administration

## Watch out
- Cards marked **In Planning** show what's coming; their screens are not built yet.
- What you see is filtered by your role — missing a card you need? Ask your administrator.
