# About this page

The journal register. Most journals here are posted automatically by the system — every invoice, receipt, payment and stock movement journalises itself. You only capture **manual** journals (accruals, depreciation, corrections).

## Manual journals
New Journal → each line is a debit or a credit → the totals must balance before you can post. Manual journals may **not** touch control accounts (Debtors, Creditors, Inventory, VAT) — those move only through their sub-ledgers. A mistake is fixed by **reversing** (a mirror journal), never by editing.
