# About this page

One supplier invoice — the supplier's bill matched against the GRN it was captured for, what it posted to accounts payable, and the payments allocated against it.

## Reading the numbers
- **Subtotal / VAT / Total** are what the supplier billed; the lines below are what we *received* — the 3-way match compares the two.
- **Paid** sums the supplier payments allocated to this invoice; **Outstanding** is what is still owed.

## Watch out
- A **disputed** invoice is not yet in AP. Only **Resolve & Post** after the buyer has approved the variance.
- Settle it from [Supplier Payments](route:finance.payments.index); the payment appears here once allocated.
