# About this page

Goods receiving (GRN) — the front door for stock. Receiving posts stock at PO cost (recalculating the average cost), assigns bins, and books the accounting accrual automatically.

## Receiving a delivery
1. Pick the PO from "awaiting delivery".
2. Count what actually arrived; enter rejections with a reason — **rejected items never enter stock**.
3. Assign bins, then **Post GRN**.

## Watch out
- Posting is final. Wrong receipt? Correct it with a [supplier return](route:purchasing.returns.index), never by editing.
- Over-receiving beyond tolerance needs a supervisor tick.
- After posting, capture the [supplier invoice](route:purchasing.invoices.index) — 3-way matching runs automatically.
