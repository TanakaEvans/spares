# About this page

One goods received note — what physically arrived from the supplier, which bin each part went into, and the accounting accrual it booked (DR Inventory / CR GRN Accruals).

## Follow the chain
The **Connected to** card traces this receipt through the procure-to-pay flow: purchase order → GRN → supplier invoice → payment. Greyed steps have not happened yet; click any solid step to open it.

## Watch out
- A posted GRN is final. Wrong quantity or damaged goods? Raise a [supplier return](route:purchasing.returns.create) — never edit.
- Capture the supplier's invoice from here once it arrives; the 3-way match checks it against these lines.
