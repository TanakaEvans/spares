# About this page

Formal quotations with an expiry date. A quote reserves nothing and posts nothing — it's a priced offer.

## Lifecycle
Open → **Convert to Order** (reserves stock) → fulfil to an invoice. Expired quotes can't convert; re-quote at current prices.

## Watch out
- On convert, any line whose current price differs is flagged — keep the quoted prices or **reprice**.
