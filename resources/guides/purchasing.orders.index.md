# About this page

Every purchase order, from draft to closed. A PO is the formal commitment to buy — nothing enters stock without one.

## The PO lifecycle
Draft → **Submit** (sent to supplier, lines lock) → Confirm → **Receive Goods** (per delivery) → Received → Closed.

## Watch out
- Only submitted/confirmed POs can be received against.
- Cost prices pre-fill from the supplier's **active price list** — keep price lists imported and current.
