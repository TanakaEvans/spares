# About this page

Compare what each supplier charges for the same part, from their imported price lists.

- Search a part number or description; every supplier offer is listed cheapest first.
- The **best** badge marks the lowest cost; the right column shows how much you save per unit against the costliest offer.

Supplier costs come from imported price lists — upload them on the supplier's page.
