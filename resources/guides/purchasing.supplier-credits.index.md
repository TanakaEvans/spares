# About this page

Supplier credit notes — the credits received against returns to supplier. Each one reduced Accounts Payable: real money recovered.

- **Recovered from suppliers** is the total credited to date.
- Credits are captured from the return's page or inline on [Returns to Supplier](route:purchasing.returns.index) — a credit can't exist without a shipped return.
