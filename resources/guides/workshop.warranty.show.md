# About this page

A single warranty claim against a supplier: the fault, the part and job it relates to, what was claimed and what the supplier has credited.

## Lifecycle
Draft → Submitted → Acknowledged → Approved → **Credited**, or **Rejected** at any point before credit. Use the **Progress claim** panel to move it forward; record the supplier's claim/RMA reference as soon as you have it, and the credit amount when the supplier credits you (it defaults to the claim amount).

## Links
Supplier, part, job card, customer, vehicle and the job's invoice are all hyperlinks. The *Connected to* card shows where the claim sits: the originating job card → this claim → the supplier credit still to come.

## Notes
- A claim does not post to the ledger on its own — the supplier credit note is captured under Purchasing when it arrives.
- Credited and rejected claims are final; raise a new claim if the supplier reopens the case.
