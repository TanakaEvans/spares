# About this page

One import consignment — where it is on its journey (ordered → in transit → customs → cleared → received) and what it will cost landed: goods + freight + duty.

## Moving it along
Press the **Mark …** button as each milestone happens; the stepper shows where the shipment is now.

## Watch out
- Landed cost is informational here — stock and accounting only move when the goods are received against a [purchase order](route:purchasing.orders.index).
