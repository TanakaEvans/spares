# About this page

The chart of accounts — the full account structure the books post into, grouped Assets / Liabilities / Equity / Revenue / Expenses, each showing its live balance.

## Control accounts
Accounts tagged **control** (Debtors 1210, Creditors 2110, Inventory 1310, VAT) can't be posted to directly — they move only through their sub-ledgers (sales, purchasing, receipts, payments, stock). Click any account to drill into its movements.
