# About this page

Every customer — walk-in cash, individuals, businesses, fleets and dealers. Open a customer to see their **account balance**, available credit and document history.

## Credit control
- **Payment terms of 0** means cash-only (no account).
- A **credit limit** caps the on-account balance; sales that would exceed it are blocked at the till.
- Place an account **on hold** to stop all account sales while still allowing cash and card.

## Watch out
- The seeded **Cash Customer** is the walk-in fallback and can't buy on account or be held.
