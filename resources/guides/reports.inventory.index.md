# About this page

What you're holding and what it's worth. The stock-value tile is reconciled against the Inventory control account (1310) — a green badge means the physical ledger and the GL agree.

## Filters
Narrow to below-reorder, out-of-stock or negative lines. The ageing chart values stock by how long it's sat without movement.
